Author · 1916 – 1990

Roald Dahl was a British author of Norwegian parentage whose children's books, including Charlie and the Chocolate Factory, Matilda and The BFG, have sold hundreds of millions of copies. Before writing he served as an RAF fighter pilot and intelligence officer.

Mark Twain Quotes on Happiness

What did Mark Twain have to say about happiness? This page gathers 8 quotes by Mark Twain on happiness from the QuotePlace collection — every one drawn from the site's Happiness topic, in one place. About the author: Mark Twain (1835–1910) — Writer.

Mark Twain

Mark Twain

Mark Twain

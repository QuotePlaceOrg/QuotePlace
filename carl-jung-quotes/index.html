Carl Jung Quotes

Carl Jung (Psychiatrist & Psychoanalyst, 1875 – 1961). Below are 37 quotes by Carl Jung from QuotePlace, each as a text quote card.

Psychiatrist & Psychoanalyst · 1875 – 1961

Swiss psychiatrist who founded analytical psychology. His concepts of the collective unconscious, archetypes and introversion and extraversion remain influential in psychology and culture.

Quotes by Carl Jung

1.Who looks outside, dreams; who looks inside, awakes.
2.The shoe that fits one person pinches another; there is no recipe for living that suits all cases.
3.Everything that irritates us about others can lead us to an understanding about ourselves.
4.Your vision will become clear only when you look into your heart. Who looks outside, dreams. Who looks inside, awakens.
5.In all chaos there is a cosmos, in all disorder a secret order.
6.Without this playing with fantasy no creative work has ever yet come to birth. The debt we owe to the play of the imagination is incalculable.
7.Through pride we are ever deceiving ourselves. But deep down below the surface of the average conscience a still, small voice says to us, Something is out of tune.
8.Knowledge rests not upon truth alone, but upon error also.
9.The least of things with a meaning is worth more in life than the greatest of things without it.
10.Knowing your own darkness is the best method for dealing with the darknesses of other people.
11.It all depends on how we look at things, and not how they are in themselves.
12.Everything that irritates us about others can lead us to a better understanding of ourselves.
13.Your vision will become clear only when you can look into your own heart. Who looks outside, dreams; who looks inside, awakes.
14.When an inner situation is not made conscious, it happens outside, as fate.
15.Neurosis is always a substitute for legitimate suffering.
16.One does not become enlightened by imagining figures of light, but by making the darkness conscious.
17.Mistakes are, after all, the foundations of truth, and if a man does not know what a thing is, it is at least an increase in knowledge if he knows what it is not.
18.Where love rules, there is no will to power, and where power predominates, love is lacking.
19.Every form of addiction is bad, no matter whether the narcotic be alcohol, morphine or idealism.
20.Until you make the unconscious conscious, it will direct your life and you will call it fate.
21.I am not what happened to me, I am what I choose to become.
22.Your visions will become clear only when you can look into your own heart. Who looks outside, dreams; who looks inside, awakes.
23.The privilege of a lifetime is to become who you truly are.
24.We cannot change anything unless we accept it. Condemnation does not liberate, it oppresses.
25.The meeting of two personalities is like the contact of two chemical substances; if there is any reaction, both are transformed.
26.Man needs difficulties; they are necessary for health.
27.Everything that irritates us about others can lead us to an understanding of ourselves.
28.One looks back with appreciation to the brilliant teachers, but with gratitude to those who touched our human feelings.
29.People will do anything, no matter how absurd, in order to avoid facing their own souls.
30.The most terrifying thing is to accept oneself completely.
31.As far as we can discern, the sole purpose of human existence is to kindle a light of meaning in the darkness of mere being.
32.Loneliness does not come from having no people about one, but from being unable to communicate the things that seem important to oneself.
33.The greatest tragedy of the family is the unlived lives of the parents.
34.Children are educated by what the grown-up is and not by his talk.
35.What you resist not only persists, but will grow in size.
36.The first half of life is devoted to forming a healthy ego, the second half is going inward and letting go of it.
37.There is no coming to consciousness without pain.

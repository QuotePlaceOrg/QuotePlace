Jeff Bezos (Founder of Amazon, born 1964). Below are 5 quotes by Jeff Bezos from QuotePlace, each as a text quote card.

Founder of Amazon · born 1964

Jeff Bezos founded Amazon in 1994 as an online bookstore and grew it into one of the world's most valuable companies, reshaping retail and cloud computing along the way. He stepped down as CEO in 2021 and also founded the spaceflight company Blue Origin.

Quotes by Jeff Bezos

Your brand is what other people say about you when you're not in the room.
I knew that if I failed I wouldn't regret that, but I knew the one thing I might regret is not trying.
If you double the number of experiments you do per year, you're going to double your inventiveness.
Invention requires a long-term willingness to be misunderstood.
What we need to do is always lean into the future.

Victor Hugo Quotes

Victor Hugo (Writer, 1802 – 1885). Below are 19 quotes by Victor Hugo from QuotePlace, each as a portrait card.

Writer · 1802 – 1885

French poet and novelist, author of Les Misérables and The Hunchback of Notre-Dame.

Quotes by Victor Hugo

1.It is nothing to die; it is frightful not to live.
2.To love another person is to see the face of God.
3.Laughter is the sun that drives winter from the human face.
4.Life's greatest happiness is to be convinced we are loved.
5.Music expresses that which cannot be said and on which it is impossible to be silent.
6.Life is the flower for which love is the honey.
7.An invasion of armies can be resisted, but not an idea whose time has come.
8.To love or have loved, that is enough.
9.Even the darkest night will end and the sun will rise.
10.The supreme happiness of life is the conviction that we are loved; loved for ourselves — say rather, loved in spite of ourselves.
11.He who opens a school door, closes a prison.
12.There is nothing like a dream to create the future.
13.Adversity makes men, and prosperity makes monsters.
14.To love or have loved, that is enough. Ask nothing further.
15.To love beauty is to see light.
16.Life is a flower of which love is the honey.
17.Music expresses that which cannot be put into words and that which cannot remain silent.
18.Change your opinions, keep to your principles; change your leaves, keep intact your roots.
19.To learn to read is to light a fire; every syllable that is spelled out is a spark.

Mir Taqi Mir Quotes

Mir Taqi Mir (Urdu poet, 1723 – 1810). Below are 4 quotes by Mir Taqi Mir from QuotePlace, each as a text quote card.

Urdu poet · 1723 – 1810

Mir Taqi Mir, honoured as Khuda-e-Sukhan (God of Poetry), was the pre-eminent Urdu poet of eighteenth-century Delhi and Lucknow. His ghazals of love, loss and the decline of Delhi set the standard for classical Urdu poetry. His six diwans and masnavis remain central to the Urdu canon.

Quotes by Mir Taqi Mir

1.This is only the beginning of love — why do you weep? Wait and watch what is yet to come.
2.Every leaf, every shrub knows the state of my heart; the flower alone does not know, though the whole garden knows.
3.You appeared before me and I lost my senses; you parted me even from my own self.
4.My existence is like a bubble; all this display is but a mirage.

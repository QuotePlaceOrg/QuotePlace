Quotes About Time

Time is the one currency we all spend and none can save. These 15 quotes about time — on urgency, patience and presence — come from thinkers who studied it closely. A good reminder to spend your hours like they matter, because they do.

Don't limit a child to your own learning, for he was born in another time.
Rabindranath Tagore
If a problem has no solution, it may not be a problem, but a fact — not to be solved, but to be coped with over time.
Shimon Peres
The season of failure is the best time for sowing the seeds of success.
Paramahansa Yogananda
If you judge people, you have no time to love them.
Mother Teresa
Let your life lightly dance on the edges of Time like dew on the tip of a leaf.
Rabindranath Tagore
Time is not measured by the passing of years but by what one does, what one feels, and what one achieves.
Jawaharlal Nehru
To learn and at due times to repeat what one has learnt, is that not after all a pleasure?
Confucius
The present moment is the only time over which we have dominion.
Thich Nhat Hanh
A person may be as clever as the sky, but as long as he does not write, he will be lost in history and drowned by time.
Pramoedya Ananta Toer
Cowards die many times before their deaths; the valiant never taste of death but once.
William Shakespeare
It is not that we have a short time to live, but that we waste a great deal of it.
Seneca
The two most powerful warriors are patience and time.
Leo Tolstoy
Do not judge me by my successes, judge me by how many times I fell down and got back up again.
Nelson Mandela
Whenever you find yourself on the side of the majority, it is time to pause and reflect.
Mark Twain
Change will not come if we wait for some other person or some other time. We are the ones we've been waiting for.
Barack Obama

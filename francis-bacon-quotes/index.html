Francis Bacon Quotes

Francis Bacon (Philosopher and statesman, 1561 – 1626). Below are 12 quotes by Francis Bacon from QuotePlace, each as a quote card.

Philosopher and statesman · 1561 – 1626

Francis Bacon was an English philosopher, statesman and essayist who championed the empirical method of inquiry. His Essays and his plan for the reform of learning in the Novum Organum helped lay the groundwork of modern science.

Quotes by Francis Bacon

1.Knowledge itself is power.
2.Some books are to be tasted, others to be swallowed, and some few to be chewed and digested.
3.A prudent question is one half of wisdom.
4.A wise man will make more opportunities than he finds.
5.Reading maketh a full man; conference a ready man; and writing an exact man.
6.Nature, to be commanded, must be obeyed.
7.If a man will begin with certainties, he shall end in doubts; but if he will be content to begin with doubts, he shall end in certainties.
8.Hope is a good breakfast, but it is a bad supper.
9.He that will not apply new remedies must expect new evils; for time is the greatest innovator.
10.He that hath wife and children hath given hostages to fortune.
11.A man that is young in years may be old in hours, if he have lost no time.
12.Revenge is a kind of wild justice.

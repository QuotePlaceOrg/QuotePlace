Author & Anthropologist · 1925 – 1998

Peruvian-born American anthropologist and author of The Teachings of Don Juan (1968) and a series of books about his apprenticeship with a Yaqui sorcerer. His works, hugely popular in the 1970s, remain controversial among scholars.

Astrophysicist and science communicator · born 1958

Neil deGrasse Tyson is an American astrophysicist who has directed New York's Hayden Planetarium since 1996. He hosted the 2014 revival of Cosmos and the talk show StarTalk, bringing astronomy to mass audiences. His book Astrophysics for People in a Hurry (2017) was a long-running bestseller.

Quotes by Neil deGrasse Tyson

The universe is under no obligation to make sense to you.
The good thing about science is that it's true whether or not you believe in it.
We are all connected; to each other, biologically; to the earth, chemically; to the rest of the universe, atomically.
Knowing how to think empowers you far beyond those who know only what to think.

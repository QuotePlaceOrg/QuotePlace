Quotes by Alfred Tennyson

1.'Tis better to have loved and lost than never to have loved at all.
2.To strive, to seek, to find, and not to yield.
3.Knowledge comes, but wisdom lingers.
4.Theirs not to reason why, theirs but to do and die.
5.The happiness of a man in this life does not consist in the absence but in the mastery of his passions.
6.Tears, idle tears, I know not what they mean.

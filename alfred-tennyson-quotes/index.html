Alfred Tennyson Quotes

Alfred Tennyson (Poet, 1809 – 1892). Below are 15 quotes by Alfred Tennyson from QuotePlace, each as a quote card.

Poet · 1809 – 1892

English Poet Laureate; author of In Memoriam and Ulysses.

Quotes by Alfred Tennyson

1.'Tis better to have loved and lost than never to have loved at all.
2.To strive, to seek, to find, and not to yield.
3.Knowledge comes, but wisdom lingers.
4.Theirs not to reason why, theirs but to do and die.
5.The happiness of a man in this life does not consist in the absence but in the mastery of his passions.
6.Tears, idle tears, I know not what they mean.
7.I am a part of all that I have met.
8.Come, my friends, 'tis not too late to seek a newer world.
9.There lives more faith in honest doubt, believe me, than in half the creeds.
10.Ring out the old, ring in the new.
11.For words, like Nature, half reveal and half conceal the Soul within.
12.Death closes all: but something ere the end, some work of noble note, may yet be done.
13.Though much is taken, much abides; and though we are not now that strength which in old days moved earth and heaven, that which we are, we are.
14.The old order changeth, yielding place to new.
15.For men may come and men may go, but I go on for ever.

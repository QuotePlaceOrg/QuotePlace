Jaun Elia (Urdu poet, 1931 – 2002). Below are 4 quotes by Jaun Elia from QuotePlace, each as a text quote card.

Urdu poet · 1931 – 2002

Syed Sibt-e-Asghar Naqvi, known as Jaun Elia, was a Pakistani Urdu poet, scholar and philosopher born in Amroha, India. His verse blends deep classical learning with a rebellious, melancholic wit unlike any other Urdu poet. His first collection, Shayad, appeared in 1991, yet his couplets are among the most quoted in the language.

Quotes by Jaun Elia

1.I too have a tongue in my mouth. If only you would ask what I have to say.
2.Who will look after this house now? Every day, something or the other breaks.
3.Why do I find no peace of mind? There was, after all, only one person in the whole world.
4.I am convinced my mother always spoke the truth — in the days of my childhood, fairies used to live in the moon.

Biographer and essayist · 46 – 119

Plutarch was a Greek biographer and essayist who lived under Roman rule. His Parallel Lives paired famous Greeks and Romans to draw moral lessons, and his Moralia essays range across philosophy, religion and everyday conduct.

Quotes by Plutarch

1.The mind is not a vessel to be filled, but a fire to be kindled.
2.What we achieve inwardly will change outer reality.
3.Know how to listen, and you will profit even from those who talk badly.
4.To make no mistakes is not in the power of man; but from their errors and mistakes the wise and good learn wisdom for the future.

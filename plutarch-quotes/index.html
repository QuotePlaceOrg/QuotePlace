Biographer and essayist · 46 – 119

Plutarch was a Greek biographer and essayist who lived under Roman rule. His Parallel Lives paired famous Greeks and Romans to draw moral lessons, and his Moralia essays range across philosophy, religion and everyday conduct.

The mind is not a vessel to be filled, but a fire to be kindled.
Plutarch

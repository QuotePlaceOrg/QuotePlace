Sachin Tendulkar Quotes

Sachin Tendulkar (Cricketer, born 1973). Below are 4 quotes by Sachin Tendulkar from QuotePlace, each as a text quote card.

Cricketer · born 1973

Indian cricket icon widely regarded as one of the greatest batsmen in history, scoring 100 international centuries across a 24-year career. He debuted for India at just 16 in 1989 and retired in 2013 after a record 200 Test matches, carrying the hopes of a billion fans.

Quotes by Sachin Tendulkar

People throw stones at you and you convert them into milestones.
My life between 22 yards for 24 years; it is hard to believe that that wonderful journey has come to an end.
I hate losing and cricket being my first love, once I enter the ground it's a different zone altogether and that hunger for winning is always there.
Enjoy the game and chase your dreams. Dreams do come true.

Jiddu Krishnamurti Quotes

Jiddu Krishnamurti (Philosopher, 1895 – 1986). Below are 8 quotes by Jiddu Krishnamurti from QuotePlace, each as a portrait card.

Philosopher · 1895 – 1986

Indian philosopher and speaker on the nature of mind, meditation, and human freedom; rejected all guru roles.

Quotes by Jiddu Krishnamurti

1.The ability to observe without evaluating is the highest form of intelligence.
2.It is no measure of health to be well adjusted to a profoundly sick society.
3.To understand the immeasurable, the mind must be extraordinarily quiet, still.
4.Freedom from the desire for an answer is essential to the understanding of a problem.
5.The moment you have in your heart this extraordinary thing called love and feel the depth, the delight, the ecstasy of it, you will discover that for you the world is transformed.
6.Tradition becomes our security, and when the mind is secure it is in decay.
7.Truth is a pathless land.
8.One is never afraid of the unknown; one is afraid of the known coming to an end.

Leonardo DiCaprio Quotes

Leonardo DiCaprio (Actor and environmentalist, born 1974). Below are 4 quotes by Leonardo DiCaprio from QuotePlace, each as a text quote card.

Actor and environmentalist · born 1974

American actor who rose from child roles to global stardom with Titanic and won the Academy Award for Best Actor for The Revenant in 2016. Off screen he is a leading environmental activist and founded the Leonardo DiCaprio Foundation in 1998 to protect wildlife and fight climate change.

Quotes by Leonardo DiCaprio

1.Climate change is real, it is happening right now. It is the most urgent threat facing our entire species, and we need to work collectively together and stop procrastinating.
2.Let us not take this planet for granted. I do not take tonight for granted.
3.If you can do what you do best and be happy, you're further along in life than most people.
4.Everybody has gone through something that has changed them in a way that they could never go back to the person they once were.

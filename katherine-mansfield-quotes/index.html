Katherine Mansfield Quotes

Katherine Mansfield (Writer, 1888 – 1923). Below are 7 quotes by Katherine Mansfield from QuotePlace, each as a portrait card.

Writer · 1888 – 1923

New Zealand modernist short-story writer, a leading figure of early 20th-century literature alongside Joyce and Woolf.

Quotes by Katherine Mansfield

1.I want to be all that I am capable of becoming.
2.Everything in life that we really accept undergoes a change.
3.I am a writer first and a woman after.
4.Risk! Risk anything! Care no more for the opinion of others, for those voices. Do the hardest thing on earth for you. Act for yourself. Face the truth.
5.Make it a rule of life never to regret and never to look back. Regret is an appalling waste of energy; you can't build on it; it's only for wallowing in.
6.Risk! Risk anything! Care no more for the opinions of others.
7.The pleasure of all reading is doubled when one lives with another who shares the same books.

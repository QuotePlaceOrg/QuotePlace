Usain Bolt Quotes

Usain Bolt (Sprinter, born 1986). Below are 4 quotes by Usain Bolt from QuotePlace, each as a text quote card.

Sprinter · born 1986

Jamaican sprinter, the fastest man in history, who set world records in the 100 metres (9.58 seconds) and 200 metres (19.19 seconds) in 2009. He won eight Olympic gold medals across the 2008, 2012 and 2016 Games and became famous for his 'lightning bolt' celebration pose.

Quotes by Usain Bolt

Worrying gets you nowhere. If you turn up worrying about how you're going to perform, you've already lost.
There are better starters than me, but I'm a strong finisher.
Don't think about the start of the race, think about the ending.
I know what I can do, so I never doubt myself.

Kahlil Gibran Quotes on Wisdom

Kahlil Gibran on wisdom: 16 quotes, pulled from the QuotePlace Wisdom collection and gathered here. Read them together and a theme emerges that the full author page can bury. About the author: Kahlil Gibran (1883–1931) — Writer and poet.

Beauty is eternity gazing at itself in a mirror.
Kahlil Gibran
The teacher who is indeed wise does not bid you to enter the house of his wisdom but rather leads you to the threshold of your mind.
Kahlil Gibran
To understand the heart and mind of a person, look not at what he has already achieved, but at what he aspires to do.
Kahlil Gibran
Beauty is not in the face; beauty is a light in the heart.
Kahlil Gibran
We choose our joys and sorrows long before we experience them.
Kahlil Gibran
Faith is an oasis in the heart which will never be reached by the caravan of thinking.
Kahlil Gibran
Half of what I say is meaningless; but I say it so that the other half may reach you.
Kahlil Gibran
You talk when you cease to be at peace with your thoughts.
Kahlil Gibran
When you reach the end of what you should know, you will be at the beginning of what you should sense.
Kahlil Gibran
The deeper that sorrow carves into your being, the more joy you can contain.
Kahlil Gibran
The most pitiful among men is he who turns his dreams into silver and gold.
Kahlil Gibran
Love knows not its own depth until the hour of separation.
Kahlil Gibran
If you reveal your secrets to the wind, you should not blame the wind for revealing them to the trees.
Kahlil Gibran
Poetry is a deal of joy and pain and wonder, with a dash of the dictionary.
Kahlil Gibran
I have learned silence from the talkative, toleration from the intolerant, and kindness from the unkind; yet, strange, I am ungrateful to those teachers.
Kahlil Gibran
Doubt is a pain too lonely to know that faith is his twin brother.
Kahlil Gibran

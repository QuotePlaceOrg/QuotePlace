Marcus Aurelius Quotes on Peace

11 quotes by Marcus Aurelius on the subject of peace, collected from across the QuotePlace library. If you came looking for Marcus Aurelius's thinking on peace specifically, rather than everything Marcus Aurelius ever said, this is the page. About the author: Marcus Aurelius (121–180) — Emperor.

You have power over your mind — not outside events. Realize this, and you will find strength.
Marcus Aurelius
Everything that happens happens as it should, and if you observe carefully, you will find this to be so.
Marcus Aurelius
He who lives in harmony with himself lives in harmony with the universe.
Marcus Aurelius
Accept the things to which fate binds you, and love the people with whom fate brings you together, but do so with all your heart.
Marcus Aurelius
You will find rest from vain fancies if you perform every act in life as though it were your last.
Marcus Aurelius
How much more grievous are the consequences of anger than the causes of it.
Marcus Aurelius

Marcus Aurelius
The soul becomes dyed with the colour of its thoughts.
Marcus Aurelius

Marcus Aurelius

Marcus Aurelius

Marcus Aurelius

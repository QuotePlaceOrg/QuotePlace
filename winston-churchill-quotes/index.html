Winston Churchill Quotes

Winston Churchill (Statesman, 1874 – 1965). Below are 13 quotes by Winston Churchill from QuotePlace, each as a portrait card.

Statesman · 1874 – 1965

British prime minister during the Second World War and Nobel laureate in Literature.

Quotes by Winston Churchill

1.We shape our buildings; thereafter they shape us.
2.Now this is not the end. It is not even the beginning of the end. But it is, perhaps, the end of the beginning.
3.Short words are best and the old words when short are best of all.
4.You have enemies? Good. That means you've stood up for something, sometime in your life.
5.Courage is what it takes to stand up and speak; courage is also what it takes to sit down and listen.
6.History will be kind to me for I intend to write it.
7.Before you can inspire with emotion, you must be swamped with it yourself. Before you can move their tears, your own must flow. To convince them, you must yourself believe.
8.The price of greatness is responsibility.
9.The pessimist sees difficulty in every opportunity. The optimist sees the opportunity in every difficulty.
10.I never worry about action, but only inaction.
11.We make a living by what we get, but we make a life by what we give.
12.Continuous effort—not strength or intelligence—is the key to unlocking our potential.
13.We shall fight on the beaches, we shall fight on the landing grounds, we shall fight in the fields and in the streets, we shall fight in the hills; we shall never surrender.

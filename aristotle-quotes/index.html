Aristotle Quotes

Aristotle (Philosopher, 384 BCE – 322 BCE). Below are 24 quotes by Aristotle from QuotePlace, each as a portrait card.

Philosopher · 384 BCE – 322 BCE

Greek philosopher and student of Plato; founder of logic and many scientific fields.

Quotes by Aristotle

1.The whole is greater than the sum of its parts.
2.What is a friend? A single soul dwelling in two bodies.
3.Hope is a waking dream.
4.Knowing yourself is the beginning of all wisdom.
5.Well begun is half done.
6.Change in all things is sweet.
7.It is the mark of an educated mind to be able to entertain a thought without accepting it.
8.Happiness depends upon ourselves.
9.In all things of nature there is something of the marvellous.
10.Those that know, do. Those that understand, teach.
11.Criticism is something you can easily avoid by saying nothing, doing nothing, and being nothing.
12.Moral excellence comes about as a result of habit. We become just by doing just acts, temperate by doing temperate acts, brave by doing brave acts.
13.The energy of the mind is the essence of life.
14.If one way be better than another, that you may be sure is natures way.
15.Man is by nature a social animal.
16.In poverty and other misfortunes of life, true friends are a sure refuge.
17.Happiness depends on leisure; for we are busy to have leisure, and make war to live in peace.
18.At his best, man is the noblest of all animals.
19.The roots of education are bitter, but the fruit is sweet.
20.All human actions have one or more of these seven causes: chance, nature, compulsions, habit, reason, passion, desire.
21.Youth is easily deceived because it is quick to hope.
22.The law is reason, free from passion.
23.The educated differ from the uneducated as much as the living differ from the dead.
24.The secret to humor is surprise.

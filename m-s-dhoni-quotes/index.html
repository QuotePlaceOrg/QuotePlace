M. S. Dhoni Quotes

M. S. Dhoni (Cricketer, born 1981). Below are 4 quotes by M. S. Dhoni from QuotePlace, each as a text quote card.

Cricketer · born 1981

Indian wicketkeeper-batsman and captain who led India to the 2007 T20 World Cup, the 2011 ODI World Cup and the 2013 Champions Trophy, the only captain to win all three ICC white-ball titles. Nicknamed 'Captain Cool', he is revered for his calm finishing of matches under pressure.

Quotes by M. S. Dhoni

1.You don't play for the crowd, you play for the country.
2.I have always believed that process is more important than results.
3.I believe in giving more than 100 percent on the field, and I don't really worry about the result if there's great commitment on the field.
4.Till the full stop doesn't come, the sentence is not complete.

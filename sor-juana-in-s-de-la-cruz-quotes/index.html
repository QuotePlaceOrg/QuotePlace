Sor Juana Inés de la Cruz Quotes

Sor Juana Inés de la Cruz (Nun, poet and scholar, 1648 – 1695). Below are 2 quotes by Sor Juana Inés de la Cruz from QuotePlace, each as a beautiful quote card.

Nun, poet and scholar · 1648 – 1695

Mexican Hieronymite nun, poet and scholar of the colonial Baroque, famed for the philosophical poem 'Primero sueño' and her bold defence of women's right to learn in the Reply to Sor Filotea. She is celebrated as the first great poet of the Americas.

I don't study to know more, but to ignore less.
Sor Juana Inés de la Cruz
One can perfectly well philosophize while cooking supper.
Sor Juana Inés de la Cruz

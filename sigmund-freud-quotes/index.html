Neurologist and founder of psychoanalysis · 1856 – 1939

Sigmund Freud was an Austrian neurologist who founded psychoanalysis, developing concepts such as the unconscious, the Oedipus complex and talk therapy. His theories profoundly influenced psychology, literature and 20th-century culture, though many are now scientifically disputed.

Hafiz Quotes

Hafiz (Persian poet, 1315 – 1390). Below are 4 quotes by Hafiz from QuotePlace, each as a text quote card.

Persian poet · 1315 – 1390

Khwaja Shams-ud-Din Muhammad, known as Hafiz of Shiraz, was a Persian lyric poet whose Divan is the most beloved poetry collection in Iran. His ghazals on love, wine and divine longing have been translated into many languages. His work later inspired Goethe's West-Eastern Divan.

Quotes by Hafiz

1.Even after all this time, the sun never says to the earth, 'You owe me.' Look what happens with a love like that. It lights the whole sky.
2.Fear is the cheapest room in the house. I would like to see you living in better conditions.
3.I wish I could show you, when you are lonely or in darkness, the astonishing light of your own being.
4.Your heart and my heart are very, very old friends.

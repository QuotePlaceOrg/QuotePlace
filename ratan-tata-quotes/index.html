Ratan Tata (Industrialist, chairman emeritus of Tata Sons, 1937 – 2024). Below are 4 quotes by Ratan Tata from QuotePlace, each as a text quote card.

Industrialist, chairman emeritus of Tata Sons · 1937 – 2024

Chairman of Tata Sons for more than two decades, Ratan Tata transformed the Tata group into a global conglomerate with acquisitions such as Tetley, Corus and Jaguar Land Rover. Revered in India for his integrity and philanthropy, he also championed the Tata Nano, the world's cheapest car.

Quotes by Ratan Tata

I don't believe in taking the right decisions. I take decisions and then make them right.
Take the stones people throw at you, and use them to build a monument.
Ups and downs in life are very important to keep us going, because a straight line even in an ECG means we are not alive.
The day I am not able to fly will be a sad day for me.

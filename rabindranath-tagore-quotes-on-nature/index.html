Rabindranath Tagore Quotes on Nature

What did Rabindranath Tagore have to say about nature? This page gathers 13 quotes by Rabindranath Tagore on nature from the QuotePlace collection — every one drawn from the site's Nature topic, in one place. About the author: Rabindranath Tagore (1861–1941) — Poet.

Let your life lightly dance on the edges of Time like dew on the tip of a leaf.
Rabindranath Tagore
Clouds come floating into my life, no longer to carry rain or usher storm, but to add color to my sunset sky.
Rabindranath Tagore
The flower which is single need not envy the thorns that are numerous.
Rabindranath Tagore
You can't cross the sea merely by standing and staring at the water.
Rabindranath Tagore
Trees are the earth's endless effort to speak to the listening heaven.
Rabindranath Tagore
If you cry because the sun has gone out of your life, your tears will prevent you from seeing the stars.
Rabindranath Tagore
The small wisdom is like water in a glass: clear, transparent, pure. The great wisdom is like the water in the sea: dark, mysterious, impenetrable.
Rabindranath Tagore
The stars are not afraid to appear like fireflies.
Rabindranath Tagore
The roots below the earth claim no rewards for making the branches fruitful.
Rabindranath Tagore
The mountain remains unmoved at its seeming defeat by the mist.
Rabindranath Tagore
By plucking her petals, you do not gather the beauty of the flower.
Rabindranath Tagore
Reach high, for stars lie hidden in you. Dream deep, for every dream precedes the goal.
Rabindranath Tagore
The water in a vessel is sparkling; the water in the sea is dark. The small truth has words which are clear; the great truth has great silence.
Rabindranath Tagore

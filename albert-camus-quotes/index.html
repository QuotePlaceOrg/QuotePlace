Albert Camus (Writer, 1913 – 1960). Below are 41 quotes by Albert Camus from QuotePlace, each as a portrait card.

Writer · 1913 – 1960

French-Algerian writer and philosopher, author of The Stranger; Nobel Prize in Literature 1957.

Quotes by Albert Camus

1.You know what charm is: a way of getting the answer yes without having asked any clear question.
2.The struggle itself toward the heights is enough to fill a man's heart.
3.In the depth of winter, I finally learned that within me there lay an invincible summer.
4.Autumn is a second spring when every leaf is a flower.
5.In the depth of winter, I finally learned that there was within me an invincible summer.
6.All men have a sweetness in their life. That is what helps them go on. It is towards that they turn when they feel too worn out.
7.You will never be happy if you continue to search for what happiness consists of. You will never live if you are looking for the meaning of life.
8.Everything considered, a determined soul will always manage.
9.One must imagine Sisyphus happy.
10.The evil that is in the world comes almost always from ignorance.
11.In the midst of winter, I found there was, within me, an invincible summer.
12.You cannot create experience. You must undergo it.
13.What is a rebel? A man who says no.
14.The only way to deal with an unfree world is to become so absolutely free that your very existence is an act of rebellion.
15.Do not wait for the Last Judgment. It happens every day.
16.Blessed are the hearts that can bend; they shall never be broken.
17.In the midst of hate, I found there was, within me, an invincible love.
18.Fiction is the lie through which we tell the truth.
19.Man is the only creature who refuses to be what he is.
20.There is but one truly serious philosophical problem and that is suicide.
21.The purpose of a writer is to keep civilization from destroying itself.
22.There are more things to admire in men than to despise.
23.There is no sun without shadow, and it is essential to know the night.
24.Nobody realizes that some people expend tremendous energy merely to be normal.
25.There is no fate that cannot be surmounted by scorn.
26.Real generosity toward the future lies in giving all to the present.
27.Freedom is nothing else but a chance to be better, whereas enslavement is a certainty of the worst.
28.The need to be right is the sign of a vulgar mind.
29.An intellectual is someone whose mind watches itself.
30.The absurd is born of this confrontation between the human need and the unreasonable silence of the world.
31.This whole thing is not about heroism. It's about decency. It may seem a ridiculous idea, but the only way to fight the plague is with decency.
32.I leave Sisyphus at the foot of the mountain! One always finds one's burden again.
33.The realization that life is absurd cannot be an end, but only a beginning.
34.We always deceive ourselves twice about the people we love — first to their advantage, then to their disadvantage.
35.Man cannot do without beauty, and this is what our era pretends to want to disregard.
36.Seeking what is true is not seeking what is desirable.
37.I rebel; therefore I exist.
38.Nothing is more despicable than respect based on fear.
39.At any street corner the feeling of absurdity can strike any man in the face.
40.Man is always prey to his truths. Once he has admitted them, he cannot free himself from them.
41.For if there is a sin against life, it consists perhaps not so much in despairing of life as in hoping for another life and in eluding the implacable grandeur of this life.

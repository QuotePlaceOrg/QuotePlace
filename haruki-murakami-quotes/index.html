Haruki Murakami Quotes

Haruki Murakami (Novelist, born 1949). Below are 3 quotes by Haruki Murakami from QuotePlace, each as a beautiful quote card.

Novelist · born 1949

Haruki Murakami is a Japanese novelist whose surreal, melancholic books — Norwegian Wood, Kafka on the Shore, and 1Q84 among them — have been translated into more than fifty languages. He is also a devoted marathon runner.

Quotes by Haruki Murakami

1.If you only read the books that everyone else is reading, you can only think what everyone else is thinking.
2.Pain is inevitable. Suffering is optional.
3.And once the storm is over, you won't remember how you made it through, how you managed to survive. But one thing is certain: when you come out of the storm, you won't be the same person who walked in.

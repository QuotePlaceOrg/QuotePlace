Thomas Edison Quotes

Thomas Edison (American inventor and businessman, 1847 – 1931). Below are 6 quotes by Thomas Edison from QuotePlace, each as a text quote card.

American inventor and businessman · 1847 – 1931

Thomas Edison was an American inventor who developed the phonograph, the motion picture camera and the first practical electric light bulb. He held over a thousand US patents and founded the first industrial research laboratory at Menlo Park, New Jersey.

Quotes by Thomas Edison

1.Genius is one percent inspiration and ninety-nine percent perspiration.
2.If we did the things we are capable of, we would astound ourselves.
3.Opportunity is missed by most because it is dressed in overalls and looks like work.
4.Many of life's failures are people who did not realize how close they were to success when they gave up.
5.The first requisite for success is the ability to apply your physical and mental energies to one problem incessantly without growing weary.
6.I have not failed. I've just found 10,000 ways that won't work.

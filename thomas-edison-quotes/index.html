Thomas Edison Quotes

Thomas Edison (American inventor and businessman, 1847 – 1931). Below are 16 quotes by Thomas Edison from QuotePlace, each as a quote card.

American inventor and businessman · 1847 – 1931

Thomas Edison was an American inventor who developed the phonograph, the motion picture camera and the first practical electric light bulb. He held over a thousand US patents and founded the first industrial research laboratory at Menlo Park, New Jersey.

Quotes by Thomas Edison

1.Genius is one percent inspiration and ninety-nine percent perspiration.
2.If we did the things we are capable of, we would astound ourselves.
3.Opportunity is missed by most because it is dressed in overalls and looks like work.
4.Many of life's failures are people who did not realize how close they were to success when they gave up.
5.The first requisite for success is the ability to apply your physical and mental energies to one problem incessantly without growing weary.
6.I have not failed. I've just found 10,000 ways that won't work.
7.Our greatest weakness lies in giving up. The most certain way to succeed is always to try just one more time.
8.I never did a day's work in my life, it was all fun.
9.The value of an idea lies in the using of it.
10.I tell you genius is hard work, stick-to-it-iveness, and common sense.
11.Restlessness is discontent — and discontent is the first necessity of progress. Show me a thoroughly satisfied man — and I will show you a failure.
12.The three great essentials to achieve anything worthwhile are, first, hard work; second, stick-to-itiveness; third, common sense.
13.Everything comes to him who hustles while he waits.
14.We don't know a millionth of one percent about anything.
15.Time is really the only capital that any human being has, and the only thing he can't afford to lose.
16.I never did anything worth doing entirely by accident and none of my inventions came about totally by accident. They came about by hard work.

Irish playwright and critic; author of Pygmalion; Nobel Prize in Literature 1925.

Quotes by George Bernard Shaw

1.The reasonable man adapts himself to the world: the unreasonable one persists in trying to adapt the world to himself.
2.A life spent making mistakes is not only more honorable, but more useful than a life spent doing nothing.
3.Life isn't about finding yourself. Life is about creating yourself.
4.A life spent making mistakes is not only more honourable but more useful than a life spent in doing nothing.
5.I am of the opinion that my life belongs to the community, and as long as I live it is my privilege to do for it whatever I can.
6.We don't stop playing because we grow old; we grow old because we stop playing.
7.My reputation grows with every failure.
8.The possibilities are numerous once we decide to act and not react.
9.You see things; and you say, 'Why?' But I dream things that never were; and I say, 'Why not?'

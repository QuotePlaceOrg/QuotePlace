Virginia Woolf Quotes

Virginia Woolf (Novelist and essayist, 1882 – 1941). Below are 6 quotes by Virginia Woolf from QuotePlace, each as a text quote card.

Novelist and essayist · 1882 – 1941

Virginia Woolf was an English writer and a central figure of literary modernism, famous for stream-of-consciousness novels such as Mrs Dalloway (1925) and To the Lighthouse (1927). Her essay A Room of One's Own (1929) became a foundational feminist text on women and writing. With her husband Leonard she founded the Hogarth Press, which published her own work and that of T. S. Eliot and others.

Quotes by Virginia Woolf

1.A woman must have money and a room of her own if she is to write fiction.
2.One cannot think well, love well, sleep well, if one has not dined well.
3.I would venture to guess that Anon, who wrote so many poems without signing them, was often a woman.
4.Life is not a series of gig lamps symmetrically arranged; life is a luminous halo, a semi-transparent envelope surrounding us from the beginning of consciousness to the end.
5.For most of history, Anonymous was a woman.
6.I am made and remade continually. Different people draw different words from me.

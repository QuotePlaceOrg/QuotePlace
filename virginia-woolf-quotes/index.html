Virginia Woolf Quotes

Virginia Woolf (Novelist and essayist, 1882 – 1941). Below are 44 quotes by Virginia Woolf from QuotePlace, each as a quote card.

Novelist and essayist · 1882 – 1941

Virginia Woolf was an English writer and a central figure of literary modernism, famous for stream-of-consciousness novels such as Mrs Dalloway (1925) and To the Lighthouse (1927). Her essay A Room of One's Own (1929) became a foundational feminist text on women and writing. With her husband Leonard she founded the Hogarth Press, which published her own work and that of T. S. Eliot and others.

19.The future is dark, which is the best thing the future can be, I think.

22.She always had the feeling that it was very, very dangerous to live even one day.
23.The mind receives a myriad impressions — trivial, fantastic, evanescent, or engraved with the sharpness of steel.
24.Books are the mirrors of the soul.
25.For books continue each other, in spite of our habit of judging them separately.
26.Each had his past shut in him like the leaves of a book known to him by heart
27.Examine for a moment an ordinary mind on an ordinary day.
28.Second-hand books are wild books, homeless books; they have come together in vast flocks of variegated feather, and have a charm which the domesticated volumes of the library lack.
29.The past only comes back when the present runs so smoothly that it is like the sliding surface of a deep river.
30.The older one grows, the more one likes indecency.
31.It's not catastrophes, murders, deaths, diseases, that age and kill us; it's the way people look and laugh, and run up the steps of omnibuses.
32.Blame it or praise it, there is no denying the wild horse in us.

38.A whole lifetime was too short to bring out, the full flavour; to extract every ounce of pleasure, every shade of meaning.
39.It was enemies one wanted, not friends.
40.Rigid, the skeleton of habit alone upholds the human frame.

Anne Frank Quotes

Anne Frank (Diarist, 1929 – 1945). Below are 48 quotes by Anne Frank from QuotePlace, each as a quote card.

Diarist · 1929 – 1945

German-born Jewish diarist whose diary documents life in hiding during the Holocaust.

Quotes by Anne Frank

1.How wonderful it is that nobody need wait a single moment before starting to improve the world.
2.Think of all the beauty still left around you and be happy.
3.We all live with the objective of being happy; our lives are all different and yet the same.
4.No one has ever become poor by giving.
5.Parents can only give good advice or put them on the right paths, but the final forming of a persons character lies in their own hands.
6.Dead people receive more flowers than the living ones because regret is stronger than gratitude.
7.Despite everything, I believe that people are really good at heart.
8.I can shake off everything as I write; my sorrows disappear, my courage is reborn.
9.Whoever is happy will make others happy too.
10.Where there's hope, there's life. It fills us with fresh courage and makes us strong again.
11.In the long run, the sharpest weapon of all is a kind and gentle spirit.
12.I simply can't build up my hopes on a foundation consisting of confusion, misery, and death.
13.I don't think of all the misery, but of the beauty that still remains.
14.I've learned one thing: you only really get to know a person after a fight. Only then can you judge their true character!
15.The best remedy for those who are afraid, lonely or unhappy is to go outside, somewhere where they can be quiet, alone with the heavens, nature and God.
16.Paper has more patience than people.
17.Laziness may appear attractive, but work gives satisfaction.
18.I have often been downcast, but never in despair.
19.I don't want to have lived in vain like most people. I want to be useful or bring enjoyment to all people, even those I've never met. I want to go on living even after my death!
20.I know what I want, I have a goal, an opinion, I have a religion and love. Let me be myself and then I am satisfied.
21.I see the world gradually being turned into a wilderness, I hear the ever approaching thunder, which will destroy us too, I can feel the sufferings of millions and yet, if I look up into the heavens, I think that it will all come right, that this cruelty too will end, and that peace and tranquility will return again.
22.The weak fall, but the strong will remain and never go under!
23.You can be lonely even when you are loved by many people, since you are still not anybody's one and only.
24.What is done cannot be undone, but one can prevent it happening again.
25.Women should be respected as well! Generally speaking, men are held in great esteem in all parts of the world, so why shouldn't women have their share?
26.I want to write, but more than that, I want to bring out all kinds of things that lie buried deep in my heart.
27.It's difficult in times like these: ideals, dreams and cherished hopes rise within us, only to be crushed by grim reality.
28.Who would ever think that so much went on in the soul of a young girl?
29.I know that I can write, a couple of my stories are good, my descriptions of the 'Secret Annex' are humorous, there's a lot in my diary that speaks, but whether I have real talent remains to be seen.
30.If I haven't any talent for writing books or newspaper articles, well, then I can always write for myself.
31.I am the best and sharpest critic of my own work.
32.I've found that there is always some beauty left — in nature, sunshine, freedom, in yourself; these can all help you.
33.It's really a wonder that I haven't dropped all my ideals, because they seem so absurd and impossible to carry out.
34.I'm afraid that people who know me as I usually am will discover I have another side, a better and finer side.
35.As long as this exists, this sunshine and this cloudless sky, and as long as I can enjoy it, how can I be sad?
36.I think it's odd that grown-ups quarrel so easily and so often and about such petty matters. Up to now I always thought bickering was just something children did and that they outgrew it.
37.Everyone has inside of him a piece of good news. The good news is that you don't know how great you can be! How much you can love! What you can accomplish! And what your potential is!
38.Deep down, the young are lonelier than the old.
39.How noble and good everyone could be if, at the end of each day, they were to review their own behavior and weigh up the rights and wrongs. They would automatically try to do better at the start of each new day and, after a while, would certainly accomplish a great deal.
40.I must work, so as not to be a fool, to get on, to become a journalist, because that's what I want!
41.I hope I will be able to confide everything to you, as I have never been able to confide in anyone, and I hope you will be a great source of comfort and support.
42.I see the eight of us in the Annex as if we were a patch of blue sky surrounded by menacing black clouds. The perfectly round spot on which we're standing is still safe, but the clouds are moving in on us.
43.I long to ride a bike, dance, whistle, look at the world, feel young and know that I'm free, and yet I can't let it show.
44.I wander from room to room, climb up and down the stairs and feel like a songbird whose wings have been ripped off and who keeps hurling itself against the bars of its dark cage.
45.For someone like me, it is a very strange habit to write in a diary. Not only that I have never written before, but it strikes me that later neither I, nor anyone else, will care for the musings of a thirteen-year-old schoolgirl.
46.If I read a book that impresses me, I have to take myself firmly by the hand, before I mix with other people; otherwise they would think my mind rather queer.
47.People can tell you to keep your mouth shut, but it doesn't stop you having your own opinions. Even if people are still very young, they shouldn't be prevented from saying what they think.
48.Forgive me, Kitty, they don't call me a bundle of contradictions for nothing!

Quotes About Dreams

Every built thing was first an imagined thing. These 11 quotes about dreams honor the part of you that still wants more — and the courage it takes to pursue it. Keep dreaming loudly; the world needs what you're picturing.

Take up one idea. Make that one idea your life — think of it, dream of it, live on that idea.
Swami Vivekananda
I slept and dreamt that life was joy. I awoke and saw that life was service. I acted and behold, service was joy.
Rabindranath Tagore
You have to dream before your dreams can come true.
A.P.J. Abdul Kalam
Do not dwell in the past, do not dream of the future, concentrate the mind on the present moment.
Gautama Buddha
Sick on my journey — my dreams wander over withered fields.
Matsuo Bashō
It does not do to dwell on dreams and forget to live.
J.K. Rowling
You may say I'm a dreamer, but I'm not the only one.
John Lennon
Life is a dream.
Pedro Calderón de la Barca
If your dreams do not scare you, they are not big enough.
Ellen Johnson Sirleaf
What happens to a dream deferred? Does it dry up like a raisin in the sun?
Langston Hughes
Go confidently in the direction of your dreams. Live the life you have imagined.
Henry David Thoreau

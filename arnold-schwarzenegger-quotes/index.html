Arnold Schwarzenegger Quotes

Arnold Schwarzenegger (Actor, bodybuilder and former governor, born 1947). Below are 5 quotes by Arnold Schwarzenegger from QuotePlace, each as a text quote card.

Actor, bodybuilder and former governor · born 1947

Austrian-born bodybuilder who won seven Mr. Olympia titles before becoming one of Hollywood's biggest action stars with films such as The Terminator and Total Recall. He later served two terms as Governor of California, from 2003 to 2011.

2.Strength does not come from winning. Your struggles develop your strengths. When you go through hardships and decide not to surrender, that is strength.
3.Just remember, you can't climb the ladder of success with your hands in your pockets.

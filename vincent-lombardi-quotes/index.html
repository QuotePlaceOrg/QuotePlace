American football coach · 1913 – 1970

Vincent Thomas Lombardi was an American football coach who led the Green Bay Packers to five NFL championships in the 1960s, including the first two Super Bowls. The Super Bowl trophy bears his name, and his sayings on winning and effort are quoted far beyond sport.

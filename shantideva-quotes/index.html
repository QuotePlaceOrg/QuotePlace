Shantideva (Buddhist monk and scholar, 685 – 763). Below are 2 quotes by Shantideva from QuotePlace, each as a beautiful quote card.

Buddhist monk and scholar · 685 – 763

Indian Buddhist monk and scholar of Nalanda. His Bodhicaryavatara is a classic guide to the bodhisattva path.

If the problem can be solved, why worry? If the problem cannot be solved, worrying will do no good.
Shantideva
All the happiness in this world arises from wishing others to be happy; all the suffering arises from wishing oneself to be happy.
Shantideva

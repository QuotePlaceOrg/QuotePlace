Denis Waitley Quotes

Denis Waitley (American motivational author, born 1933). Below are 5 quotes by Denis Waitley from QuotePlace, each as a text quote card.

American motivational author · born 1933

Denis Waitley is an American motivational speaker and author of books on personal achievement, including The Psychology of Winning (1979).

Quotes by Denis Waitley

There are two primary choices in life: to accept conditions as they exist, or accept the responsibility for changing them.
You must welcome change as the rule but not as your ruler.
Happiness cannot be travelled to, owned, earned, worn or consumed. Happiness is the spiritual experience of living every minute with love, grace and gratitude.
A dream is your creative vision for your life in the future. You must break out of your current comfort zone and become comfortable with the unfamiliar and the unknown.
The only person who never makes mistakes is the person who never does anything.

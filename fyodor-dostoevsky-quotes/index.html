Fyodor Dostoevsky Quotes

Writer · 1821 – 1881

Russian novelist, author of Crime and Punishment and The Brothers Karamazov.

Quotes by Fyodor Dostoevsky

1.Taking a new step, uttering a new word, is what people fear most.
2.The mystery of human existence lies not in just staying alive, but in finding something to live for.
3.Pain and suffering are always inevitable for a large intelligence and a deep heart.
4.Man only likes to count his troubles; he doesn't calculate his happiness.
5.The darker the night, the brighter the stars.
6.The soul is healed by being with children.
7.It is not the brains that matter most, but that which guides them — character, heart, generous qualities, progressive ideas.
8.Man is fond of counting his troubles, but he does not count his joys.
9.Above all, don't lie to yourself.
10.To go wrong in one's own way is better than to go right in someone else's.
11.It is not the brains that matter most, but that which guides them — the character, the heart, generous qualities, progressive ideas.
12.What is hell? I maintain that it is the suffering of being unable to love.
13.Suffering is the sole origin of consciousness.
14.The wisest of all, in my opinion, is he who can, if only once a month, call himself a fool — a faculty unheard of nowadays.
15.It takes something more than intelligence to act intelligently.
16.Compassion is the chief law of human existence.
17.It is better to be unhappy and know the worst, than to be happy in a fool's paradise.
18.Love in action is a harsh and dreadful thing compared with love in dreams.
19.Talking nonsense is the sole privilege mankind possesses over the other organisms. It's by talking nonsense that one gets to the truth! I talk nonsense, therefore I'm human.
20.Is the world to go to pot, or am I to go without my tea? I say that the world may go to pot for me so long as I always get my tea.
21.Man is a mystery. It must be unraveled, and if you spend your whole life unraveling it, do not say that you have wasted your time; I occupy myself with this mystery because I want to be a human being.
22.To be a man among people and remain a man for ever, not to be downhearted nor to fall in whatever misfortunes may befall me — this is life; this is the task of life.
23.The awful thing is that beauty is mysterious as well as terrible. God and the devil are fighting there and the battlefield is the heart of man.
24.Love the animals, love the plants, love everything. If you love everything, you will perceive the divine mystery in things. Once you perceive it, you will begin to comprehend it better every day, and you will come at last to love the whole world with an all-embracing love.
25.The more I love humanity in general, the less I love man in particular.
26.But how could you live and have no story to tell?
27.Every man has reminiscences which he would not tell to everyone, but only to his friends.
28.It seems, in fact, as though the second half of a man's life is made up of nothing but the habits he has accumulated during the first half.
29.Nothing in this world is harder than speaking the truth, nothing easier than flattery.
30.Man is unhappy because he doesn't know he's happy. It's only that.
31.Love is such a priceless treasure that you can redeem the whole world by it, and expiate not only your own sins but the sins of others.

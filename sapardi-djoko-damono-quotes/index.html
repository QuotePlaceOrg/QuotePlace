Poet · 1940 – 2020

Sapardi Djoko Damono was an Indonesian poet beloved for lyrical, deceptively simple verse, including the collection Hujan Bulan Juni ('June Rain'). His line 'what perishes is time; we are eternal' is quoted across Indonesia.

What perishes is time; we are eternal.
Sapardi Djoko Damono

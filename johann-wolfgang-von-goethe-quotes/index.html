Johann Wolfgang von Goethe Quotes

Johann Wolfgang von Goethe (Writer and polymath, 1749 – 1832). Below are 25 quotes by Johann Wolfgang von Goethe from QuotePlace, each as a text quote card.

Writer and polymath · 1749 – 1832

Johann Wolfgang von Goethe was a German writer, poet, playwright and natural philosopher, regarded as Germany's greatest literary figure. His works include the drama Faust, The Sorrows of Young Werther and Wilhelm Meister's Apprenticeship.

Quotes by Johann Wolfgang von Goethe

1.Difficulties increase the nearer we get to the goal.
2.Great talent finds happiness in execution.
3.Character develops itself in the stream of life.
4.A really great talent finds its happiness in execution.
5.Mountains cannot be surmounted except by winding paths.
6.Knowing is not enough; we must apply!
7.In the end we retain from our studies only that which we practically apply.
8.The person born with a talent they are meant to use will find their greatest happiness in using it.
9.People are so constituted that everybody would rather undertake what they see others do, whether they have an aptitude for it or not.
10.If you must tell me your opinions, tell me what you believe in. I have plenty of douts of my own.
11.Treat people as if they were what they ought to be and you help them to become what they are capable of being.
12.Correction does much, but encouragement does more.
13.A man sees in the world what he carries in his heart.
14.Kindness is the golden chain by which society is bound together.
15.What is not started today is never finished tomorrow.
16.Wherever a man may happen to turn, whatever a man may undertake, he will always end up by returning to the path which nature has marked out for him.
17.The really unhappy person is the one who leaves undone what they can do, and starts doing what they don't understand; no wonder they come to grief.
18.Just trust yourself, then you will know how to live.
19.Sometimes our fate resembles a fruit tree in winter. Who would think that those branches would turn green again and blossom, but we hope it, we know it.
20.To think is easy. To act is hard. But the hardest thing in the world is to act in accordance with your thinking.
21.We are shaped and fashioned by what we love.
22.To be pleased with one's limits is a wretched state.
23.In the beginning was the deed.
24.None are more hopelessly enslaved than those who falsely believe they are free.
25.Talent is formed in stillness, character in the world.

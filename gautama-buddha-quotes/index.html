Gautama Buddha Quotes

Gautama Buddha (Spiritual teacher, c. 563 BCE – c. 483 BCE). Below are 51 quotes by Gautama Buddha from QuotePlace, each as a portrait card.

Spiritual teacher · c. 563 BCE – c. 483 BCE

Founder of Buddhism; born in Lumbini (present-day Nepal), his teachings form the basis of one of the world's major religions.

Quotes by Gautama Buddha

1.There is no path to happiness: happiness is the path.
2.Three things cannot be long hidden: the sun, the moon, and the truth.
3.Health is the greatest gift, contentment the greatest wealth, faithfulness the best relationship.
4.Do not dwell in the past, do not dream of the future, concentrate the mind on the present moment.
5.Holding on to anger is like grasping a hot coal with the intent of throwing it at someone else; you are the one who gets burned.
6.Hatred does not cease through hatred at any time. Hatred ceases through love. This is an unalterable law.
7.Conquer anger with non-anger. Conquer badness with goodness. Conquer meanness with generosity.
8.Drop by drop is the water pot filled. Likewise, the wise man, gathering it little by little, fills himself with good.
9.Work out your own salvation. Do not depend on others.
10.He is able who thinks he is able.
11.Those who are free of resentful thoughts surely find peace.
12.What we think, we become.
13.It is better to travel well than to arrive.
14.The mind is everything. What you think you become.
15.In separateness lies the world's great misery, in compassion lies the world's true strength.
16.Happiness comes when your work and words are of benefit to yourself and others.
17.Just as a candle cannot burn without fire, men cannot live without a spiritual life.
18.If you light a lamp for somebody, it will also brighten your path.
19.Your worst enemy cannot harm you as much as your own unguarded thoughts.
20.The way is not in the sky. The way is in the heart.
21.You will not be punished for your anger, you will be punished by your anger.
22.In a controversy the instant we feel anger we have already ceased striving for the truth, and have begun striving for ourselves.
23.Do not overrate what you have received, nor envy others. He who envies others does not obtain peace of mind.
24.To keep the body in good health is a duty... otherwise we shall not be able to keep our mind strong and clear.
25.There are only two mistakes one can make along the road to truth; not going all the way, and not starting.
26.To live a pure unselfish life, one must count nothing as ones own in the midst of abundance.
27.We are what we think. All that we are arises with our thoughts. With our thoughts, we make the world.
28.Your work is to discover your world and then with all your heart give yourself to it.
29.All that we are is the result of what we have thought. The mind is everything. What we think we become.
30.The foot feels the foot when it feels the ground.
31.No one saves us but ourselves. No one can and no one may. We ourselves must walk the path.
32.Thousands of candles can be lighted from a single candle, and the life of the candle will not be shortened. Happiness never decreases by being shared.
33.In the sky, there is no distinction of east and west; people create distinctions out of their own minds and then believe them to be true.
34.Thousands of candles can be lit from a single, and the life of the candle will not be shortened. Happiness never decreases by being shared.
35.Always be mindful of the kindness and not the faults of others.
36.Better than a thousand hollow words, is one word that brings peace.
37.A jug fills drop by drop.
38.Every human being is the author of his own health or disease.
39.Your body is precious. It is our vehicle for awakening. Treat it with care.
40.Chaos is inherent in all compounded things. Strive on with diligence.
41.No matter how hard the past, you can always begin again.
42.If we could see the miracle of a single flower clearly, our whole life would change.
43.You cannot travel the path until you have become the path itself.
44.We are shaped by our thoughts; we become what we think. When the mind is pure, joy follows like a shadow that never leaves.
45.Remembering a wrong is like carrying a burden on the mind.
46.The only real failure in life is not to be true to the best one knows.
47.However many holy words you read, However many you speak, What good will they do you If you do not act on upon them?
48.Meditation brings wisdom; lack of mediation leaves ignorance. Know well what leads you forward and what hold you back, and choose the path that leads to wisdom.
49.If you propose to speak, always ask yourself, is it true, is it necessary, is it kind.
50.An idea that is developed and put into action is more important than an idea that exists only as an idea.
51.What you are is what you have been. What you’ll be is what you do now.

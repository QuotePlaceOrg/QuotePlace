Hindi poet · 1907 – 1987

Mahadevi Varma was a leading poet of the Chhayavaad movement in Hindi literature and a pioneer of women's education in India. Her collections, including Rashmi and Neerja, weave mysticism, longing and compassion. She received the Jnanpith Award and the Padma Vibhushan.

Quotes by Mahadevi Varma

I am a cloud filled with the water of sorrow.
Burn on, sweetly, sweetly, O my little lamp.
If you had come even once, flowers of tenderness and tidings would have lain strewn along your path.

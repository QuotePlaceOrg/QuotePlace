Helen Keller Quotes

Helen Keller (Author, activist, 1880 – 1968). Below are 22 quotes by Helen Keller from QuotePlace, each as a portrait card.

Author, activist · 1880 – 1968

American author and activist who, deaf and blind from childhood, became a renowned advocate for disability rights.

Quotes by Helen Keller

1.Life is either a daring adventure or nothing at all.
2.Alone we can do so little; together we can do so much.
3.Keep your face to the sunshine and you cannot see a shadow.
4.Although the world is full of suffering, it is also full of the overcoming of it.
5.Keep yourself to the sunshine and you cannot see the shadow.
6.Never bend your head. Always hold it high. Look the world right in the eye.
7.The most beautiful things in the world cannot be seen or even touched. They must be felt with the heart.
8.We could never learn to be brave and patient if there were only joy in the world.
9.Face your deficiencies and acknowledge them; but do not let them master you. Let them teach you patience, sweetness, insight.
10.No pessimist ever discovered the secrets of the stars, or sailed to an uncharted land, or opened a new heaven to the human spirit.
11.Character cannot be developed in ease and quiet. Only through experience of trial and suffering can the soul be strengthened, vision cleared, ambition inspired, and success achieved.
12.The best and most beautiful things in the world cannot be seen, nor touched... but are felt in the heart.
13.One can never consent to creep when one feels an impulse to soar.
14.Optimism is the faith that leads to achievement.
15.What we have once enjoyed we can never lose. All that we love deeply becomes a part of us.
16.So long as you can sweeten another's pain, life is not in vain.
17.I am only one, but still I am one. I cannot do everything, but still I can do something.
18.Knowledge is love and light and vision.
19.The best and most beautiful things in the world cannot be seen or even touched — they must be felt with the heart.
20.Optimism is the faith that leads to achievement. Nothing can be done without hope and confidence.
21.I would rather walk with a friend in the dark, than alone in the light.
22.The only thing worse than being blind is having sight but no vision.

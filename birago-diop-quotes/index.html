Senegalese poet and storyteller · 1906 – 1989

Birago Diop was a Senegalese poet and storyteller who retold Wolof folktales in Tales of Amadou Koumba and celebrated Africa's oral tradition in poems such as 'Breaths'.

Listen more often to things than to beings.
Birago Diop

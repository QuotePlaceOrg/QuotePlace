Poet · 1914 – 1953

Dylan Thomas was a Welsh poet whose sonorous, musical verse made him famous on both sides of the Atlantic. Under Milk Wood, his play for voices, and poems such as Fern Hill and Do Not Go Gentle into That Good Night, are classics of 20th-century poetry.

Do not go gentle into that good night. Rage, rage against the dying of the light.
Dylan Thomas

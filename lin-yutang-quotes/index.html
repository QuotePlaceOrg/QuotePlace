Lin Yutang Quotes

Lin Yutang (Writer, 1895 – 1976). Below are 3 quotes by Lin Yutang from QuotePlace, each as a beautiful quote card.

Writer · 1895 – 1976

Lin Yutang was a Chinese writer and inventor who introduced Chinese philosophy and everyday life to Western readers with books such as My Country and My People and The Importance of Living. He championed a humorous, unhurried philosophy of life.

Quotes by Lin Yutang

Besides the noble art of getting things done, there is the noble art of leaving things undone. The wisdom of life consists in the elimination of non-essentials.
No one realizes how beautiful it is to travel until he comes home and rests his head on his old, familiar pillow.
I have done my best: that is about all the philosophy of living one needs.

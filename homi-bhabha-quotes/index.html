Nuclear physicist · 1909 – 1966

Homi Jehangir Bhabha was an Indian nuclear physicist who founded the Tata Institute of Fundamental Research and chaired the Atomic Energy Commission. Regarded as the father of India's nuclear programme, he built it around a three-stage plan based on India's thorium reserves.

Quotes by Homi Bhabha

1.No power is more expensive than no power.
2.My success will not depend on what A or B thinks of me. My success will be what I make of my work.
3.A scientific institution has to be grown with great care like a tree — its growth in terms of quality and achievement can only be accelerated to a very limited extent.

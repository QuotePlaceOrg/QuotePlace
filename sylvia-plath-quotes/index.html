Sylvia Plath Quotes

Sylvia Plath (Poet and novelist, 1932 – 1963). Below are 4 quotes by Sylvia Plath from QuotePlace, each as a text quote card.

Poet and novelist · 1932 – 1963

Sylvia Plath was an American poet and novelist best known for her novel The Bell Jar and the posthumous Ariel poems. Her intensely confessional writing explored identity, depression and the natural world. She was awarded a posthumous Pulitzer Prize for her Collected Poems in 1982.

Quotes by Sylvia Plath

1.I took a deep breath and listened to the old brag of my heart. I am, I am, I am.
2.I can never read all the books I want; I can never be all the people I want and live all the lives I want.
3.Out of the ash I rise with my red hair, and I eat men like air.
4.Kiss me, and you will see how important I am.

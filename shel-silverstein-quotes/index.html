Writer, cartoonist and songwriter · 1930 – 1999

Sheldon Allan Silverstein, American writer, cartoonist and songwriter; author of the children's classics The Giving Tree, Where the Sidewalk Ends and A Light in the Attic, and writer of the Johnny Cash hit "A Boy Named Sue".

Quotes by Shel Silverstein

2.I will not play at tug o' war. I'd rather play at hug o' war.

Quotes by Byron Pulsifer

1.Fate is in your hands and no one elses
2.What you give is what you get.
3.The best teacher is experience learned from failures.
4.What you fear is that which requires action to overcome.
5.If you cannot be silent be brilliant and thoughtful.
6.Someone is special only if you tell them.
7.Give thanks for the rain of life that propels us to reach new horizons.
8.Transformation doesn't take place with a vacuum; instead, it occurs when we are indirectly and directly connected to all those around us.
9.Your destiny isn't just fate; it is how you use your own developed abilities to get what you want.
10.Everyone can taste success when the going is easy, but few know how to taste victory when times get tough.
11.Patience is a virtue but you will never ever accomplish anything if you don't exercise action over patience.
12.Many people think of prosperity that concerns money only to forget that true prosperity is of the mind.
13.Today, give a stranger a smile without waiting for it may be the joy they need to have a great day.
14.Sadness may be part of life but there is no need to let it dominate your entire life.
15.To give hope to someone occurs when you teach them how to use the tools to do it for themselves.
16.You can be what you want to be. You have the power within and we will help you always.
17.Courage is not about taking risks unknowingly but putting your own being in front of challenges that others may not be able to.
18.Responsibility is not inherited, it is a choice that everyone needs to make at some point in their life.
19.You can't create in a vacuum. Life gives you the material and dreams can propel new beginnings.
20.You can't trust without risk but neither can you live in a cocoon.
21.Look forward to spring as a time when you can start to see what nature has to offer once again.
22.Fear of failure is one attitude that will keep you at the same point in your life.
23.To be thoughtful and kind only takes a few seconds compared to the timeless hurt caused by one rude gesture.
24.If you have no respect for your own values how can you be worthy of respect from others.
25.Wishes can be your best avenue of getting what you want when you turn wishes into action. Action moves your wish to the forefront from thought to reality.
26.Adversity isn't set against you to fail; adversity is a way to build your character so that you can succeed over and over again through perseverance.
27.Truth isn't all about what actually happens but more about how what has happened is interpreted.
28.Passion creates the desire for more and action fuelled by passion creates a future.
29.Experience can only be gained by doing not by thinking or dreaming.
30.It can't be spring if your heart is filled with past failures.
31.I may not know everything, but everything is not known yet anyway.
32.Transformation does not start with some one else changing you; transformation is an inner self reworking of what you are now to what you will be.
33.Time is not a measure the length of a day or month or year but more a measure of what you have accomplished.
34.Complaining doesn't change a thing only taking action does.
35.Strength to carry on despite the odds means you have faith in your own abilities and know how.
36.Spring is a time for rebirth and the fulfilment of new life.
37.Respect is not something that you can ask for, buy or borrow. Respect is what you earn from each person no matter their background or status.
38.Bold is not the act of foolishness but the attribute and inner strength to act when others will not so as to move forward not backward.
39.Staying in one place is the best path to be taken over and surpassed by many.
40.To know your purpose is to live a life of direction, and in that direction is found peace and tranquillity.
41.Into each life rain must fall but rain can be the giver of life and it is all in your attitude that makes rain produce sunshine.
42.Once a brother, always a brother, no matter the distance, no matter the difference, and no matter the issue.
43.The finest memories are those where we have helped someone without expecting anything in return.
44.Give your love to all, trust in the team to which you are joined, and do not do wrong to others for your own self-gratification.
45.There is one truth that must be sought after first before you can go further and that truth is that you must take charge of your own life for you cannot lead a life of truth if you let someone else take responsibility for your thoughts or actions.
46.Make each day of the week like Friday and your life will take on new enthusiasm.
47.Wednesday is a day to help others celebrate life. You and only you are accountable for what you extend and give to others.

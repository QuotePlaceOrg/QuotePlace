Miyamoto Musashi Quotes

Miyamoto Musashi (Swordsman and strategist, 1584 – 1645). Below are 24 quotes by Miyamoto Musashi from QuotePlace, each as a portrait card.

Swordsman and strategist · 1584 – 1645

Legendary Japanese swordsman, undefeated in over sixty duels, author of The Book of Five Rings and the Dokkodo.

Quotes by Miyamoto Musashi

1.Accept everything just the way it is.
2.Think lightly of yourself and deeply of the world.
3.Be detached from desire your whole life long.
4.Do not seek pleasure for its own sake.
5.Perceive that which cannot be seen with the eye.
6.You can only fight the way you practice.
7.Resentment and complaint are appropriate neither for oneself nor others.
8.In strategy it is important to see distant things as if they were close and to take a distanced view of close things.
9.In all things, have no preferences.
10.Do nothing that is of no use.
11.You must understand that there is more than one path to the top of the mountain.
12.It may seem difficult at first, but everything is difficult at first.
13.Today is victory over yourself of yesterday; tomorrow is your victory over lesser men.
14.The true science of martial arts means practicing them in such a way that they will be useful at any time, and to teach them in such a way that they will be useful in all things.
15.From one thing, know ten thousand things.
16.If you know the way broadly you will see it in everything.
17.The ultimate aim of martial arts is not having to use them.
18.Do not regret what you have done.
19.It is difficult to understand the universe if you only study one planet.
20.Be detached from desire your whole life.
21.Get beyond love and grief: exist for the good of man.
22.You should not have any special fondness for a particular weapon, or anything else, for that matter.
23.Do not, under any circumstances, depend on a partial feeling.
24.Step by step walk the thousand-mile road.

Eckhart Tolle Quotes

Eckhart Tolle (Spiritual teacher and author, born 1948). Below are 7 quotes by Eckhart Tolle from QuotePlace, each as a text quote card.

Spiritual teacher and author · born 1948

Eckhart Tolle is a German-born spiritual teacher living in Canada, author of The Power of Now (1997) and A New Earth (2005), books on presence and the ego that became international bestsellers.

Quotes by Eckhart Tolle

1.It is not uncommon for people to spend their whole life waiting to start living.
2.You cannot find yourself by going into the past. You can find yourself by coming into the present.
3.The past has no power to stop you from being present now. Only your grievance about the past can do that.
4.Whenever something negative happens to you, there is a deep lesson concealed within it.
5.You do not become good by trying to be good, but by finding the goodness that is already within you.
6.The greater part of human pain is unnecessary. It is self-created as long as the unobserved mind runs your life.
7.Realize deeply that the present moment is all you ever have.

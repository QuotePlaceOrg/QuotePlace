English novelist known for Pride and Prejudice and Emma, celebrated for irony and social observation.

Quotes by Jane Austen

1.There is no charm equal to tenderness of heart.

3.Ah! There is nothing like staying at home, for real comfort.
4.I declare after all there is no enjoyment like reading!

Poet · 365 – 427

Tao Yuanming was a Chinese poet who gave up a government career to farm and write in the countryside. His 'Peach Blossom Spring' and drinking poems made him the patron saint of the simple, self-chosen life.

I pluck chrysanthemums beneath the eastern hedge, and gaze at ease at the southern mountains.
Tao Yuanming

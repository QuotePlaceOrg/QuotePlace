Mahatma Gandhi Quotes

Mahatma Gandhi (Leader of Indian independence movement, 1869 – 1948). Below are 17 quotes by Mahatma Gandhi from QuotePlace, each as a portrait card.

Leader of Indian independence movement · 1869 – 1948

Leader of India's nonviolent independence movement; pioneered satyagraha and became the father of the Indian nation.

Quotes by Mahatma Gandhi

1.In a gentle way, you can shake the world.
2.Your beliefs become your thoughts, your thoughts become your words, your words become your actions, your actions become your habits, your habits become your values, your values become your destiny.
3.The weak can never forgive. Forgiveness is the attribute of the strong.
4.A man is but the product of his thoughts. What he thinks, he becomes.
5.Strength does not come from physical capacity. It comes from an indomitable will.
6.Earth provides enough to satisfy every man's needs, but not every man's greed.
7.You must not lose faith in humanity. Humanity is an ocean; if a few drops of the ocean are dirty, the ocean does not become dirty.
8.Happiness is when what you think, what you say, and what you do are in harmony.
9.Prayer is not asking. It is a longing of the soul.
10.Where there is love there is life.
11.It is the quality of our work which will please God, not the quantity.
12.Our greatness lies not so much in being able to remake the world as being able to remake ourselves.
13.Non-violence is the greatest force at the disposal of mankind.
14.What do I think of Western civilization? I think it would be a very good idea.
15.An eye for an eye will only make the whole world blind.
16.My life is my message.
17.Be the change that you wish to see in the world.

Michael Phelps Quotes

Michael Phelps (Swimmer, born 1985). Below are 4 quotes by Michael Phelps from QuotePlace, each as a text quote card.

Swimmer · born 1985

American swimmer and the most decorated Olympian of all time, with 28 Olympic medals, 23 of them gold. He won a record eight golds at the 2008 Beijing Games and has since become a prominent advocate for mental health awareness.

Quotes by Michael Phelps

1.You can't put a limit on anything. The more you dream, the farther you get.
2.I think goals should never be easy, they should force you to work, even if they are uncomfortable at the time.
3.If you want to be the best, you have to do things that other people aren't willing to do.
4.Things won't go perfect. It's all about how you adapt from those things and learn from mistakes.

Mary Kay Ash (Businesswoman, 1918 – 2001). Below are 3 quotes by Mary Kay Ash from QuotePlace, each as a text quote card.

Businesswoman · 1918 – 2001

Mary Kay Ash founded Mary Kay Cosmetics (now Mary Kay Inc.) in 1963 with $5,000, building it into one of the world's largest direct sellers of skin care and cosmetics through a network of independent beauty consultants.

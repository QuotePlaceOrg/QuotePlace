Playwright and president · 1936 – 2011

Václav Havel was a Czech playwright and dissident who became the first president of post-communist Czechoslovakia and then of the Czech Republic. His essays, especially The Power of the Powerless, articulated the moral resistance of living in truth under totalitarianism.

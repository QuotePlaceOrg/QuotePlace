Kazi Nazrul Islam Quotes

Kazi Nazrul Islam (Bengali poet, 1899 – 1976). Below are 4 quotes by Kazi Nazrul Islam from QuotePlace, each as a text quote card.

Bengali poet · 1899 – 1976

Kazi Nazrul Islam, the Bidrohi Kavi or Rebel Poet, was a Bengali poet, musician and revolutionary voice against colonial oppression. His poem Bidrohi and his songs of Hindu-Muslim unity made him a legend across Bengal. He is the national poet of Bangladesh.

Quotes by Kazi Nazrul Islam

1.Say, O hero! Say — my head is held high!
2.Break open the iron gates of that prison; smash the blood-stained altar of stone.
3.We are two flowers upon a single stalk — the Hindu and the Muslim.
4.I sing the song of equality, where all barriers and differences have melted into one.

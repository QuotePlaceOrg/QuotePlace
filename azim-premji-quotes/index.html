Azim Premji (Chairman of Wipro, born 1945). Below are 3 quotes by Azim Premji from QuotePlace, each as a text quote card.

Chairman of Wipro · born 1945

Azim Premji took over his family's cooking-oil business at twenty-one and diversified Wipro into a global IT services leader. One of India's most generous philanthropists, he was the first Indian to sign the Giving Pledge and has endowed the Azim Premji Foundation with most of his wealth.

Quotes by Azim Premji

1.If people are not laughing at your goals, your goals are too small.
2.Success is achieved twice: once in the mind, and the second time in the real world.
3.I strongly believe that those of us who are privileged to have wealth should contribute significantly to try and create a better world for the millions who are far less privileged.

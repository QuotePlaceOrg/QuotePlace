Ramana Maharshi Quotes

Ramana Maharshi (Hindu sage, 1879 – 1950). Below are 4 quotes by Ramana Maharshi from QuotePlace, each as a portrait card.

Hindu sage · 1879 – 1950

Indian sage of Advaita Vedanta known for his teachings on self-inquiry ('Who am I?') at Arunachala.

Quotes by Ramana Maharshi

Your own Self-realization is the greatest service you can render the world.
Happiness is your nature. It is not wrong to desire it. What is wrong is seeking it outside when it is inside.
Silence is also conversation.
There is no greater mystery than this: being reality ourselves, we seek to gain reality.

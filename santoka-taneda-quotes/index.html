Poet · 1882 – 1940

Santoka Taneda was a Japanese Zen monk and free-verse haiku poet who wandered the country on foot for years, begging his way from temple to temple. His spare poems record mountains, rain, and sake with disarming honesty.

Deeper and deeper I go, into the green mountains.
Santoka Taneda

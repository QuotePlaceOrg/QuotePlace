Poet and statesman · 1037 – 1101

Su Shi, also known as Su Dongpo, was a Song-dynasty poet, essayist, calligrapher, and statesman. Exiled repeatedly for his outspokenness, he answered misfortune with some of the most joyful and philosophical verse in Chinese literature.

Quotes by Su Shi

1.What is life like, wherever we travel? A wild goose, leaving footprints on the snow.
2.I would rather eat a meal without meat than live in a house without bamboo.

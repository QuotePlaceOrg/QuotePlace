Ramakrishna Quotes

Ramakrishna (Hindu mystic, 1836 – 1886). Below are 4 quotes by Ramakrishna from QuotePlace, each as a portrait card.

Hindu mystic · 1836 – 1886

19th-century Indian mystic of Bengal; his teachings on religious pluralism influenced modern Hindu thought.

Quotes by Ramakrishna

1.As many faiths, so many paths.
2.Do not be small minded. Do not pray for gourds and pumpkins from God, when you should be asking for pure love and pure knowledge to dawn within every heart.
3.God can be realized through all paths. All religions are true.
4.As long as I live, so long do I learn.

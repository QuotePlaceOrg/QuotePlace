Bill Gates Quotes

Bill Gates (Co-founder of Microsoft, born 1955). Below are 5 quotes by Bill Gates from QuotePlace, each as a text quote card.

Co-founder of Microsoft · born 1955

Bill Gates co-founded Microsoft in 1975 and built the software that powered the personal computer revolution, leading the company for 25 years. He later devoted himself to philanthropy, co-founding the Gates Foundation, one of the world's largest charitable organisations.

3.We always overestimate the change that will occur in the next two years and underestimate the change that will occur in the next ten years.

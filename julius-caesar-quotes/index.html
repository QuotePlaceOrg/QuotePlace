Roman general and statesman · 100 BC – 44 BC

Gaius Julius Caesar was a Roman general and statesman whose conquest of Gaul extended Rome's power to the Rhine and the English Channel. As dictator he reformed the calendar and the state before his assassination on the Ides of March, 44 BC. His commentaries on the Gallic Wars remain classics of Latin prose.

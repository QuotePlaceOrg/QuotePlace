Confucius Quotes

Confucius (Philosopher, 551 – 479). Below are 17 quotes by Confucius from QuotePlace, each as a portrait quote card.

Philosopher · 551 – 479

Chinese philosopher whose teachings in the Analects shaped East Asian ethics, education, and governance for two millennia.

Quotes by Confucius

The superior man is modest in his speech, but exceeds in his actions.
To see what is right and not to do it is want of courage.
He who learns but does not think, is lost. He who thinks but does not learn is in great danger.
When we see men of a contrary character, we should turn inwards and examine ourselves.
Real knowledge is to know the extent of one's ignorance.
The superior man thinks always of virtue; the common man thinks of comfort.
To go beyond is as wrong as to fall short.
To learn and at due times to repeat what one has learnt, is that not after all a pleasure?
The superior man is conversant with righteousness; the mean man is conversant with gain.
By nature, men are nearly alike; by practice, they get to be wide apart.
What you do not want done to yourself, do not do to others.
Everything has beauty, but not everyone sees it.
It does not matter how slowly you go as long as you do not stop.
Wherever you go, go with all your heart.
When anger rises, think of the consequences.
To know what you know and what you do not know, that is true knowledge.
If you make a mistake and do not correct it, this is called a mistake.

William Shakespeare Quotes

William Shakespeare (Playwright and poet, 1564 – 1616). Below are 9 quotes by William Shakespeare from QuotePlace, each as a portrait quote card.

Playwright and poet · 1564 – 1616

English playwright and poet, widely regarded as the greatest writer in the English language; author of Hamlet and Macbeth.

Quotes by William Shakespeare

The fault, dear Brutus, is not in our stars, but in ourselves.
Brevity is the soul of wit.
Cowards die many times before their deaths; the valiant never taste of death but once.
Love all, trust a few, do wrong to none.
Some are born great, some achieve greatness, and some have greatness thrust upon them.
The course of true love never did run smooth.
All that glitters is not gold.
Love looks not with the eyes, but with the mind, and therefore is winged Cupid painted blind.
How far that little candle throws his beams! So shines a good deed in a weary world.

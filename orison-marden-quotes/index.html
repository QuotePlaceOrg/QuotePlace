Inspirational author · 1848 – 1924

Orison Swett Marden was an American inspirational author and founder of Success magazine in 1897. His books, such as Pushing to the Front, were early classics of the self-help genre.

Quotes by Orison Marden

The Creator has not given you a longing to do that which you have no ability to do.
Most of our obstacles would melt away if, instead of cowering before them, we should make up our minds to walk boldly through them.
All men who have achieved great things have been great dreamers.

Novelist · 1913 – 1994

Ralph Ellison was an American novelist from Oklahoma City whose single completed novel, Invisible Man, won the National Book Award in 1953 and became a classic of American literature. Trained first as a jazz musician, he brought a musician's ear to his prose about race and identity.

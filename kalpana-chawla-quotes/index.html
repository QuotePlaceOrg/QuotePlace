Astronaut · 1962 – 2003

Kalpana Chawla was an Indian-American astronaut and engineer, the first woman of Indian origin to fly in space. She flew on two Space Shuttle Columbia missions and died in the Columbia disaster of 1 February 2003, along with her six crewmates.

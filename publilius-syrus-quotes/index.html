Publilius Syrus Quotes

Publilius Syrus (Latin writer of maxims, 85 BC – 43 BC). Below are 7 quotes by Publilius Syrus from QuotePlace, each as a text quote card.

Latin writer of maxims · 85 BC – 43 BC

Publilius Syrus was a Latin writer of the 1st century BC who was brought to Italy as a slave from Syria and later freed. He is known for his Sententiae, a collection of moral maxims drawn from his mimes.

Quotes by Publilius Syrus

1.A rolling stone gathers no moss.
2.While we stop to think, we often miss our opportunity.
3.Better be ignorant of a matter than half know it.
4.I have often regretted my speech, never my silence.
5.Do not turn back when you are just at the goal.
6.Never promise more than you can perform.
7.Anyone can hold the helm when the sea is calm.

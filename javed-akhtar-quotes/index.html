Poet, lyricist and screenwriter · born 1945

Javed Akhtar is an Indian poet, film lyricist and screenwriter who, with Salim Khan, wrote classics such as Sholay and Deewaar. His Urdu poetry collections Tarkash and Lava combine romance with sharp social conscience. He has won multiple National Film Awards and the Padma Bhushan.

Quotes by Javed Akhtar

2.Everyone stands one step away from happiness; every house is short of just one room.
3.I do not like going where everyone goes; I do not like travelling the well-trodden road.
4.Everywhere, in every place, there are countless people — and yet man remains a victim of loneliness.

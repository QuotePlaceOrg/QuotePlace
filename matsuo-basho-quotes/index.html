Matsuo Basho (Poet, 1644 – 1694). Below are 2 quotes by Matsuo Basho from QuotePlace, each as a beautiful quote card.

Poet · 1644 – 1694

Matsuo Basho was Japan's greatest haiku master, who raised the short comic verse of his day into a profound art. His travel diary The Narrow Road to the Deep North records a life spent walking Japan's roads in search of poetry.

Quotes by Matsuo Basho

1.Every day is a journey, and the journey itself is home.
2.There is nothing you can see that is not a flower; there is nothing you can think that is not the moon.

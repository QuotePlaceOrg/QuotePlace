German poet and playwright · 1759 – 1805

Friedrich Schiller was a German poet, playwright and philosopher whose dramas include The Robbers, Wallenstein and Wilhelm Tell. His "Ode to Joy" was set to music by Beethoven in the Ninth Symphony.

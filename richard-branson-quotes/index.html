Founder of the Virgin Group · born 1950

British entrepreneur Richard Branson founded the Virgin brand with a mail-order record business in 1970 and expanded it into airlines, telecoms, hotels and space tourism. A flamboyant risk-taker, he has set world records in ballooning and flew to the edge of space aboard his own Virgin Galactic craft in 2021.

2.Business opportunities are like buses, there's always another one coming.

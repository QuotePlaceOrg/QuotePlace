Nelson Mandela Quotes

Nelson Mandela (Anti-apartheid leader, President of South Africa, 1918 – 2013). Below are 49 quotes by Nelson Mandela from QuotePlace, each as a portrait card.

Anti-apartheid leader, President of South Africa · 1918 – 2013

South African anti-apartheid revolutionary who spent 27 years in prison and became the country's first Black president in 1994.

Quotes by Nelson Mandela

1.Education is the most powerful weapon which you can use to change the world.
2.Do not judge me by my successes, judge me by how many times I fell down and got back up again.
3.There can be no keener revelation of a society's soul than the way in which it treats its children.
4.After climbing a great hill, one only finds that there are many more hills to climb.
5.There is no passion to be found playing small — in settling for a life that is less than the one you are capable of living.
6.I learned that courage was not the absence of fear, but the triumph over it.
7.May your choices reflect your hopes, not your fears.
8.There is nothing like returning to a place that remains unchanged to find the ways in which you yourself have altered.
9.No one is born hating another person because of the color of his skin, or his background, or his religion.
10.A good head and a good heart are always a formidable combination.
11.Man's goodness is a flame that can be hidden but never extinguished.
12.For to be free is not merely to cast off one's chains, but to live in a way that respects and enhances the freedom of others.
13.It always seems impossible until it's done.
14.Part of being optimistic is keeping one's head pointed toward the sun, one's feet moving forward.
15.I never lose. I either win or learn.
16.I am fundamentally an optimist. Whether that comes from nature or nurture, I cannot say.
17.Resentment is like drinking poison and then hoping it will kill your enemies.
18.Money won't create success, the freedom to make it will.
19.A winner is a dreamer who never gives up.
20.A good head and good heart are always a formidable combination.
21.I am fundamentally an optimist. Whether that comes from nature or nurture, I cannot say. Part of being optimistic is keeping one's head pointed toward the sun, one's feet moving forward.
22.We must use time wisely and forever realize that the time is always ripe to do right.
23.Education is the great engine of personal development. It is through education that the daughter of a peasant can become a doctor, that the son of a mineworker can become the head of the mine, that a child of farm workers can become the president of a great nation.
24.No country can really develop unless its citizens are educated.
25.Courageous people do not fear forgiving, for the sake of peace.
26.I have cherished the ideal of a democratic and free society in which all persons live together in harmony and with equal opportunities.
27.Lead from the back, and let others believe they are in front.
28.If you talk to a man in a language he understands, that goes to his head. If you talk to him in his language, that goes to his heart.
29.The greatest glory in living lies not in never falling, but in rising every time we fall.
30.If you want to make peace with your enemy, you have to work with your enemy. Then he becomes your partner.
31.People must learn to hate, and if they can learn to hate, they can be taught to love, for love comes more naturally to the human heart than its opposite.
32.As I walked out the door toward the gate that would lead to my freedom, I knew if I didn't leave my bitterness and hatred behind, I'd still be in prison.
33.Overcoming poverty is not a gesture of charity. It is an act of justice.
34.I am not a saint, unless you think of a saint as a sinner who keeps on trying.
35.Never, never, and never again shall it be that this beautiful land will again experience the oppression of one by another.
36.Like slavery and apartheid, poverty is not natural. It is man-made and it can be overcome and eradicated by the actions of human beings.
37.Sometimes it falls upon a generation to be great. You can be that great generation.
38.Only free men can negotiate; prisoners cannot enter into contracts. Your freedom and mine cannot be separated.
39.Our march to freedom is irreversible. We must not allow fear to stand in our way.
40.They recognised that an injury to one is an injury to all and therefore acted together in defense of justice and a common human decency.
41.I have walked that long road to freedom. I have tried not to falter; I have made missteps along the way.
42.It is in your hands, to make a better world for all who live in it.
43.There is no easy walk to freedom anywhere, and many of us will have to pass through the valley of the shadow of death again and again before we reach the mountaintop of our desires.
44.I was not born with a hunger to be free. I was born free — free in every way that I could know.
45.A man who takes away another man's freedom is a prisoner of hatred, he is locked behind the bars of prejudice and narrow-mindedness.
46.Freedom is indivisible; the chains on any one of my people were the chains on all of them, the chains on all of my people were the chains on me.
47.The brave man is not he who does not feel afraid, but he who conquers that fear.
48.I am not truly free if I am taking away someone else's freedom, just as surely as I am not free when my freedom is taken from me.
49.When I walked out of prison, that was my mission, to liberate the oppressed and the oppressor both.

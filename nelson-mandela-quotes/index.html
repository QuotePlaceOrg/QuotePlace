Nelson Mandela Quotes

Nelson Mandela (Anti-apartheid leader, President of South Africa, 1918 – 2013). Below are 10 quotes by Nelson Mandela from QuotePlace, each as a portrait card.

Anti-apartheid leader, President of South Africa · 1918 – 2013

South African anti-apartheid revolutionary who spent 27 years in prison and became the country's first Black president in 1994.

Quotes by Nelson Mandela

1.Education is the most powerful weapon which you can use to change the world.
2.Do not judge me by my successes, judge me by how many times I fell down and got back up again.
3.There can be no keener revelation of a society's soul than the way in which it treats its children.
4.After climbing a great hill, one only finds that there are many more hills to climb.
5.There is no passion to be found playing small — in settling for a life that is less than the one you are capable of living.
6.I learned that courage was not the absence of fear, but the triumph over it.
7.May your choices reflect your hopes, not your fears.
8.There is nothing like returning to a place that remains unchanged to find the ways in which you yourself have altered.
9.No one is born hating another person because of the color of his skin, or his background, or his religion.
10.A good head and a good heart are always a formidable combination.

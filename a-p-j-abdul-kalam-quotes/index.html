A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Quotes

A.P.J. Abdul Kalam (Scientist and President of India, 1931 – 2015). Below are 19 quotes by A.P.J. Abdul Kalam from QuotePlace, each as a portrait card.

Scientist and President of India · 1931 – 2015

Indian aerospace scientist and the 11th President of India (2002-2007), known as the 'Missile Man of India'.

Quotes by A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

1.All of us do not have equal talent. But, all of us have an equal opportunity to develop our talents.
2.If you want to shine like a sun, first burn like a sun.
3.Don't take rest after your first victory because if you fail in second, more lips are waiting to say that your first victory was just luck.
4.You have to dream before your dreams can come true.
5.Thinking should become your capital asset, no matter whatever ups and downs you come across in your life.
6.Failure will never overtake me if my determination to succeed is strong enough.
7.Man needs his difficulties because they are necessary to enjoy success.
8.Dream is not that which you see while sleeping; it is something that does not let you sleep.
9.To succeed in your mission, you must have single-minded devotion to your goal.
10.Let us sacrifice our today so that our children can have a better tomorrow.
11.Small aim is a crime; have great aim.
12.Excellence is a continuous process and not an accident.
13.Thinking is the capital, Enterprise is the way, Hard Work is the solution.
14.You cannot change your future, but you can change your habits, and surely your habits will change your future.
15.Great dreams of great dreamers are always transcended.
16.Don't read success stories, you will only get a message. Read failure stories, you will get some ideas to get success.
17.We should not give up and we should not allow the problem to defeat us.
18.If four things are followed, having a great aim, acquiring knowledge, hard work, and perseverance, then anything can be achieved.
19.If you fail, never give up because FAIL means First Attempt In Learning.

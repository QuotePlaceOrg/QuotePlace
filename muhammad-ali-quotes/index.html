Muhammad Ali Quotes

Muhammad Ali (Boxer, 1942 – 2016). Below are 19 quotes by Muhammad Ali from QuotePlace, each as a quote card.

Boxer · 1942 – 2016

American heavyweight boxing champion and cultural icon known as much for his activism as his titles.

Quotes by Muhammad Ali

1.I hated every minute of training, but I said, 'Don't quit. Suffer now and live the rest of your life as a champion.'
2.He who is not courageous enough to take risks will accomplish nothing in life.
3.Float like a butterfly, sting like a bee.
4.Service to others is the rent you pay for your room here on earth.
5.To be able to give away riches is mandatory if you wish to possess them. This is the only way that you will be truly rich.
6.I am the greatest, I said that even before I knew I was.
7.The man who views the world at 50 the same as he did at 20 has wasted 30 years of his life.
8.The fight is won or lost far away from witnesses — behind the lines, in the gym, and out there on the road, long before I dance under those lights.
9.It isn't the mountains ahead to climb that wear you out; it's the pebble in your shoe.
10.The man who has no imagination has no wings.
11.I know where I'm going and I know the truth, and I don't have to be what you want me to be. I'm free to be what I want.
12.It's lack of faith that makes people afraid of meeting challenges, and I believed in myself.
13.I shook up the world!
14.I never thought of losing, but now that it's happened, the only thing is to do it right. That's my obligation to all the people who believe in me. We all have to take defeats in life.
15.Champions aren't made in gyms. Champions are made from something they have deep inside them — a desire, a dream, a vision.
16.To be a great champion you must believe you are the best. If you're not, pretend you are.
17.I don't count my sit-ups. I only start counting when it starts hurting because they're the only ones that count.
18.Inside of a ring or out, ain't nothing wrong with going down. It's staying down that's wrong.
19.It's not bragging if you can back it up.

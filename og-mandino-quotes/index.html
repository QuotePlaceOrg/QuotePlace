Og Mandino Quotes

Og Mandino (Author and sales mentor, 1923 – 1996). Below are 5 quotes by Og Mandino from QuotePlace, each as a text quote card.

Author and sales mentor · 1923 – 1996

Og Mandino was an American author and sales mentor best known for The Greatest Salesman in the World (1968), a bestselling parable of success principles told through scrolls.

Quotes by Og Mandino

1.Each misfortune you encounter will carry in it the seed of tomorrows good luck.
2.I will love the light for it shows me the way, yet I will endure the darkness because it shows me the stars.
3.I seek constantly to improve my manners and graces, for they are the sugar to which all are attracted.
4.Always do your best. What you plant now, you will harvest later.
5.Always seek out the seed of triumph in every adversity.

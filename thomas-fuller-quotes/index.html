English clergyman and aphorist · 1654 – 1734

Thomas Fuller was an English physician and clergyman best known for Gnomologia (1732), a vast collection of proverbs and aphorisms, many of them his own. His concise moral sayings remain a staple of quotation collections.

Quotes by Thomas Fuller

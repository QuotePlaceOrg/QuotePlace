Chuang Tzu (Chinese philosopher, 369 BC – 286 BC). Below are 2 quotes by Chuang Tzu from QuotePlace, each as a text quote card.

Chinese philosopher · 369 BC – 286 BC

Chuang Tzu (Zhuangzi) was an influential Chinese philosopher of the Warring States period and, with Laozi, a founder of Taoism. The book bearing his name is one of the foundational texts of Taoist thought.

Quotes by Chuang Tzu

1.When deeds and words are in accord, the whole world is transformed.
2.Flow with whatever is happening and let your mind be free. Stay centred by accepting whatever you are doing. This is the ultimate.

Wayne Dyer (American self-help author and speaker, 1940 – 2015). Below are 31 quotes by Wayne Dyer from QuotePlace, each as a text quote card.

American self-help author and speaker · 1940 – 2015

Wayne Dyer was an American self-help author and motivational speaker whose first book, Your Erroneous Zones (1976), became one of the best-selling books of all time. He wrote more than forty books and lectured widely on self-development and spirituality.

Virat Kohli (Cricketer, born 1988). Below are 4 quotes by Virat Kohli from QuotePlace, each as a text quote card.

Cricketer · born 1988

Indian batsman and former national captain, celebrated for his intense fitness, aggressive batting and record-breaking run chases in one-day internationals. He captained India to the 2008 Under-19 World Cup title as a teenager and became one of the most prolific run-scorers in modern cricket.

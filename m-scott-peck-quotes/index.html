Psychiatrist and author · 1936 – 2005

M. Scott Peck was an American psychiatrist whose book 'The Road Less Traveled' (1978) - opening with the line 'Life is difficult' - spent years on the bestseller lists and blended psychology with spirituality. He later wrote 'The Different Drum' and 'People of the Lie'.

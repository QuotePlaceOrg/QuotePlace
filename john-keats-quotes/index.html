John Keats (Poet, 1795 – 1821). Below are 11 quotes by John Keats from QuotePlace, each as a portrait card.

Poet · 1795 – 1821

English Romantic poet of the great Odes, including Ode to a Nightingale.

Quotes by John Keats

1.A thing of beauty is a joy for ever.
2.Beauty is truth, truth beauty, — that is all ye know on earth, and all ye need to know.
3.A thing of beauty is a joy forever.
4.Heard melodies are sweet, but those unheard are sweeter.
5.I am certain of nothing but the holiness of the Heart's affections and the truth of Imagination.
6.My heart aches, and a drowsy numbness pains my sense.
7.Give me books, French wine, fruit, fine weather and a little music out of doors, played by somebody I do not know.
8.Much have I travelled in the realms of gold.
9.The poetry of earth is never dead.
10.Do you not see how necessary a world of pains and troubles is to school an intelligence and make it a soul?
11.I almost wish we were butterflies and liv'd but three summer days — three such days with you I could fill with more delight than fifty common years could ever contain.

Novelist · 1871 – 1922

Marcel Proust was a French novelist whose seven-volume 'In Search of Lost Time' ('À la recherche du temps perdu'), published between 1913 and 1927, is regarded as one of the greatest works of modern literature, exploring memory, time and art.

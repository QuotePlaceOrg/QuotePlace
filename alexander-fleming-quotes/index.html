Bacteriologist who discovered penicillin · 1881 – 1955

Sir Alexander Fleming was a Scottish bacteriologist whose accidental discovery of penicillin in 1928, from a mould contaminating his culture plates at St Mary's Hospital, London, ushered in the antibiotic era. Developed into a drug by Florey and Chain, penicillin transformed medicine and saved hundreds of millions of lives. Fleming shared the 1945 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine for the discovery.

Quotes by Alexander Fleming

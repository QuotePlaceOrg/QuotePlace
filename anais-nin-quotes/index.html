Anais Nin Quotes

Anais Nin (Writer, 1903 – 1977). Below are 52 quotes by Anais Nin from QuotePlace, each as a quote card.

Writer · 1903 – 1977

Anaïs Nin, French-born American diarist and novelist. She is celebrated for her multi-volume Diary, begun when she was eleven, and for works such as Delta of Venus.

Quotes by Anais Nin

1.The possession of knowledge does not kill the sense of wonder and mystery. There is always more mystery.
2.Dreams pass into the reality of action. From the actions stems the dream again; and this interdependence produces the highest form of living.
3.The personal life deeply lived always expands into truths beyond itself.
4.Age does not protect you from love. But love, to some extent, protects you from age.
5.The dream was always running ahead of me. To catch up, to live for a moment in unison with it, that was the miracle.
6.There is not one big cosmic meaning for all, there is only the meaning we each give to our life.
7.Life shrinks or expands in proportion to one's courage.
8.We don't see things as they are, we see them as we are.
9.Dreams are necessary to life.
10.The only abnormality is the incapacity to love.
11.We write to taste life twice, in the moment and in retrospect.
12.Love never dies a natural death. It dies because we don't know how to replenish its source. It dies of blindness and errors and betrayals. It dies of illness and wounds; it dies of weariness, of witherings, of tarnishings.
13.Life is a process of becoming, a combination of states we have to go through. Where people fail is that they wish to elect a state and remain in it. This is a kind of death.
14.Each friend represents a world in us, a world possibly not born until they arrive, and it is only by this meeting that a new world is born.
15.You cannot save people, you can only love them.
16.I postpone death by living, by suffering, by error, by risking, by giving, by losing.
17.The role of a writer is not to say what we can all say, but what we are unable to say.
18.I, with a deeper instinct, choose a man who compels my strength, who makes enormous demands on me, who does not doubt my courage or my toughness, who does not believe me naïve or innocent, who has the courage to treat me like a woman.
19.The writer's responsibility is to increase, develop our senses, expand our vision, heighten our awareness and enrich our articulateness.
20.Ordinary life does not interest me. I seek only the high moments. I am in accord with the surrealists, searching for the marvelous.
21.People living deeply have no fear of death.
22.Throw your dreams into space like a kite, and you do not know what it will bring back, a new life, a new friend, a new love, a new country.
23.If you do not breathe through writing, if you do not cry out in writing, or sing in writing, then don't write, because our culture has no use for it.
24.Anxiety is love's greatest killer. It creates the failures. It makes others feel as you might when a drowning man holds on to you. You want to save him, but you know he will strangle you with his panic.
25.We travel, some of us forever, to seek other states, other lives, other souls.
26.How wrong it is for woman to expect the man to build the world she wants, rather than set out to create it herself.
27.I must be a mermaid, Rango. I have no fear of depths and a great fear of shallow living.
28.We do not grow absolutely, chronologically. We grow sometimes in one dimension, and not in another; unevenly. We grow partially. We are relative. We are mature in one realm, childish in another.
29.There are very few human beings who receive the truth, complete and staggering, by instant illumination. Most of them acquire it fragment by fragment, on a small scale, by successive developments, cellularly, like a laborious mosaic.
30.I am an excitable person who only understands life lyrically, musically, in whom feelings are much stronger than reason.
31.Why one writes I can answer easily, having so often asked it of myself. I believe one writes because one has to create a world in which one can live.
32.Writing to me means thinking, digging, pondering, creating, shattering.
33.What I cannot love, I overlook. Is that real friendship?
34.When we blindly adopt a religion, a political system, a literary dogma, we become automatons. We cease to grow.
35.The final lesson a writer learns is that everything can nourish the writer. The dictionary, a new word, a voyage, an encounter, a talk on the street, a book, a phrase learned.
36.Something is always born of excess: great art was born of great terrors, great loneliness, great inhibitions, instabilities, and it always balances them.
37.Our life is composed greatly from dreams, from the unconscious, and they must be brought into connection with action. They must be woven together.
38.I could be healed by the sheer courage of continuing to live. I could heal myself.
39.It is the function of art to renew our perception. What we are familiar with we cease to see.
40.There are two ways to reach me: by way of kisses or by way of the imagination. But there is a hierarchy: the kisses alone don't work.
41.There are many ways to be free. One of them is to transcend reality by imagination, as I try to do.
42.I watched life and wanted to be a part of it but found it painfully difficult.
43.Anything I can not transform into something marvelous, I let go. Reality doesn't impress me.
44.I only believe in intoxication, in ecstasy, and when ordinary life shackles me, I escape, one way or another. No more walls.
45.I hate men who are afraid of women's strength.
46.I will not adjust myself to the world. I am adjusted to myself.
47.For me, the adventures of the mind, each inflection of thought, each movement, nuance, growth, discovery, is a source of exhilaration.
48.Love is the axis and breath of my life. The art I produce is a byproduct, an excrescence of love, the song I sing, the joy which must explode, the overabundance — that is all!
49.The basis of insincerity is the idealized image we hold of ourselves and wish to impose on others.
50.The past, present, and future mingle and pull us backward, forward, or fix us in the present.
51.We are made of layers, cells, constellations.
52.When others asked the truth of me, I was convinced it was not the truth they wanted, but an illusion they could bear to live with.

Anais Nin Quotes

Anais Nin (Writer, 1903 – 1977). Below are 9 quotes by Anais Nin from QuotePlace, each as a text quote card.

Writer · 1903 – 1977

Anaïs Nin, French-born American diarist and novelist. She is celebrated for her multi-volume Diary, begun when she was eleven, and for works such as Delta of Venus.

Quotes by Anais Nin

1.The possession of knowledge does not kill the sense of wonder and mystery. There is always more mystery.
2.Dreams pass into the reality of action. From the actions stems the dream again; and this interdependence produces the highest form of living.
3.The personal life deeply lived always expands into truths beyond itself.
4.Age does not protect you from love. But love, to some extent, protects you from age.
5.The dream was always running ahead of me. To catch up, to live for a moment in unison with it, that was the miracle.
6.There is not one big cosmic meaning for all, there is only the meaning we each give to our life.
7.Life shrinks or expands in proportion to one's courage.
8.We don't see things as they are, we see them as we are.
9.Dreams are necessary to life.

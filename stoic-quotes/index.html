Stoic Quotes

The Stoics had a simple project: focus on what you control, release the rest. These 9 stoic quotes distill two thousand years of that clear-eyed philosophy into lines you can carry through a chaotic day. Ancient advice, surprisingly modern relief.

The impediment to action advances action. What stands in the way becomes the way.
Marcus Aurelius
The universe is change; our life is what our thoughts make it.
Marcus Aurelius
The best revenge is to be unlike him who performed the injury.
Marcus Aurelius
Dwell on the beauty of life.
Marcus Aurelius
If it is not right do not do it; if it is not true do not say it.
Marcus Aurelius
The soul becomes dyed with the color of its thoughts.
Marcus Aurelius
It is not that we have a short time to live, but that we waste a great deal of it.
Seneca
While we wait for life, life passes.
Seneca
Begin at once to live, and count each separate day as a separate life.
Seneca

Quotes by Faiz Ahmed Faiz

My love, do not ask of me the love I once had for you.
Speak, for your lips are free; speak, your tongue is still your own.
There are other sorrows in this world besides the sorrows of love.
Do not ask of me, my love, the love I once had for you.

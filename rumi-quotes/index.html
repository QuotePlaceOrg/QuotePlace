Rumi Quotes

Rumi (Poet and mystic, 1207 – 1273). Below are 17 quotes by Rumi from QuotePlace, each as a portrait quote card.

Poet and mystic · 1207 – 1273

13th-century Persian poet and Sufi mystic whose Masnavi is among the most influential works of Islamic literature.

Quotes by Rumi

The minute I heard my first love story, I started looking for you, not knowing how blind that was.
Raise your words, not your voice. It is rain that grows flowers, not thunder.
Don't grieve. Anything you lose comes round in another form.
Yesterday I was clever, so I wanted to change the world. Today I am wise, so I am changing myself.
Goodbyes are only for those who love with their eyes. Because for those who love with heart and soul there is no such thing as separation.
What you seek is seeking you.
Love is the bridge between you and everything.
Lovers don't finally meet somewhere. They're in each other all along.
Stop acting so small. You are the universe in ecstatic motion.
Be like a tree and let the dead leaves drop.
Don't be satisfied with stories, how things have gone with others. Unfold your own myth.
Where there is ruin, there is hope for a treasure.
Out beyond ideas of wrongdoing and rightdoing there is a field. I'll meet you there.
Let yourself be silently drawn by the strange pull of what you really love. It will not lead you astray.
Your task is not to seek for love, but merely to seek and find all the barriers within yourself that you have built against it.
Set your life on fire. Seek those who fan your flames.
Sell your cleverness and buy bewilderment.

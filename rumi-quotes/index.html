Rumi Quotes

Rumi (Poet and mystic, 1207 – 1273). Below are 30 quotes by Rumi from QuotePlace, each as a portrait card.

Poet and mystic · 1207 – 1273

13th-century Persian poet and Sufi mystic whose Masnavi is among the most influential works of Islamic literature.

Quotes by Rumi

1.The minute I heard my first love story, I started looking for you, not knowing how blind that was.
2.Raise your words, not your voice. It is rain that grows flowers, not thunder.
3.Don't grieve. Anything you lose comes round in another form.
4.Yesterday I was clever, so I wanted to change the world. Today I am wise, so I am changing myself.
5.Goodbyes are only for those who love with their eyes. Because for those who love with heart and soul there is no such thing as separation.
6.What you seek is seeking you.
7.Love is the bridge between you and everything.
8.Lovers don't finally meet somewhere. They're in each other all along.
9.Stop acting so small. You are the universe in ecstatic motion.
10.Be like a tree and let the dead leaves drop.
11.Don't be satisfied with stories, how things have gone with others. Unfold your own myth.
12.Where there is ruin, there is hope for a treasure.
13.Out beyond ideas of wrongdoing and rightdoing there is a field. I'll meet you there.
14.Let yourself be silently drawn by the strange pull of what you really love. It will not lead you astray.
15.Your task is not to seek for love, but merely to seek and find all the barriers within yourself that you have built against it.
16.Set your life on fire. Seek those who fan your flames.
17.Sell your cleverness and buy bewilderment.
18.Something opens our wings. Something makes boredom and hurt disappear. Someone fills the cup in front of us: We taste only sacredness.
19.Everyone has been made for some particular work, and the desire for that work has been put in every heart.
20.Let the beauty of what you love be what you do.
21.Let yourself be silently drawn by the stronger pull of what you really love.
22.The lion who breaks the enemy's ranks is a minor hero compared to the lion who overcomes himself.
23.Fortunate is he who does not carry envy as a companion.
24.Come, seek, for search is the foundation of fortune: every success depends upon focusing the heart.
25.This place is a dream. Only a sleeper considers it real. Then death comes like dawn, and you wake up laughing at what you thought was your grief.
26.When I am silent, I have thunder hidden inside.
27.If you are irritated by every rub, how will your mirror be polished?
28.Listen to the reed, how it tells a tale, complaining of separations.
29.How many flee from one calamity into another; how many leap from the snake towards the dragon.
30.Why do you stay in prison, when the door is so wide open?

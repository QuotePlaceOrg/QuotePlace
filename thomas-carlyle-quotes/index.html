Thomas Carlyle Quotes

Thomas Carlyle (Scottish historian and essayist, 1795 – 1881). Below are 6 quotes by Thomas Carlyle from QuotePlace, each as a text quote card.

Scottish historian and essayist · 1795 – 1881

Thomas Carlyle was a Scottish historian, philosopher and essayist whose works, including Sartor Resartus and On Heroes, Hero-Worship, and the Heroic in History, shaped Victorian thought. His history of the French Revolution is regarded as a classic of the genre.

Quotes by Thomas Carlyle

1.Silence is deep as Eternity, Speech is shallow as Time.
2.By nature man hates change; seldom will he quit his old home till it has actually fallen around his ears.
3.This world, after all our science and sciences, is still a miracle; wonderful, inscrutable, magical and more, to whosoever will think of it.
4.Do not be embarrassed by your mistakes. Nothing can teach us better than our understanding of them. This is one of the best ways of self-education.
5.Instead of saying that man is the creature of circumstance, it would be nearer the mark to say that man is the architect of circumstance.
6.Go as far as you can see; when you get there, you'll be able to see further.

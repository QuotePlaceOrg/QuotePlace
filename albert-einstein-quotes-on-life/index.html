Albert Einstein Quotes on Life

Albert Einstein on life: 10 quotes, pulled from the QuotePlace Life collection and gathered here. Read them together and a theme emerges that the full author page can bury. About the author: Albert Einstein (1879–1955) — Physicist.

I never think of the future. It comes soon enough.
Albert Einstein
Life is like riding a bicycle. To keep your balance you must keep moving.
Albert Einstein
I believe that a simple and unassuming manner of life is best for everyone, best both for the body and the mind.
Albert Einstein
If A is success in life, then A equals x plus y plus z. Work is x; y is play; and z is keeping your mouth shut.
Albert Einstein
Great ideas often receive violent opposition from mediocre minds.
Albert Einstein
There are only two ways to live your life. One is as though nothing is a miracle. The other is as though everything is a miracle.
Albert Einstein
The world is a dangerous place to live; not because of the people who are evil, but because of the people who don't do anything about it.
Albert Einstein
Look deep into nature, and then you will understand everything better.
Albert Einstein
A calm and modest life brings more happiness than the pursuit of success combined with constant restlessness.
Albert Einstein
Only a life lived for others is a life worthwhile.
Albert Einstein

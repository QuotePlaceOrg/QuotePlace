“Time is the substance I am made of. Time is a river which sweeps me along, but I am the river.”

— Jorge Luis Borges · Wisdom quote

Writer and poet · 1899 – 1986

Argentine short-story writer, essayist and poet whose labyrinthine tales in Ficciones and The Aleph reshaped modern literature. Director of Argentina's National Library, he explored time, infinity and invented worlds with unmatched precision.

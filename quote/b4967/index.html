“Man is the only animal that laughs and weeps; for he is the only animal that is struck with the difference between what things are, and what they ought to be.”

— William Hazlitt · Wisdom quote

English essayist and critic · 1778 – 1830

William Hazlitt was an English essayist, literary critic and philosopher regarded as one of the greatest essayists in the English language. His collections, including Table-Talk and The Spirit of the Age, profiled the writers and politics of the Romantic era.

“The hurricane does not roar in pentameters.”

— Kamau Brathwaite · Wisdom quote

Barbadian poet and historian · 1930 – 2020

Barbadian poet and historian whose idea of 'nation language' reshaped Caribbean poetry. He argued its rhythms should follow the hurricane and the calypso rather than the English pentameter, and he chronicled African retentions in Caribbean culture.

“Always bear in mind that the people are not fighting for ideas, for the things in anyone's head. They are fighting to win material benefits, to live better and in peace, to see their lives go forward, to guarantee the future of their children.”

— Amílcar Cabral · Courage quote

Guinea-Bissau independence leader · 1924 – 1973

Amílcar Cabral was an agronomist and revolutionary who led the liberation movement of Guinea-Bissau and Cape Verde against Portuguese colonial rule. He was assassinated in 1973, months before Guinea-Bissau declared independence.

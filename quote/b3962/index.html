“How very little can be done under the spirit of fear.”

— Florence Nightingale · Courage quote

Nurse and reformer · 1820 – 1910

Florence Nightingale was an English social reformer and the founder of modern nursing, famed for organising care of wounded soldiers during the Crimean War. A gifted statistician, she used data to drive reforms in hospitals, sanitation and public health.

“When your desires are strong enough you will appear to possess superhuman powers to achieve.”

— NAPOLEON HILL

Self-help author · 1883 – 1970

Napoleon Hill was an American self-help author best known for Think and Grow Rich (1937), one of the best-selling books of its kind. His work popularized the idea that success follows from definite purpose and a positive mental attitude.

“Never promise more than you can perform.”

— PUBLILIUS SYRUS

Latin writer of maxims · 85 BC – 43 BC

Publilius Syrus was a Latin writer of the 1st century BC who was brought to Italy as a slave from Syria and later freed. He is known for his Sententiae, a collection of moral maxims drawn from his mimes.

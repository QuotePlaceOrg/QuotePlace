“Empty your mind, be formless, shapeless — like water. You put water into a cup, it becomes the cup. Be water, my friend.”

— Bruce Lee · Wisdom quote

Martial artist and actor · 1940 – 1973

Bruce Lee was a Hong Kong-American martial artist and actor who transformed martial-arts cinema with films such as Enter the Dragon. His philosophy of adaptability — 'be water' — blended Taoist ideas with his own Jeet Kune Do.

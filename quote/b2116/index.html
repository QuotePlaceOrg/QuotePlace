“Never regret. If it's good, it's wonderful. If it's bad, it's experience.”

— VICTORIA HOLT

British novelist · 1906 – 1993

Victoria Holt was the best-known pen name of Eleanor Hibbert, a prolific British author of romantic suspense novels such as Mistress of Mellyn. Under her various pen names, including Jean Plaidy and Philippa Carr, she sold over a hundred million books.

“Great minds discuss ideas; average minds discuss events; small minds discuss people.”

— Eleanor Roosevelt · Wisdom quote

Diplomat and activist · 1884 – 1962

Eleanor Roosevelt was the longest-serving First Lady of the United States, from 1933 to 1945, and reinvented the role as a platform for civil rights and social reform. Afterward she became a US delegate to the United Nations and chaired the commission that drafted the Universal Declaration of Human Rights.

“It doesn't matter whether a cat is black or white, as long as it catches mice.”

— Deng Xiaoping · Wisdom quote

Statesman · 1904 – 1997

Deng Xiaoping was the Chinese leader who opened China to market reforms after 1978, lifting hundreds of millions out of poverty. His pragmatism is summed up in his saying that it does not matter whether a cat is black or white, as long as it catches mice.

American folk singer-songwriter · 1912 – 1967

Woody Guthrie was an American folk singer-songwriter whose songs about the Dust Bowl, migration and social justice, including 'This Land Is Your Land', shaped American folk music. He wrote hundreds of songs and influenced generations of musicians, including Bob Dylan.

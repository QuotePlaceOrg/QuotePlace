“I would rather drudge out my life on a cotton plantation, till the grave opened to give me rest, than to live with an unprincipled master and a jealous mistress.”

— Harriet Jacobs · Courage quote

American abolitionist and writer (1813–1897), author of Incidents in the Life of a Slave Girl (1861), the first full-length narrative by a Black woman published in the United States.

“Be regular and orderly in your life, so that you may be violent and original in your work.”

— Gustave Flaubert · Habits quote

Novelist · 1821 – 1886

Gustave Flaubert was a French novelist who pursued perfection of style with legendary discipline, rewriting pages until every word seemed inevitable. His Madame Bovary, prosecuted for obscenity in 1857, is a landmark of literary realism.

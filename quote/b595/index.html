“Friendship improves happiness and abates misery, by the doubling of our joy and the dividing of our grief.”

— Cicero · Friendship quote

Statesman and orator · 106 BC – 43 BC

Marcus Tullius Cicero was a Roman statesman, lawyer and orator considered the master of Latin prose. His speeches, letters and philosophical works, including On Friendship, transmitted Greek philosophy to the Roman world.

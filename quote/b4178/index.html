“Once a king or queen of Narnia, always a king or queen of Narnia.”

— C. S. Lewis · Life quote

Writer and scholar · 1898 – 1963

C. S. Lewis was an Irish-born scholar and writer at Oxford, author of The Chronicles of Narnia, Mere Christianity and The Screwtape Letters. A close friend of Tolkien, he moved from atheism to Christianity and became one of its most popular modern interpreters.

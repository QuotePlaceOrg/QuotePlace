“Science is the key to our future, and if you don't believe in science, then you're holding everybody back.”

— BILL NYE

Science educator and television presenter · born 1955

Bill Nye is an American science educator and mechanical engineer who hosted the award-winning children's show Bill Nye the Science Guy (1993-1998), which taught science to a generation of viewers. He later became CEO of the Planetary Society and a prominent voice on climate change. His Netflix series Bill Nye Saves the World (2017-2018) applied his signature style to adult audiences.

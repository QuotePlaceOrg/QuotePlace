“Don't give up, that's what life is: to continue the journey, to follow your dreams, to unknot time, to move the rubble and uncover the sky.”

— Mario Benedetti · Courage quote

Poet and novelist · 1920 – 2009

Uruguayan poet, novelist and journalist, author of The Truce and beloved poems like 'No te rindas' (Don't Give Up). Exiled during Uruguay's dictatorship, he wrote of ordinary people's loves, offices and quiet resilience.

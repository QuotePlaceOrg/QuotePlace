“The state is shattered, but mountains and rivers remain.”

— Du Fu · Life quote

Poet · 712 – 770

Du Fu is regarded as China's greatest poet, a Tang-dynasty writer whose work earned him the title 'Poet-Sage'. Writing through war and political collapse, he fused personal hardship with deep compassion for ordinary people.

“A priceless treasure is easy to find; a heart that truly loves is hard to find.”

— Bai Juyi · Love quote

Poet · 772 – 846

Bai Juyi was a Tang-dynasty Chinese poet famous for verse so plainspoken that, legend says, he read each poem to an old washerwoman and rewrote whatever she could not understand. His 'Song of Everlasting Regret' remains beloved across East Asia.

“Destroy the mosque, destroy the temple, destroy whatever you please — but do not break a human heart, for God dwells there.”

— Bulleh Shah · Love quote

Sufi poet · 1680 – 1757

Punjabi Sufi poet and humanist. His kafis challenge religious ritualism and caste, and are still widely sung today.

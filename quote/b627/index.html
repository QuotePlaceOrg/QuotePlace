“From where the sun now stands, I will fight no more forever.”

— Chief Joseph · Courage quote

Nez Perce leader · 1840 – 1904

Leader of the Wallowa band of the Nez Perce, famed for his people's 1877 fighting retreat toward Canada and his surrender words, 'I will fight no more forever.' He spent his later years pleading his people's cause in Washington.

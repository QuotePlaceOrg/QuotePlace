“He who angers you conquers you.”

— ELIZABETH KENNY

Australian nurse · 1880 – 1952

Sister Elizabeth Kenny was an Australian nurse who developed a controversial method of treating polio through physical therapy rather than immobilisation. Her clinics in Australia and the United States helped change polio care.

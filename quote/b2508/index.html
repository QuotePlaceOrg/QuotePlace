“Vision without power does bring moral elevation but cannot give a lasting culture.”

— ALLAMA IQBAL

Poet and philosopher · 1877 – 1938

Sir Muhammad Iqbal, known as Allama Iqbal, was a poet-philosopher of British India who wrote in Urdu and Persian. His poetry, including Shikwa and the philosophy of Khudi (selfhood), stirred a generation to self-respect and action. He is honoured as the national poet of Pakistan.

“Industrialize or perish.”

— M. VISVESVARAYA

Civil engineer and statesman · 1861 – 1962

Mokshagundam Visvesvaraya was an Indian civil engineer who designed the Krishna Raja Sagara dam and served as Diwan of Mysore from 1912 to 1918. A pioneer of planned industrialisation whose slogan was 'Industrialize or perish', he founded institutions including the Mysore Soap Factory and the State Bank of Mysore.

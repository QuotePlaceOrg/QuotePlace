“There is always another side, always.”

— Jean Rhys · Wisdom quote

Dominican-born novelist · 1890 – 1979

Novelist born in Dominica, best known for Wide Sargasso Sea, her haunting prequel to Jane Eyre told from a Caribbean Creole woman's perspective. Her spare prose gave voice to women living on the margins of empire.

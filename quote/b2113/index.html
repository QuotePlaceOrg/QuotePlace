“Work for something because it is good, not just because it stands a chance to succeed.”

— VACLAV HAVEL

Czech playwright and statesman · 1936 – 2011

Václav Havel was a Czech playwright, essayist and dissident who became the last President of Czechoslovakia and the first President of the Czech Republic after the Velvet Revolution. His plays and essays, including The Power of the Powerless, made him a symbol of resistance to communism.

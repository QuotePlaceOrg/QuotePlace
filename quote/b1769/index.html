“We must overcome the notion that we must be regular. It robs you of the chance to be extraordinary and leads you to the mediocre.”

— UTA HAGEN

German-American actress and acting teacher · 1919 – 2004

Uta Hagen was a German-American actress and influential acting teacher, author of the classic manuals Respect for Acting and A Challenge for the Actor. She originated the role of Martha in Edward Albee's Who's Afraid of Virginia Woolf? and co-founded the HB Studio in New York.

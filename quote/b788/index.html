“The future starts today, not tomorrow.”

— John Paul II · Action quote

Pope · 1920 – 2005

John Paul II, born Karol Wojtyła in Poland, was pope of the Roman Catholic Church from 1978 to 2005, one of the longest pontificates in history. A former actor and philosopher, his travels, writings and role in the fall of European communism made him a global moral figure.

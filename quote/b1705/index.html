“Yesterday is history. Tomorrow is a mystery. And today? Today is a gift. That is why we call it the present.”

— BABATUNDE OLATUNJI

Drummer & Educator · 1927 – 2003

Nigerian master drummer whose album Drums of Passion (1959) introduced African drumming to American audiences. He founded a centre for African culture in Harlem and taught drumming for decades.

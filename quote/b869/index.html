“Grow old along with me! The best is yet to be.”

— Robert Browning · Life quote

Poet · 1812 – 1889

Robert Browning was an English poet famed for his dramatic monologues, in which speakers reveal themselves in their own words. Married to the poet Elizabeth Barrett, he spent much of his life in Italy and published his masterpiece, The Ring and the Book, in 1868-69.

“It is easier to fight for one's principles than to live up to them.”

— Alfred Adler · Courage quote

Psychologist · 1870 – 1937

Austrian physician and psychotherapist who founded the school of individual psychology. He introduced influential concepts including the inferiority complex and the importance of birth order.

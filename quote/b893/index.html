“Nothing in life is to be feared, it is only to be understood. Now is the time to understand more, so that we may fear less.”

— Marie Curie · Courage quote

Physicist and chemist · 1867 – 1934

Marie Curie was a Polish-born physicist and chemist who pioneered the study of radioactivity, a term she coined. She remains the only person to win Nobel Prizes in two different sciences — Physics in 1903 and Chemistry in 1911 — and died of illness caused by her long exposure to radiation.

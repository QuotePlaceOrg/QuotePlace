“There is surely nothing other than the single purpose of the present moment.”

— Yamamoto Tsunetomo · Calm quote

Samurai and author · 1659 – 1719

Yamamoto Tsunetomo was a Japanese samurai of the Nabeshima clan whose spoken reflections were recorded as the Hagakure, a guide to the way of the warrior centered on devotion and living fully in the present moment.

“Every day is a journey, and the journey itself is home.”

— Matsuo Basho · Life quote

Poet · 1644 – 1694

Matsuo Basho was Japan's greatest haiku master, who raised the short comic verse of his day into a profound art. His travel diary The Narrow Road to the Deep North records a life spent walking Japan's roads in search of poetry.

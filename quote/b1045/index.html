“All serious daring starts from within.”

— HARRIET BEECHER STOWE

Novelist and abolitionist · 1811 – 1896

Harriet Beecher Stowe was an American author and abolitionist whose novel Uncle Tom's Cabin (1852) galvanized anti-slavery sentiment in the United States and abroad. She wrote more than 30 books, including novels, travel memoirs and collections of articles.

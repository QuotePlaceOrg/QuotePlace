“The poet is intimate with truth, while the scientist approaches it awkwardly.”

— JAGADISH CHANDRA BOSE

Physicist and biologist · 1858 – 1937

Jagadish Chandra Bose was an Indian physicist and plant physiologist who pioneered the investigation of radio and microwave optics and demonstrated that plants respond to stimuli. He founded the Bose Institute in Calcutta in 1917 and is considered a father of radio science.

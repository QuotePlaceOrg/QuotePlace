“Caste in India means an artificial chopping off of the population into fixed and definite units, each one prevented from fusing into another through the custom of endogamy.”

— B.R. Ambedkar · Life quote

Social reformer · 1891 – 1956

Indian jurist and social reformer who chaired the drafting of India's Constitution and campaigned against caste discrimination.

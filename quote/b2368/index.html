“The true way to render ourselves happy is to love our work and find in it our pleasure.”

— FRANCOISE DE MOTTEVILLE

French memoirist · 1621 – 1689

Françoise de Motteville was a French memoirist whose Memoirs of Anne of Austria offer a close account of the court of Louis XIII and the Fronde. She was a lady-in-waiting and confidante of Anne of Austria.

“Philosophy is written in that great book which ever lies before our eyes — I mean the universe — but we cannot understand it if we do not first learn the language and grasp the symbols, in which it is written.”

— Galileo Galilei · Wisdom quote

Italian astronomer and physicist · 1564 – 1642

Galileo Galilei was an Italian astronomer and physicist whose telescopic observations supported the heliocentric model of the solar system. His defence of Copernican astronomy brought him into conflict with the Catholic Church.

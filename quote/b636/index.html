“Knowledge is the beginning of practice; doing is the completion of knowledge.”

— Wang Yangming · Wisdom quote

Philosopher · 1472 – 1529

Wang Yangming was a Chinese Ming-dynasty philosopher, general, and calligrapher, founder of the School of Mind within Neo-Confucianism. He taught the unity of knowledge and action: to truly know something is to put it into practice.

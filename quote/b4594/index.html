“The good fighters of old first put themselves beyond the possibility of defeat, and then waited for an opportunity of defeating the enemy.”

— Sun Tzu · Wisdom quote

Military strategist · c. 544 BCE – c. 496 BCE

Ancient Chinese general traditionally credited with The Art of War, a treatise on strategy studied worldwide for over two thousand years.

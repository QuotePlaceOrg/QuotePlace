“I was a queen, and you took away my crown; a wife, and you killed my husband; a mother, and you deprived me of my children. My blood alone remains: take it, but do not make me suffer long.”

— MARIE ANTOINETTE

Queen of France · 1755 – 1793

The Austrian-born queen consort of Louis XVI, Marie Antoinette became a symbol of royal excess during the French Revolution, though the infamous 'let them eat cake' line was never actually hers. She was guillotined in Paris in October 1793, months after her husband.

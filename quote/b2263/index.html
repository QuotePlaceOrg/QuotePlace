“If only wed stop trying to be happy wed have a pretty good time.”

— EDITH WHARTON

American novelist · 1862 – 1937

Edith Wharton was an American novelist known for depicting the upper-class society of old New York in works such as The House of Mirth. She became the first woman to win the Pulitzer Prize for Fiction, for The Age of Innocence (1920).

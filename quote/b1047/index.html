— MURRAY GELL-MANN

Physicist · 1929 – 2019

Murray Gell-Mann was an American physicist who proposed the quark model of particle physics and won the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1969. He later co-founded the Santa Fe Institute to study complex systems.

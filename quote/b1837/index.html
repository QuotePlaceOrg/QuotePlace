“The wisest men follow their own direction.”

— EURIPIDES

Greek playwright · 480 BC – 406 BC

Euripides was an Athenian tragedian, one of the three great playwrights of classical Greek theatre alongside Aeschylus and Sophocles. His plays, including Medea and The Bacchae, explored psychology and questioned traditional myths.

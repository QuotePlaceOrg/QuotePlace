“What love can be purer and more noble than love of one's own country?”

— Andres Bonifacio · Love quote

Revolutionary leader · 1863 – 1897

Andres Bonifacio was a Filipino revolutionary who founded the Katipunan, the secret society that launched the Philippine Revolution against Spain in 1896. A warehouse clerk by trade, he was also a poet of fierce love for his homeland.

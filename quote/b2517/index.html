“You will never tire, you will never stop, you will never turn back — take this oath, take this oath, take this oath! Agneepath! Agneepath! Agneepath!”

— HARIVANSH RAI BACHCHAN

Hindi poet · 1907 – 2003

Harivansh Rai Bachchan was one of the most popular Hindi poets of the twentieth century, famed for Madhushala (1935) and the fiery poem Agneepath. He taught at Allahabad University and earned a doctorate from Cambridge on the poet W. B. Yeats. He was the father of actor Amitabh Bachchan.

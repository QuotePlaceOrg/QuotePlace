“Your sacred space is where you can find yourself again and again.”

— JOSEPH CAMPBELL

Mythologist and author · 1904 – 1987

Joseph Campbell was an American mythologist and professor whose 'The Hero with a Thousand Faces' (1949) described the hero's journey pattern found in myths worldwide. His televised conversations with Bill Moyers, 'The Power of Myth' (1988), brought his ideas to a wide audience.

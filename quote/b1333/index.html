English novelist and poet · 1840 – 1928

Thomas Hardy was an English novelist and poet whose works, including Tess of the d'Urbervilles, Far from the Madding Crowd and Jude the Obscure, are set in the fictional county of Wessex. His writing is marked by a pessimistic view of fate and Victorian society.

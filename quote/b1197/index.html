“Blaze with the fire that is never extinguished.”

— LUISA SIGEA

Humanist scholar and poet · 1522 – 1560

Luisa Sigea de Velasco was a Spanish-Portuguese Renaissance humanist and poet at the court of Lisbon, celebrated across Europe for her mastery of Latin, Greek, Hebrew and other languages. Her works include the Latin poem 'Syntra' (1546).

“He who can listen to the music in the midst of noise can achieve great things.”

— VIKRAM SARABHAI

Physicist and space pioneer · 1919 – 1971

Vikram Sarabhai was an Indian physicist and innovator who founded the Indian Space Research Organisation and the Physical Research Laboratory. Known as the father of the Indian space programme, he insisted that advanced technology be applied to the real problems of man and society.

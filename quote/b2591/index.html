“We cannot solve a crisis without treating it as a crisis.”

— GRETA THUNBERG

Climate activist · born 2003

Swedish climate activist Greta Thunberg began a solo school strike outside Sweden's parliament in August 2018, aged fifteen. Her Fridays for Future protest grew into a global youth movement, and she has addressed the United Nations and the Davos forum demanding urgent action on the climate crisis.

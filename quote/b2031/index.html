Austrian composer · 1756 – 1791

Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart was an Austrian composer of the Classical era, a child prodigy who composed more than six hundred works before his death at thirty-five. His operas, symphonies and concertos, including Don Giovanni and The Marriage of Figaro, are cornerstones of Western music.

“All shall be well, and all shall be well, and all manner of thing shall be well.”

— Julian of Norwich · Calm quote

Mystic and anchoress · born 1343

Julian of Norwich was an English anchoress who lived in a cell attached to a church in Norwich and wrote the earliest surviving book in English by a woman. Her Revelations of Divine Love, recording visions she received during a grave illness, is famed for its message of hope.

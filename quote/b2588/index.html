“Each person must live their life as a model for others.”

— ROSA PARKS

Civil rights activist · 1913 – 2005

A seamstress and NAACP activist in Montgomery, Alabama, Rosa Parks refused to surrender her bus seat to a white passenger on 1 December 1955. Her arrest sparked the 381-day Montgomery bus boycott and made her an enduring icon of the American civil rights movement.

“Intelligence is the ability to adapt to change.”

— STEPHEN HAWKING

Theoretical physicist and cosmologist · 1942 – 2018

Stephen Hawking was an English theoretical physicist and cosmologist who predicted that black holes emit radiation, now called Hawking radiation. Diagnosed with motor neurone disease at 21 and given a few years to live, he spent over five decades in a wheelchair communicating through a speech synthesizer. His book A Brief History of Time (1988) sold more than 10 million copies and made him the world's most famous scientist.

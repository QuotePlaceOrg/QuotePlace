“Give others confidence, give others joy, give others hope, give others convenience.”

— Hsing Yun · Friendship quote

Buddhist monk · 1927 – 2023

Hsing Yun was a Chinese Buddhist monk who founded the Fo Guang Shan monastery and the Buddha's Light International Association in Taiwan. He promoted 'Humanistic Buddhism' and his 'four givings': confidence, joy, hope, and convenience to others.

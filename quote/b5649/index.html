“It's an infernal nuisance to love Life as I do. I seem to love it more as time goes on rather than less. It never becomes a habit to me. It's always a marvel.”

— Katherine Mansfield · Life quote

Writer · 1888 – 1923

New Zealand modernist short-story writer, a leading figure of early 20th-century literature alongside Joyce and Woolf.

“Hope arouses, as nothing else can arouse, a passion for the possible.”

— WILLIAM SLOANE COFFIN

American clergyman and peace activist · 1924 – 2006

William Sloane Coffin Jr. was an American Christian clergyman and peace activist who served as chaplain of Yale University and senior minister of Riverside Church in New York. A former CIA officer, he became a leading voice of the civil rights and anti-war movements.

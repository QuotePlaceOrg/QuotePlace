“Sometimes life hits you in the head with a brick. Don't lose faith.”

— Steve Jobs · Courage quote

Co-founder of Apple · 1955 – 2011

Co-founder of Apple, Steve Jobs helped launch the personal computer era and later reinvented music, phones and tablets with the iPod, iPhone and iPad. Forced out of Apple in 1985, he founded NeXT and built Pixar into a major studio before returning in 1997 to rescue the company he had started.

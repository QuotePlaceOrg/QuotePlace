“Let us put our minds together and see what life we can make for our children.”

— Sitting Bull · Friendship quote

Hunkpapa Lakota leader · c. 1831 – 1890

Hunkpapa Lakota leader and holy man who united the Plains tribes in resistance to United States expansion and led them to victory at the Little Bighorn in 1876. He remains an enduring symbol of Indigenous sovereignty and dignity.

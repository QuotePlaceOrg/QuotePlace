“I know I have the body of a weak and feeble woman; but I have the heart and stomach of a king, and of a king of England too.”

— ELIZABETH I

Queen of England · 1533 – 1603

The last Tudor monarch, Elizabeth I reigned over England from 1558 to 1603, an era of flourishing arts, exploration and naval triumph. Never married, she famously declared herself wedded to her kingdom and defended it against the Spanish Armada in 1588.

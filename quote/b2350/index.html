“No one has a finer command of language than the person who keeps his mouth shut.”

— SAM RAYBURN

U.S. politician · 1882 – 1961

Sam Rayburn was an American politician from Texas who served as Speaker of the U.S. House of Representatives for 17 years, longer than anyone else. He was a mentor to Lyndon B. Johnson and a dominant figure in Congress for decades.

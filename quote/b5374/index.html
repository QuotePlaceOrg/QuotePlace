“To learn which questions are unanswerable, and not to answer them: this skill is most needful in times of stress and darkness.”

— Ursula K. Le Guin · Wisdom quote

Ursula K. Le Guin (1929–2018) was an American author of science fiction and fantasy, known for A Wizard of Earthsea, The Left Hand of Darkness, and The Dispossessed.

“Hope lies in dreams, in imagination, and in the courage of those who dare to make dreams into reality.”

— Jonas Salk · Courage quote

Physician and medical researcher · 1914 – 1995

Jonas Salk was an American physician and virologist who developed one of the first successful polio vaccines, announced in 1955. He later founded the Salk Institute for Biological Studies in California.

“The chains of habit are too weak to be felt until they are too strong to be broken.”

— Samuel Johnson · Habits quote

Writer and lexicographer · 1709 – 1784

Samuel Johnson was an English writer who compiled the first great Dictionary of the English Language in 1755. Poet, essayist and the most quoted conversationalist of his age, his life was immortalised in Boswell's famous biography.

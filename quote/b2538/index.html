“My name will be lost, this face will change; my voice alone is my identity.”

— GULZAR

Poet, lyricist and filmmaker · born 1934

Gulzar, born Sampooran Singh Kalra, is an Indian poet, lyricist and filmmaker writing in Urdu and Hindi. His film songs, from Anand to the Oscar-winning 'Jai Ho', and his three-line Triveni poetry made him a household name. He has received the Dadasaheb Phalke Award, the Sahitya Akademi Award and the Jnanpith.

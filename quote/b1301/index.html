Clergyman and writer · 1795 – 1855

Julius Charles Hare was an English cleric and writer, Archdeacon of Lewes, who co-authored 'Guesses at Truth' (1827) with his brother Augustus Hare and wrote widely on theology, literature and German philosophy.

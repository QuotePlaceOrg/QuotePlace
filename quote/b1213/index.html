— THOMAS DEWAR

Scottish whisky distiller · 1864 – 1930

Thomas Dewar, later Baron Dewar, was a Scottish businessman who, with his brother John, built Dewar's whisky into a global brand. A celebrated traveller and wit, he was famous for his aphorisms, known as 'Dewarisms'.

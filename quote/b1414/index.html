“Judge nothing, you will be happy. Forgive everything, you will be happier. Love everything, you will be happiest.”

— SRI CHINMOY

Indian spiritual teacher and writer · 1931 – 2007

Sri Chinmoy was an Indian spiritual teacher who moved to New York in 1964 and taught meditation in the West. He wrote prolifically on spirituality and was also known as an artist, musician, poet and athlete who founded the Sri Chinmoy Marathon Team.

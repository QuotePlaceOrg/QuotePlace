“Great is the art of beginning, but greater is the art of ending.”

— LAZARUS LONG

Fictional character

Lazarus Long is a fictional character created by science fiction author Robert A. Heinlein, appearing in novels including 'Methuselah's Children' and 'Time Enough for Love' (1973), where his collected maxims appear as 'The Notebooks of Lazarus Long'.

“A map is not the territory it represents, but, if correct, it has a similar structure to the territory, which accounts for its usefulness.”

— Alfred Korzybski · Wisdom quote

Philosopher & Founder of General Semantics · 1879 – 1950

Polish-American scholar who founded general semantics, presented in Science and Sanity (1933). He is famous for the maxim that the map is not the territory.

“What is necessary to change a person is to change his awareness of himself.”

— ABRAHAM MASLOW

Psychologist · 1908 – 1970

American psychologist who founded humanistic psychology. He is best known for his hierarchy of needs, set out in Motivation and Personality (1954), and for popularising the concept of self-actualisation.

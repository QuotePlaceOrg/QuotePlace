— SADHGURU

Yogi and founder of the Isha Foundation · born 1957

Sadhguru, born Jaggi Vasudev in Mysuru, India, is a yogi and mystic who founded the Isha Foundation in 1992, a non-profit offering yoga programs worldwide. His book Inner Engineering (2016) became an international bestseller. He is a frequent speaker at the United Nations, World Economic Forum and leading universities.

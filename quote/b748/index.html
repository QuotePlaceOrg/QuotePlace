“Since ancient times, who has not died? Let my loyal heart shine in the annals of history.”

— Wen Tianxiang · Courage quote

Poet and statesman · 1236 – 1283

Wen Tianxiang was a Song-dynasty scholar-official and poet who refused to serve the Mongol conquerors and was executed after years in prison. His 'Song of Righteousness' and his prison poems made him a lasting symbol of loyalty.

“Individually, we are one drop. Together, we are an ocean.”

— Ryunosuke Satoro · Friendship quote

Poet

Ryunosuke Satoro is a Japanese poet whose brief verses on togetherness and the natural world are quoted around the world. His best-known lines compare individuals to single drops of water and humanity to an ocean.

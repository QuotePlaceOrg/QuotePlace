“If you hate a person, you hate something in him that is part of yourself. What isn't part of ourselves doesn't disturb us.”

— Hermann Hesse · Wisdom quote

Novelist and poet · 1877 – 1962

Hermann Hesse was a German-Swiss novelist and poet whose works explore self-discovery and spirituality, including Siddhartha, Steppenwolf and The Glass Bead Game. He received the Nobel Prize in Literature in 1946.

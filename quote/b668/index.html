“The land belongs to those who work it with their hands.”

— Emiliano Zapata · Action quote

Revolutionary leader · 1879 – 1919

Mexican revolutionary and leader of the Liberation Army of the South, whose Plan of Ayala demanded land for the peasants who worked it. A central figure of the Mexican Revolution, his cry of land and liberty endures in Mexican memory.

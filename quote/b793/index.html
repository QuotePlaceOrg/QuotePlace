“In the beginner's mind there are many possibilities; in the expert's mind there are few.”

— Shunryu Suzuki · Wisdom quote

Zen master · 1904 – 1971

Shunryu Suzuki was a Japanese Soto Zen master who founded the San Francisco Zen Center and helped establish Zen in America. His book Zen Mind, Beginner's Mind is one of the most loved introductions to Zen practice.

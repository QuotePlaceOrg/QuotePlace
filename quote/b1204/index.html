Prime Minister of India · 1917 – 1984

Indira Gandhi was the first and only woman prime minister of India, serving 1966–1977 and again from 1980 until her assassination in 1984. The daughter of Jawaharlal Nehru, she led India through war, the Green Revolution and the controversial Emergency.

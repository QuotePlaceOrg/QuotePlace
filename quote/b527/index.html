“Failure is impossible.”

— Susan B. Anthony · Courage quote

Women's suffrage leader · 1820 – 1906

Susan B. Anthony spent more than fifty years campaigning for women's right to vote, co-founding the National Woman Suffrage Association with Elizabeth Cady Stanton. Arrested for voting in 1872, she died fourteen years before the Nineteenth Amendment finally secured women's suffrage.

“We are what we imagine. Our very existence consists in our imagination of ourselves.”

— N. Scott Momaday · Life quote

Kiowa novelist and poet · 1934 – 2024

Kiowa novelist, poet and essayist who won the Pulitzer Prize for House Made of Dawn in 1969, a landmark that launched the Native American Renaissance. His essays in The Man Made of Words explore language, story and identity.

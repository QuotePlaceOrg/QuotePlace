— SRI SRI RAVI SHANKAR

Spiritual leader and founder of the Art of Living Foundation · born 1956

Sri Sri Ravi Shankar is an Indian spiritual leader who founded the Art of Living Foundation in 1982, which teaches the Sudarshan Kriya breathing technique and stress-relief programs in more than 150 countries. He has been widely recognized for humanitarian and peace work, including mediation efforts in conflict zones. His teachings emphasize meditation, service and a stress-free, violence-free society.

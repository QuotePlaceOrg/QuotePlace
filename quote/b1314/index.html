— MARQUIS VAUVENARGUES

French moralist · 1715 – 1747

Luc de Clapiers, Marquis de Vauvenargues, was a French moralist and aphorist whose 'Réflexions et maximes' (1746) is a classic of French moral literature, published shortly before his early death at thirty-one.

— JOHN MUIR

Naturalist and conservationist · 1838 – 1914

John Muir was a Scottish-American naturalist, author and early advocate for the preservation of wilderness in the United States. He co-founded the Sierra Club, and his writings helped inspire the creation of national parks including Yosemite.

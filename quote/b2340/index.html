“If I am not for myself, who will be for me? If I am not for others, what am I? And if not now, when?”

— RABBI HILLEL

Jewish sage · 110 BC – 10

Hillel the Elder was a Jewish religious leader and sage of the 1st century BC who helped develop the Mishnah and Talmud. He is famous for summarizing the Torah as: 'What is hateful to you, do not do to your fellow.'

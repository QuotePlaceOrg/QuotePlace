“God does not need to be defended.”

— Abdurrahman Wahid · Wisdom quote

Religious leader and president · 1940 – 2009

Abdurrahman Wahid, known as Gus Dur, was an Indonesian Muslim scholar, leader of Nahdlatul Ulama, and Indonesia's fourth president. A defender of pluralism and minorities, he is remembered for the quip that God does not need to be defended.

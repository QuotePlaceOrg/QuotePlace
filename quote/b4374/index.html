“Everyone sees what you appear to be, few experience what you really are.”

— Niccolò Machiavelli · Wisdom quote

Political philosopher · 1469 – 1527

Niccolò Machiavelli was a Florentine diplomat and writer whose treatise The Prince analysed power with unsentimental realism. Long read as a manual of cunning statecraft, his works also include discourses defending republican government.

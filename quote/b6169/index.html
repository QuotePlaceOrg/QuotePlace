“The only difference between me and a madman is that I'm not mad.”

— Salvador Dalí · Wisdom quote

Artist · 1904 – 1989

Salvador Dalí was a Spanish Surrealist artist famous for dreamlike images such as the melting clocks of The Persistence of Memory. A brilliant self-publicist and technician, he worked in painting, film, sculpture and design from his base in Catalonia.

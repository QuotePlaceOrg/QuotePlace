— Emily Brontë · Love quote

Novelist and poet · 1818 – 1848

Emily Brontë was an English novelist and poet whose single novel, Wuthering Heights, is a strange and powerful story of love and revenge on the Yorkshire moors. Intensely private, she published under the name Ellis Bell and died at thirty.

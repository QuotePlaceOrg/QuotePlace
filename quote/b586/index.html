“Nations are born in the hearts of poets; they prosper and die in the hands of politicians.”

— Muhammad Iqbal · Wisdom quote

Poet and philosopher · 1877 – 1938

Philosopher, poet and politician of British India, writing in Persian and Urdu. His ideas inspired the Pakistan movement and his poetry is revered across South Asia.

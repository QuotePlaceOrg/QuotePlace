“I was the conductor of the Underground Railroad for eight years, and I can say what most conductors can't say — I never ran my train off the track and I never lost a passenger.”

— Harriet Tubman · Courage quote

Abolitionist · c. 1822 – 1913

Born into slavery in Maryland, Harriet Tubman escaped in 1849 and then made some thirteen missions back into the South to rescue around seventy enslaved people through the Underground Railroad. During the Civil War she served the Union Army as a scout, spy and nurse.

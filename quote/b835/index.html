“A dream you dream alone is only a dream. A dream you dream together is reality.”

— Yoko Ono · Love quote

Artist · born 1933

Yoko Ono is a Japanese artist, musician, and peace activist, a pioneer of conceptual art whose instruction pieces in Grapefruit invited audiences to complete the work in their imagination. She was married to John Lennon.

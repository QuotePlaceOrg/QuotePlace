American writer and publisher · 1856 – 1915

Elbert Hubbard was an American writer, publisher and philosopher who founded the Roycroft arts-and-crafts community. His essay "A Message to Garcia" (1899) was one of the most widely reprinted works of its era; he died in the sinking of the Lusitania.

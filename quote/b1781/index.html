— HENRI-FREDERIC AMIEL

Philosopher and poet · 1821 – 1881

Henri-Frédéric Amiel was a Swiss philosopher, poet and critic, and a professor at the University of Geneva. He is best known for his Journal Intime, a vast private diary published after his death.

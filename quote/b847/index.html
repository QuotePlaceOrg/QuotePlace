“I want our nation to be the most beautiful in the world — not the most powerful, but the most beautiful.”

— Kim Gu · Wisdom quote

Independence leader · 1876 – 1949

Kim Gu was a leader of the Korean independence movement and head of the Provisional Government of the Republic of Korea in exile. His memoir Baekbeom Ilji dreams of a Korea that is not the richest or strongest nation, but the most beautiful.

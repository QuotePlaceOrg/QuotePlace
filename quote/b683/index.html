“Remember the sky that you were born under, know each of the star's stories.”

— Joy Harjo · Life quote

Muscogee poet and musician · born 1951

Muscogee (Creek) poet, musician and author who served three terms as United States Poet Laureate, the first Native American to hold the post. Poems such as Remember call readers back to sky, earth and ancestry.

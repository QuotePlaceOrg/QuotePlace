— Johann Wolfgang von Goethe · Wisdom quote

Writer and polymath · 1749 – 1832

Johann Wolfgang von Goethe was a German writer, poet, playwright and natural philosopher, regarded as Germany's greatest literary figure. His works include the drama Faust, The Sorrows of Young Werther and Wilhelm Meister's Apprenticeship.

“There is no hurry. We shall get there some day.”

— A. A. Milne · Calm quote

Author · 1882 – 1956

A. A. Milne was an English author best known for the Winnie-the-Pooh stories, created for his son Christopher Robin and inspired by his stuffed animals. A successful playwright and humourist before Pooh, he also wrote the classic children's poems of When We Were Very Young.

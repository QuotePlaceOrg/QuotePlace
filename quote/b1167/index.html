“Pick battles big enough to matter, small enough to win.”

— JONATHAN KOZOL

Writer and educator · born 1936

Jonathan Kozol is an American writer and educator whose books expose inequality in public education, beginning with 'Death at an Early Age' (1967), drawn from his experience teaching in Boston schools. Later works include 'Savage Inequalities' (1991) and 'The Shame of the Nation' (2005).

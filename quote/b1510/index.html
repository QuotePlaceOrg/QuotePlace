American inspirational writer · 1921 – 1994

William Ward, usually credited as William Arthur Ward, was an American writer of inspirational maxims and short poems, famous for lines about gratitude, attitude and possibility. His epigrams appeared in newspapers and magazines for decades and are widely quoted in motivational literature.

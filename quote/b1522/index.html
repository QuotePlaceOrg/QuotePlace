“I can't imagine a person becoming a success who doesn't give this game of life everything hes got.”

— WALTER CRONKITE

American broadcast journalist · 1916 – 2009

Walter Cronkite was an American broadcast journalist who anchored the CBS Evening News for nineteen years and was often called 'the most trusted man in America'. He reported on events from the Second World War to the Moon landing, signing off with 'And that's the way it is.'

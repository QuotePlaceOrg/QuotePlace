“I hope the exit is joyful — and I hope never to return.”

— Frida Kahlo · Life quote

Painter · 1907 – 1954

Mexican painter famed for raw, symbolic self-portraits that turned a life of pain after a devastating bus accident into art of extraordinary power. Her diary and letters, like her paintings, are celebrated for their honesty and defiance.

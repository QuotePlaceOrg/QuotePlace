“Life is a learning experience, only if you learn.”

— YOGI BERRA

American baseball player and coach · 1925 – 2015

Yogi Berra was an American professional baseball catcher who played nineteen seasons with the New York Yankees, winning ten World Series championships. He was almost equally famous for his paradoxical, humorous sayings — 'Yogi-isms' such as 'It ain't over till it's over'.

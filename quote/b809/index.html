“Be patient toward all that is unsolved in your heart and try to love the questions themselves.”

— Rainer Maria Rilke · Calm quote

Poet · 1875 – 1926

Rainer Maria Rilke was an Austrian poet born in Prague, author of the Duino Elegies and the Sonnets to Orpheus. His Letters to a Young Poet, advising patience and solitude, have guided generations of artists; he wrote in German and later in French.

— ELLA WILCOX

American poet · 1850 – 1919

Ella Wheeler Wilcox was an American poet whose verse appeared in newspapers across the country. Her best-known lines, "Laugh, and the world laughs with you; weep, and you weep alone", come from the poem "Solitude" (1883).

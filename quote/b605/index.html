“Nothing is lost if one has the courage to proclaim that all is lost and we must begin again.”

— Julio Cortázar · Courage quote

Novelist and short-story writer · 1914 – 1984

Argentine novelist and master of the short story, author of the experimental novel Hopscotch (1963) and story collections such as Blow-Up. A central figure of the Latin American Boom, he blended fantasy, play and everyday life.

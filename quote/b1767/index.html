“The secret of joy in work is contained in one word — excellence. To know how to do something well is to enjoy it.”

— PEARL BUCK

Writer · 1892 – 1973

Pearl S. Buck was an American writer who spent much of her early life in China, where her parents were missionaries. Her novel The Good Earth won the Pulitzer Prize, and she received the Nobel Prize in Literature in 1938.

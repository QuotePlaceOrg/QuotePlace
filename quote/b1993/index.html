“Sometimes the most important thing in a whole day is the rest we take between two deep breaths.”

— ETTY HILLESUM

Dutch diarist · 1914 – 1943

Etty Hillesum was a Dutch Jewish diarist and letter-writer whose writings from Amsterdam and the Westerbork transit camp became a moving record of spiritual life under Nazi occupation. She was murdered at Auschwitz in 1943.

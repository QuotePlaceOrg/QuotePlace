“Authority without wisdom is like a heavy axe without an edge, fitter to bruise than polish.”

— Anne Bradstreet · Wisdom quote

Poet · 1612 – 1672

English-born colonial American poet and the first writer from Britain's North American colonies to be published. Her collection The Tenth Muse Lately Sprung Up in America appeared in 1650.

“Strong people make as many mistakes as weak people. Difference is that strong people admit their mistakes, laugh at them, learn from them. That is how they become strong.”

— RICHARD NEEDHAM

Humour columnist · 1912 – 1996

Richard J. Needham was a Canadian humour columnist for The Globe and Mail, known for his witty, curmudgeonly observations of everyday life. He won the Stephen Leacock Award for Humour.

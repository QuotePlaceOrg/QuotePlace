“Dare to know! Have the courage to use your own understanding.”

— Immanuel Kant · Courage quote

Philosopher · 1724 – 1804

Immanuel Kant was a German philosopher whose Critique of Pure Reason reshaped modern philosophy by examining the limits of human knowledge. His moral philosophy grounded duty in reason and human dignity, and his essays championed enlightenment and perpetual peace.

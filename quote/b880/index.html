“Idealism is the last luxury that only the young possess.”

— Tan Malaka · Life quote

Revolutionary and writer · 1897 – 1949

Tan Malaka was an Indonesian independence activist, philosopher, and writer who spent decades in exile agitating against Dutch colonial rule. His book Madilog argued for reason and materialism as tools of liberation.

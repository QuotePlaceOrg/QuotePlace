“The greatest part of our happiness depends on our dispositions, not our circumstances.”

— MARTHA WASHINGTON

First First Lady of the United States · 1731 – 1802

Martha Washington was the wife of George Washington and the first First Lady of the United States (1789-1797). She managed the Mount Vernon estate and, during the Revolutionary War, spent winters with the Continental Army supporting the troops.

“If liberty means anything at all, it means the right to tell people what they do not want to hear.”

— George Orwell · Courage quote

Novelist and essayist · 1903 – 1950

George Orwell (born Eric Arthur Blair) was an English novelist, essayist and critic. His dystopian novel Nineteen Eighty-Four and the allegory Animal Farm made terms like 'Big Brother' and 'thoughtcrime' part of the language.

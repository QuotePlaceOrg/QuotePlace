“Solitude is the profoundest fact of the human condition. Man is the only being who knows he is alone.”

— Octavio Paz · Wisdom quote

Poet and diplomat · 1914 – 1998

Mexican poet, essayist and diplomat, winner of the Nobel Prize in Literature (1990). His essay The Labyrinth of Solitude is a landmark meditation on Mexican identity, and his poetry bridges Mexican and European modernism.

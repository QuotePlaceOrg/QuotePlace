“Quality is never an accident; it is always the result of intelligent effort.”

— JOHN RUSKIN

Art critic and writer · 1819 – 1900

John Ruskin was the leading English art critic of the Victorian era, championing J.M.W. Turner and the Pre-Raphaelites in 'Modern Painters'. His later writings on society, work and education influenced reformers from William Morris to Gandhi.

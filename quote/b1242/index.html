Personal growth author · 1921 – 1995

Ken Keyes Jr. was an American personal-growth author best known for 'The Handbook to Higher Consciousness' (1973). Paralysed by polio as a young man, he founded a centre in Oregon teaching his 'Living Love' system for achieving happiness.

“Talent hits a target no one else can hit; genius hits a target no one else can see.”

— Arthur Schopenhauer · Success quote

Philosopher · 1788 – 1860

Arthur Schopenhauer was a German philosopher whose major work, The World as Will and Representation, portrayed life as driven by blind striving. Long neglected, his pessimistic yet lucid philosophy later influenced Tolstoy, Wagner, Nietzsche and many artists.

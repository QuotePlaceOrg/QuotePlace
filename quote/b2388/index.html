“You can stand tall without standing on someone. You can be a victor without having victims.”

— HARRIET WOODS

Politician · 1927 – 2007

Harriet Woods was an American politician who served as lieutenant governor of Missouri from 1985 to 1989, the first woman elected to statewide office there. She later led the National Women's Political Caucus.

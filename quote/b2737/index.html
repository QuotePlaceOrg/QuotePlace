Actress and humanitarian · 1929 – 1993

Audrey Hepburn was a British actress and humanitarian celebrated for films such as Roman Holiday, Breakfast at Tiffany's and My Fair Lady. In later life she devoted herself to UNICEF, serving as a Goodwill Ambassador in some of the world's poorest regions.

“I have ventured to send you the accompanying article for your perusal and opinion.”

— SATYENDRA NATH BOSE

Physicist · 1894 – 1974

Satyendra Nath Bose was an Indian physicist whose 1924 work on quantum statistics, developed with Albert Einstein, predicted a new state of matter, the Bose-Einstein condensate. The class of particles known as bosons is named after him.

“A leader is a dealer in hope.”

— Napoleon Bonaparte · Wisdom quote

French military leader and emperor · 1769 – 1821

Napoleon Bonaparte was a French military leader who rose to prominence during the French Revolution and became Emperor of the French in 1804. His Napoleonic Wars reshaped Europe, and his Napoleonic Code influenced legal systems worldwide. He died in exile on Saint Helena.

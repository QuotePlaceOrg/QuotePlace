Writer · 1766 – 1817

Germaine de Staël, known as Madame de Staël, was a Swiss-French writer and political thinker whose works, including 'Corinne' (1807) and 'De l'Allemagne' (1810), shaped European Romanticism. A prominent critic of Napoleon, she spent years in exile from France.

“Reading maketh a full man; conference a ready man; and writing an exact man.”

— Francis Bacon · Wisdom quote

Philosopher and statesman · 1561 – 1626

Francis Bacon was an English philosopher, statesman and essayist who championed the empirical method of inquiry. His Essays and his plan for the reform of learning in the Novum Organum helped lay the groundwork of modern science.

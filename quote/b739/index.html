“On the empty mountain no one is seen, but the sound of voices is heard.”

— Wang Wei · Calm quote

Poet · 699 – 759

Wang Wei was a Tang-dynasty Chinese poet, painter, and Buddhist known for serene landscape poetry. Poems such as 'Deer Enclosure' distill a whole mountainside into a few quiet lines.

“Life is what you make of it. Always has been, always will be.”

— GRANDMA MOSES

Folk artist · 1860 – 1961

Anna Mary Robertson Moses, known as Grandma Moses, was an American folk artist who began painting in earnest at the age of 78. Her scenes of rural American life became beloved worldwide, and she painted more than 1,500 canvases.

— JIM BISHOP

Journalist and author · 1907 – 1987

Jim Bishop was an American journalist and author whose narrative nonfiction, including The Day Lincoln Was Shot (1955), sold millions of copies. He also wrote a widely syndicated newspaper column for decades.

“Beauty is no more than the promise of happiness.”

— Stendhal · Happiness quote

Novelist · 1783 – 1842

Stendhal, the pen name of Marie-Henri Beyle, was a French novelist whose The Red and the Black and The Charterhouse of Parma pioneered psychological realism. Little appreciated in his lifetime, he predicted his fame would begin around 1880 — and was right.

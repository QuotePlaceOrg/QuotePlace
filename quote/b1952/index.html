“Trust your hunches. They're usually based on facts filed away just below the conscious level.”

— JOYCE BROTHERS

Psychologist and television personality · 1927 – 2013

Dr Joyce Brothers was an American psychologist who became a pioneering television and radio advice personality after winning the quiz show 'The $64,000 Question' in 1955. She wrote a widely syndicated newspaper column and several bestselling books on relationships and behaviour.

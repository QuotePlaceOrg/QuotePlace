“Life is a gorgeous robe, crawling with lice.”

— Eileen Chang · Life quote

Writer · 1920 – 1995

Eileen Chang was a Chinese writer celebrated for her psychologically sharp fiction of love and war in 1940s Shanghai and Hong Kong, including Lust, Caution and Love in a Fallen City. She later lived in the United States.

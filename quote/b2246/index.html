“It is with words as with sunbeams. The more they are condensed, the deeper they burn.”

— ROBERT SOUTHEY

Poet · 1774 – 1843

Robert Southey was an English poet of the Romantic school and Poet Laureate from 1813 until his death. He is remembered for poems such as 'After Blenheim' and for the first recorded version of 'The Story of the Three Bears'.

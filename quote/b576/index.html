“A word after a word after a word is power.”

— Margaret Atwood · Wisdom quote

Novelist and poet · born 1939

Margaret Atwood is a Canadian novelist, poet and essayist best known for The Handmaid's Tale, her dystopian novel that became a global cultural phenomenon. She has twice won the Booker Prize, for The Blind Assassin and The Testaments.

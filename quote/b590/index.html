“The master's tools will never dismantle the master's house.”

— Audre Lorde · Wisdom quote

Poet and essayist · 1934 – 1992

Audre Lorde, who described herself as a 'Black, lesbian, mother, warrior, poet,' was an American writer whose poetry and essays confronted racism, sexism and homophobia head-on. Her essay collection Sister Outsider and The Cancer Journals remain foundational texts of modern feminism.

“Life is a succession of moments. To live each one is to succeed.”

— CORITA KENT

American artist · 1918 – 1986

Corita Kent, born Frances Elizabeth Kent, was an American artist and educator who entered the Sisters of the Immaculate Heart of Mary. Her colourful silk-screen prints combined pop art with messages of love and social justice.

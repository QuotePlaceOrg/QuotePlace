“The thief left it behind — the moon at my window.”

— Ryokan · Calm quote

Zen monk and poet · 1758 – 1831

Ryokan was a Japanese Zen monk who lived as a hermit in a small hut, writing gentle poems and playing with village children. Offered charity all his life, he owned almost nothing and is remembered for his warmth and simplicity.

— OVID

Poet · 43 BC – 17

Ovid was a Roman poet celebrated for the Metamorphoses, a vast narrative poem of mythological transformations. Exiled by the emperor Augustus to the Black Sea coast, he spent his last decade writing poems of lament and endurance.

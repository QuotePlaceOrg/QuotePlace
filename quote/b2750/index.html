“There are things which seem incredible to most men who have not studied mathematics.”

— ARCHIMEDES

Mathematician and inventor · 287 BC – 212 BC

Archimedes of Syracuse was a Greek mathematician, physicist and inventor regarded as one of the greatest mathematicians of antiquity. He calculated an accurate approximation of pi, formulated the principle of buoyancy, and designed machines such as the screw pump and compound pulleys.

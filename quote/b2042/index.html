— JOHN LUBBOCK

Banker, politician and polymath · 1834 – 1913

John Lubbock, 1st Baron Avebury, was an English banker, politician, naturalist and author who as an MP introduced the Bank Holidays Act 1871. He wrote popular works on science and the conduct of life, including 'The Pleasures of Life' (1887).

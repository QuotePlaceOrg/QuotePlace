“The thoughts we choose to think are the tools we use to paint the canvas of our lives.”

— LOUISE HAY

Author and publisher · 1926 – 2017

Louise Hay was an American motivational author whose 'You Can Heal Your Life' (1984) became a foundational text of the self-help movement. In 1987 she founded Hay House, which grew into a major publisher of self-help and spiritual books.

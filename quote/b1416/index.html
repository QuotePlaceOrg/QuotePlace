“We are either progressing or retrograding all the while. There is no such thing as remaining stationary in this life.”

— JAMES FREEMAN CLARKE

Minister and author · 1810 – 1888

James Freeman Clarke was an American Unitarian minister, theologian and author, and a figure in the transcendentalist circle. He founded the Church of the Disciples in Boston and promoted religious tolerance.

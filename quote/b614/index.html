“Don't go outside your house to see flowers. My friend, don't bother with that excursion. Inside your body there are flowers.”

— Kabir · Happiness quote

Mystic poet · 1440 – 1518

Indian mystic poet and saint whose dohas challenged religious orthodoxy. He is revered by Hindus, Muslims and Sikhs alike, and his verses are included in the Sikh scriptures.

“We must teach science in the mother tongue. Otherwise, science will become a highbrow activity. It will not be an activity in which all people can participate.”

— C. V. RAMAN

Physicist · 1888 – 1970

Chandrasekhara Venkata Raman was an Indian physicist who won the 1930 Nobel Prize in Physics for discovering that light changes wavelength when it passes through a transparent material, the Raman effect. He founded the Indian Academy of Sciences and the Raman Research Institute in Bengaluru.

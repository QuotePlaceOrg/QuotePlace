“Nothing whatsoever should be clung to as 'I' or 'mine'.”

— Buddhadasa Bhikkhu · Wisdom quote

Buddhist monk · 1906 – 1993

Buddhadasa Bhikkhu was a Thai Buddhist monk and reformer who founded the Suan Mokkh forest monastery. He taught a rational, demythologized Buddhism centered on dependent origination and freedom from clinging to 'I' and 'mine'.

“The world hates change, yet it is the only thing that has brought progress.”

— Charles Kettering · Wisdom quote

Inventor & Engineer · 1876 – 1958

Charles F. Kettering, American inventor and head of research at General Motors for nearly three decades. He invented the electric self-starter for automobiles and founded the Delco electronics company.

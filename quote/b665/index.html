“There is nothing on this earth more to be prized than true friendship.”

— Thomas Aquinas · Friendship quote

Theologian and philosopher · 1225 – 1274

Thomas Aquinas was an Italian Dominican friar and the foremost scholastic theologian of the Middle Ages. His Summa Theologica attempted a complete rational account of Christian doctrine and remains central to Catholic philosophy.

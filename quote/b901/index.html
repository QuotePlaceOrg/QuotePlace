“I would rather have a government run like hell by Filipinos than a government run like heaven by Americans.”

— Manuel L. Quezon · Courage quote

President · 1878 – 1944

Manuel L. Quezon was the first president of the Commonwealth of the Philippines, remembered as the father of the national language. His blunt declaration that he would rather have a government run like hell by Filipinos than like heaven by Americans became famous.

“To establish a heart for Heaven and Earth, to secure a destiny for the people, to carry on the lost teachings of the sages, to open peace for all generations to come.”

— Zhang Zai · Wisdom quote

Philosopher · 1020 – 1077

Zhang Zai was a Chinese Neo-Confucian philosopher of the Song dynasty. His famous four 'Hengqu Sentences' — setting the heart at rest for Heaven and Earth and opening peace for generations to come — became a creed for Chinese scholars.

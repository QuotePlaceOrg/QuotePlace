“The good we secure for ourselves is precarious and uncertain, is floating in mid-air, until it is secured for all of us and incorporated into our common life.”

— Jane Addams · Life quote

Social reformer · 1860 – 1935

Jane Addams was an American settlement activist and reformer who co-founded Hull House in Chicago, a model for community social work. She became the first American woman to receive the Nobel Peace Prize, in 1931.

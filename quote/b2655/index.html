“Performance leads to recognition. Recognition leads to respect. Respect leads to power.”

— NARAYANA MURTHY

Co-founder of Infosys · born 1946

N. R. Narayana Murthy co-founded Infosys in 1981 with six colleagues and a small borrowed sum, and led it into a global IT services giant. A champion of corporate governance and what he calls compassionate capitalism, he is widely regarded as the father of India's IT sector.

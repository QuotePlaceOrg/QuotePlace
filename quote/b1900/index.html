“The truth which has made us free will in the end make us glad also.”

— FELIX ADLER

American philosopher and educator · 1851 – 1933

Felix Adler was a German-American educator and philosopher who founded the Ethical Culture movement in 1876, emphasising ethics and social reform outside organised religion. He became the first professor of political and social ethics at Columbia University.

“Living beings are not of the same nature; there are birds which fly high in the sky and fish which sink low in the water.”

— Kūkai · Wisdom quote

Monk and scholar · 774 – 835

Japanese Buddhist monk who founded the Shingon school and helped create the kana syllabaries used in Japanese writing.

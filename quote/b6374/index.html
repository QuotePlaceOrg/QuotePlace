“It isn't the thing you do, dear, it's the thing you leave undone, that gives you a bit of a heartache at the setting of the sun.”

— Margaret Sangster · Life quote

Poet, author and editor · 1838 – 1912

Margaret E. Sangster was an American poet, author and editor who edited Harper's Bazaar from 1889 to 1899 and wrote popular books and verses for young women, including 'Winsome Womanhood' (1900).

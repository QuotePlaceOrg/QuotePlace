“All perceiving is also thinking, all reasoning is also intuition, all observation is also invention.”

— RUDOLF ARNHEIM

Art theorist and psychologist · 1904 – 2007

Rudolf Arnheim was a German-American art theorist and psychologist of perception. His book Art and Visual Perception (1954) applied Gestalt psychology to the visual arts and influenced generations of artists and designers.

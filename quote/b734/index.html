“Knowledge is of no value unless you put it into practice.”

— Anton Chekhov · Action quote

Playwright and story writer · 1860 – 1904

Anton Chekhov was a Russian playwright and master of the short story, a practising doctor who wrote The Cherry Orchard, Uncle Vanya and hundreds of stories. His restrained, humane art reshaped both the theatre and short fiction of the 20th century.

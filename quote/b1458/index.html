— NIELS BOHR

Physicist · 1885 – 1962

Niels Bohr was a Danish physicist who made foundational contributions to understanding atomic structure and quantum theory, for which he received the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1922. He founded the Institute of Theoretical Physics in Copenhagen.

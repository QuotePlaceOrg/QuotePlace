“Six essential qualities that are the key to success: Sincerity, personal integrity, humility, courtesy, wisdom, charity.”

— WILLIAM MENNINGER

American psychiatrist · 1899 – 1966

William Menninger was an American psychiatrist who, with his father and brother Karl, built the Menninger Clinic in Topeka, Kansas, into a leading psychiatric centre. As the Army's chief of psychiatry during the Second World War, he transformed military mental-health care.

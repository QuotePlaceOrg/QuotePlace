“To dare is to lose ones footing momentarily. To not dare is to lose oneself.”

— SOREN KIERKEGAARD

Danish philosopher and theologian · 1813 – 1855

Søren Kierkegaard was a Danish philosopher, theologian and poet widely regarded as the first existentialist thinker. His works, including Fear and Trembling and Either/Or, explored faith, anxiety, despair and the individual's subjective relationship to truth.

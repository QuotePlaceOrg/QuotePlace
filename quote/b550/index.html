“If you have no confidence in self, you are twice defeated in the race of life. With confidence, you have won before you have started.”

— Marcus Garvey · Success quote

Jamaican Pan-African leader · 1887 – 1940

Jamaican publisher and Pan-African leader who founded the Universal Negro Improvement Association. His call for Black pride, self-reliance and African unity influenced liberation movements across the world.

“Compassion is not a relationship between the healer and the wounded. It's a relationship between equals.”

— Pema Chodron · Love quote

Buddhist nun and author · born 1936

Pema Chodron is an American nun in the Tibetan Buddhist tradition and a widely read author and teacher. Her books, including When Things Fall Apart, bring Buddhist teachings on compassion and difficulty to a broad audience.

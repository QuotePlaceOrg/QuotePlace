— ALFRED SHEINWOLD

Bridge Columnist & Author · 1912 – 1997

American bridge expert whose syndicated newspaper column ran for decades. He wrote the classic 5 Weeks to Winning Bridge (1949) and co-created the Kaplan-Sheinwold bidding system.

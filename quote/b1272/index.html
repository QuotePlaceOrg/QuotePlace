— MAN RAY

Artist and photographer · 1890 – 1976

Man Ray, born Emmanuel Radnitzky, was an American visual artist central to the Dada and Surrealist movements in Paris. He is known for his experimental 'rayograph' photographs and his portraits of leading artists and writers.

“Nobody made a greater mistake than he who did nothing because he could do only a little.”

— Edmund Burke · Action quote

Statesman and philosopher · 1729 – 1797

Edmund Burke was an Irish-born British statesman and political thinker, celebrated as a parliamentary orator. His Reflections on the Revolution in France made him a founding voice of modern conservatism, while he also supported American grievances and prosecuted abuses in India.

“Love, n. A temporary insanity curable by marriage or by removal of the patient from the influences under which he incurred the disorder.”

— Ambrose Bierce · Love quote

Writer & Satirist · 1842 – 1914

American short-story writer, satirist and Civil War veteran, author of An Occurrence at Owl Creek Bridge and The Devil's Dictionary (1906). He disappeared in Mexico in 1914 while observing the revolution.

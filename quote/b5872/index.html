“When you write, it's like braiding your hair. Taking a handful of coarse, unruly strands and attempting to bring them into unity.”

— Edwidge Danticat · Wisdom quote

Haitian-American writer · born 1969

Haitian-American novelist and memoirist, author of Breath, Eyes, Memory and the essay collection Create Dangerously. Her work bears witness to Haiti's history and to the lives of its diaspora.

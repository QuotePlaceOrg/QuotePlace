“Men are like the stars; some generate their own light while others reflect the brilliance they receive.”

— José Martí · Wisdom quote

Poet and independence leader · 1853 – 1895

Cuban poet, essayist and national hero who organised Cuba's independence movement and died fighting for it at Dos Ríos. His Versos sencillos (which gave the world 'Guantanamera') and essays shaped Latin American prose and thought.

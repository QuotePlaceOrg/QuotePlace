“Many things we need can wait. The child cannot. Now is the time his bones are formed, his mind developed. To him we cannot say tomorrow, his name is today.”

— Gabriela Mistral · Action quote

Poet and educator · 1889 – 1957

Chilean poet, educator and diplomat who in 1945 became the first Latin American to win the Nobel Prize in Literature. Her collections Desolación and Ternura sing of motherhood, children, nature and the rural poor she spent her life teaching.

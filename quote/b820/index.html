“And once the storm is over, you won't remember how you made it through, how you managed to survive. But one thing is certain: when you come out of the storm, you won't be the same person who walked in.”

— Haruki Murakami · Life quote

Novelist · born 1949

Haruki Murakami is a Japanese novelist whose surreal, melancholic books — Norwegian Wood, Kafka on the Shore, and 1Q84 among them — have been translated into more than fifty languages. He is also a devoted marathon runner.

Religious leader · 1910 – 2008

Gordon B. Hinckley served as the 15th president of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints from 1995 until his death. He oversaw extensive temple building and the church's global growth, and received the Presidential Medal of Freedom in 2004.

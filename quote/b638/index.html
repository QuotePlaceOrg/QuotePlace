“The most authentic thing about us is our capacity to create, to overcome, to endure, to transform, to love and to be greater than our suffering.”

— Ben Okri · Life quote

Nigerian poet and novelist · born 1959

Ben Okri is a Nigerian poet and novelist whose novel The Famished Road won the Booker Prize in 1991. His lyrical work blends everyday realism with the spirit world of West African storytelling.

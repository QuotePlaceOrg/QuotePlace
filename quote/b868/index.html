“Within the span of a hundred years, what a bitter struggle is waged between talent and fate.”

— Nguyen Du · Life quote

Poet · 1765 – 1820

Nguyen Du was a Vietnamese poet whose verse novel The Tale of Kieu is considered the greatest work of Vietnamese literature. Written in the nom script, it tells of a talented young woman sacrificed to fate and family duty.

“The only lies for which we are truly punished are those we tell ourselves.”

— V. Naipaul · Wisdom quote

Novelist and Nobel laureate · 1932 – 2018

V. S. Naipaul was a Trinidadian-British novelist known for works such as A House for Mr Biswas and In a Free State, which won the Booker Prize. He received the Nobel Prize in Literature in 2001 and was knighted in 1990.

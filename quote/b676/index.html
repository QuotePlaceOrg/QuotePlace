“But as long as you remember what you have seen, then nothing is gone. As long as you remember, it is part of this story we have together.”

— Leslie Marmon Silko · Life quote

Laguna Pueblo writer · born 1948

Laguna Pueblo novelist and storyteller whose Ceremony (1977) is a central work of Native American literature. Her writing weaves story, land, memory and healing into a single fabric, in the Pueblo oral tradition.

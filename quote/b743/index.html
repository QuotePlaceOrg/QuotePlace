— Alexandre Dumas · Friendship quote

Novelist · 1802 – 1870

Alexandre Dumas was a French novelist whose swashbuckling historical adventures, The Three Musketeers and The Count of Monte Cristo, have never been out of print. One of the most widely read French authors in the world, he wrote hundreds of plays, novels and travel books.

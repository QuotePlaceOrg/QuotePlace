“There are two ways to reach me: by way of kisses or by way of the imagination. But there is a hierarchy: the kisses alone don't work.”

— Anais Nin · Love quote

Writer · 1903 – 1977

Anaïs Nin, French-born American diarist and novelist. She is celebrated for her multi-volume Diary, begun when she was eleven, and for works such as Delta of Venus.

14th Dalai Lama · born 1935

Tenzin Gyatso is the 14th Dalai Lama, the spiritual leader of Tibetan Buddhism, recognised as the reincarnation of his predecessor at the age of two. He has lived in exile in India since 1959 and received the Nobel Peace Prize in 1989 for his nonviolent advocacy for Tibet.

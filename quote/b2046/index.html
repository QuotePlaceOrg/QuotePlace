“The most decisive actions of our life... are most often unconsidered actions.”

— ANDRÉ GIDE

Novelist · 1869 – 1951

André Gide was a French novelist and critic whose works, including The Immoralist and The Counterfeiters, explored freedom, desire and self-deception. He won the Nobel Prize in Literature in 1947 and influenced generations of European writers.

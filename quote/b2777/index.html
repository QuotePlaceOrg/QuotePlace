“The International Space Station is my happy place.”

— SUNITA WILLIAMS

Astronaut · born 1965

Sunita Williams is an American astronaut and retired US Navy officer of Indian-Slovenian heritage who has flown multiple long-duration missions to the International Space Station. She has commanded the ISS and set records for spacewalking by a woman.

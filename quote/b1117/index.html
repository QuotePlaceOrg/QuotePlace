“Time is the most valuable thing a man can spend.”

— THEOPHRASTUS

Greek philosopher and botanist · 371 BC – 287 BC

Theophrastus was a Greek philosopher who succeeded Aristotle as head of the Lyceum in Athens. Known as the father of botany for his works on plants, he also wrote Characters, a set of sketches of moral types that influenced later literature.

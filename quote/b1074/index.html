“Life is movement-we breathe, we eat, we walk, we move!”

— JOHN PIERRAKOS

Psychiatrist · 1921 – 2001

John C. Pierrakos was a Greek-American psychiatrist who co-founded Bioenergetic Analysis with Alexander Lowen and later founded Core Energetics, a body-centred approach to psychotherapy and personal growth. He authored 'Core Energetics' (1990) and 'Eros, Love and Sexuality'.

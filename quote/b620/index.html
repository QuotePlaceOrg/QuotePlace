“The first peace, which is the most important, is that which comes within the souls of people when they realize their relationship, their oneness, with the universe and all its powers.”

— Black Elk · Calm quote

Oglala Lakota holy man · 1863 – 1950

Oglala Lakota holy man whose great vision and life story, recorded in Black Elk Speaks, became one of the most widely read accounts of Native American spirituality. He witnessed the Little Bighorn and the Wounded Knee massacre.

“Strengthen the female mind by enlarging it, and there will be an end to blind obedience.”

— Mary Wollstonecraft · Action quote

Writer and women's rights advocate · 1759 – 1797

Mary Wollstonecraft was an English writer and advocate of women's rights, best known for A Vindication of the Rights of Woman (1792). She argued that women are not naturally inferior to men but appear so because of a lack of education. She was the mother of Frankenstein author Mary Shelley.

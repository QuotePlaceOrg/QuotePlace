Industrialist and philanthropist · 1835 – 1919

Scottish-American steel magnate Andrew Carnegie built Carnegie Steel into the largest steel empire of the nineteenth century. After selling his company in 1901, he devoted his fortune to philanthropy, funding some 2,500 public libraries and preaching his 'Gospel of Wealth'.

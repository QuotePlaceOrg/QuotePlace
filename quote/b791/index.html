“Peace is not an absence of war; it is a virtue, a state of mind, a disposition for benevolence, confidence, justice.”

— Baruch Spinoza · Calm quote

Philosopher · 1632 – 1677

Baruch Spinoza was a Dutch philosopher of Portuguese-Jewish descent who ground lenses for a living while writing works that reshaped philosophy. Expelled from his synagogue for his views, he argued in the Ethics for a rational, pantheistic understanding of God and nature.

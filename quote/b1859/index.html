“Where all think alike, no one thinks very much.”

— WALTER LIPPMANN

American journalist and commentator · 1889 – 1974

Walter Lippmann was an American writer, reporter and political commentator who won two Pulitzer Prizes. His book Public Opinion (1922) is a foundational work on media and democracy, and he helped popularise the term 'Cold War'.

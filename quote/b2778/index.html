“Saare Jahan Se Achha.”

— RAKESH SHARMA

Cosmonaut · born 1949

Rakesh Sharma is a former Indian Air Force pilot who, on 3 April 1984, became the first Indian citizen to travel to space, flying aboard the Soviet Soyuz T-11 to the Salyut 7 station. Asked by Prime Minister Indira Gandhi how India looked from space, he famously replied, 'Saare Jahan Se Achha.'

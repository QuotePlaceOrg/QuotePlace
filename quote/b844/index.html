“Until the day I die, I long to look up at the heavens without a speck of shame.”

— Yun Dong-ju · Life quote

Poet · 1917 – 1945

Yun Dong-ju was a Korean poet who wrote lyrical, conscience-stricken verse under Japanese colonial rule. Arrested for independence activities, he died in a Fukuoka prison at twenty-eight; his poem 'Foreword' is known to every Korean schoolchild.

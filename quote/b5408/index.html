“Not doing wrong action, sincerely doing every kind of good, naturally clarifies this mind. This is the Teaching of all the Buddhas.”

— Dogen · Wisdom quote

Zen master · 1200 – 1253

Founder of the Sōtō school of Zen in Japan and author of the Shōbōgenzō, a landmark of Buddhist philosophy.

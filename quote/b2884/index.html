“A foolish consistency is the hobgoblin of little minds.”

— Ralph Waldo Emerson · Wisdom quote

Essayist and philosopher · 1803 – 1882

Ralph Waldo Emerson was an American essayist and philosopher who led the Transcendentalist movement, arguing for self-reliance, intuition and the spiritual presence in nature. His essays 'Nature' and 'Self-Reliance' shaped American thought, and he mentored both Thoreau and Whitman.

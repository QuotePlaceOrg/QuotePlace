“You are never given a wish without also being given the power to make it true. You may have to work for it, however.”

— Richard Bach · Action quote

Writer · born 1936

Richard Bach is an American writer best known for the bestselling novella Jonathan Livingston Seagull (1970) and Illusions (1977). A former Air Force pilot, his work often combines flying with spiritual themes.

“A writer is someone for whom writing is more difficult than it is for other people.”

— Thomas Mann · Habits quote

Novelist · 1875 – 1955

Thomas Mann was a German novelist whose Buddenbrooks, The Magic Mountain and Death in Venice dissect European bourgeois culture with irony and depth. He won the Nobel Prize in Literature in 1929 and spent the Nazi years in exile, broadcasting against Hitler from America.

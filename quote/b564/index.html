“In overthrowing me, you have done no more than cut down the trunk of the tree of the black liberty in Saint-Domingue; it will spring back from the roots, for they are numerous and deep.”

— Toussaint Louverture · Courage quote

Haitian revolutionary leader · 1743 – 1803

Formerly enslaved leader of the Haitian Revolution who defeated French, Spanish and British forces and abolished slavery in Saint-Domingue. His generalship and statecraft laid the ground for Haiti, the first free Black republic.

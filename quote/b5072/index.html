“It's far better to buy a wonderful company at a fair price than a fair company at a wonderful price.”

— Warren Buffett · Wisdom quote

Investor, CEO of Berkshire Hathaway · born 1930

Investor Warren Buffett, the 'Oracle of Omaha', turned Berkshire Hathaway into one of the world's largest conglomerates through patient, disciplined value investing. Famous for his annual shareholder letters and frugal personal habits, he has pledged to give away the great majority of his fortune.

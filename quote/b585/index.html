“My mouth shall be the mouth of those calamities that have no mouth, my voice, the freedom of those who break down in the prison-cell of despair.”

— Aimé Césaire · Action quote

Martinican poet and statesman · 1913 – 2008

Martinican poet and politician who co-founded the Negritude movement with Leopold Senghor and Leon Damas. His Notebook of a Return to the Native Land is a landmark of anti-colonial poetry, written in a surge of surrealist imagery.

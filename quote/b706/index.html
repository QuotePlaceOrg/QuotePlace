“Marriage is like a besieged fortress: those who are outside want to get in, and those who are inside want to get out.”

— Qian Zhongshu · Life quote

Writer · 1910 – 1998

Qian Zhongshu was a Chinese scholar and novelist best known for Fortress Besieged (1947), a witty satire of intellectuals in wartime China and one of the great Chinese novels of the twentieth century.

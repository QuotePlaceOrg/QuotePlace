“Your heart and my heart are very, very old friends.”

— HAFIZ

Persian poet · 1315 – 1390

Khwaja Shams-ud-Din Muhammad, known as Hafiz of Shiraz, was a Persian lyric poet whose Divan is the most beloved poetry collection in Iran. His ghazals on love, wine and divine longing have been translated into many languages. His work later inspired Goethe's West-Eastern Divan.

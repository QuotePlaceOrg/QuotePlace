“No act of kindness, no matter how small, is ever wasted.”

— Aesop · Life quote

Fabulist · 620 BC – 564 BC

Aesop was a storyteller of ancient Greece, traditionally said to have been an enslaved man who won freedom through his wit. The animal fables credited to him, such as The Tortoise and the Hare, have taught moral lessons for over two millennia.

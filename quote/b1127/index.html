“Without faith, nothing is possible. With it, nothing is impossible.”

— MARY MCLEOD BETHUNE

Educator and civil rights leader · 1875 – 1955

Mary McLeod Bethune was an American educator and civil rights leader who founded a school for girls in Daytona Beach, Florida, that became Bethune-Cookman University. She advised President Franklin D. Roosevelt and founded the National Council of Negro Women in 1935.

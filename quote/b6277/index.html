“If God asked me what my wish was, I would reply unhesitatingly, "Korean independence." If he asked me what my second wish was, I would again answer, "My country's independence." If he asked me what my third wish was, I would reply in an even louder voice, "My wish is the complete independence of my country, Korea."”

— Kim Gu · Courage quote

Independence leader · 1876 – 1949

Kim Gu was a leader of the Korean independence movement and head of the Provisional Government of the Republic of Korea in exile. His memoir Baekbeom Ilji dreams of a Korea that is not the richest or strongest nation, but the most beautiful.

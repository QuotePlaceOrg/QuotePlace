“When you meet the one among the millions, when amid millions of years, across the borderless wastes of time, you happen to catch him or her, neither a step too early nor a step too late, what else is there to do except to ask softly: "So you're here, too?"”

— Eileen Chang · Love quote

Writer · 1920 – 1995

Eileen Chang was a Chinese writer celebrated for her psychologically sharp fiction of love and war in 1940s Shanghai and Hong Kong, including Lust, Caution and Love in a Fallen City. She later lived in the United States.

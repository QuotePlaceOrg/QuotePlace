“The revolution is not an apple that falls when it is ripe. You have to make it fall.”

— Che Guevara · Action quote

Revolutionary and writer · 1928 – 1967

Argentine Marxist revolutionary, physician and writer whose journeys across Latin America, recorded in The Motorcycle Diaries, shaped his convictions. A major figure of the Cuban Revolution, his essays and speeches remain widely read and debated.

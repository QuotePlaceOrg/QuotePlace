“Nature takes away any faculty that is not used.”

— WILLIAM R. INGE

English clergyman and author · 1860 – 1954

William Ralph Inge was an English Anglican priest, professor and author who served as Dean of St Paul's Cathedral in London. Nicknamed 'the Gloomy Dean' for his pessimistic newspaper columns, he wrote widely on mysticism and Plotinus.

“One isn't born one's self. One is born with a mass of expectations, a mass of other people's ideas — and you have to work through it all.”

— V. S. Naipaul · Life quote

Trinidadian novelist · 1932 – 2018

Trinidad-born novelist who won the Nobel Prize in Literature in 2001. Works such as A House for Mr Biswas and A Bend in the River examine colonial and postcolonial societies with unmatched precision.

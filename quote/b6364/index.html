“All that is not prose is verse; and all that is not verse is prose.”

— Molière · Wisdom quote

Playwright · 1622 – 1673

Molière, born Jean-Baptiste Poquelin, was a French playwright and actor considered the greatest master of comedy in French literature. Tartuffe, The Misanthrope and The Imaginary Invalid satirise hypocrisy and pretension with undiminished freshness.

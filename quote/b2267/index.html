Physician and poet · 1809 – 1894

Oliver Wendell Holmes Sr. was an American physician, poet and polymath, and one of the Fireside Poets. He coined the term 'anesthesia' and wrote the popular 'Breakfast-Table' series of essays and poems, including 'Old Ironsides'.

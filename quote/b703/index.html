— Jack Ma · Success quote

Entrepreneur · born 1964

Jack Ma is a Chinese entrepreneur who co-founded Alibaba, one of the world's largest e-commerce companies, after a string of early rejections including dozens of failed job applications. He is known for blunt, resilient talks about failure and persistence.

“I have no enemies, and no hatred.”

— Liu Xiaobo · Wisdom quote

Writer and activist · 1955 – 2017

Liu Xiaobo was a Chinese literary critic, writer, and human-rights activist who co-authored the Charter 08 manifesto. He was awarded the Nobel Peace Prize in 2010 while imprisoned, and his final statement was titled 'I Have No Enemies'.

“Students of the Way must not study Buddhism for the sake of themselves. They must study Buddhism only for the sake of Buddhism.”

— Dogen · Wisdom quote

Zen master · 1200 – 1253

Founder of the Sōtō school of Zen in Japan and author of the Shōbōgenzō, a landmark of Buddhist philosophy.

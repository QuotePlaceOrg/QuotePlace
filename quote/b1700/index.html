“There is one thing you have got to learn about our movement. Three people are better than no people.”

— FANNIE HAMER

American civil rights activist · 1917 – 1977

Fannie Lou Hamer was an American civil rights activist who co-founded the Mississippi Freedom Democratic Party and challenged the state's all-white delegation at the 1964 Democratic National Convention. Her testimony about being beaten for registering to vote drew national attention.

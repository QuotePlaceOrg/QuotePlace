“Time is the coin of your life. It is the only coin you have, and only you can determine how it will be spent. Be careful lest you let other people spend it for you.”

— Carl Sandburg · Life quote

Poet & Biographer · 1878 – 1967

American poet, journalist and historian. He won three Pulitzer Prizes, two for poetry collections such as Chicago Poems and one for his biography of Abraham Lincoln.

“Weakness and ignorance are not barriers to survival, but arrogance is.”

— Liu Cixin · Wisdom quote

Writer · born 1963

Liu Cixin is a Chinese science-fiction writer, author of the Remembrance of Earth's Past trilogy that begins with The Three-Body Problem. He is the first Asian writer to win the Hugo Award for Best Novel.

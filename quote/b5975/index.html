“It's clear that there is no peace without justice. There is no peace without truth.”

— Rigoberta Menchú · Wisdom quote

Indigenous rights activist · born 1959

K'iche' Maya activist from Guatemala who in 1992 became the first Indigenous person to receive the Nobel Peace Prize. Her testimony I, Rigoberta Menchú brought the struggles of Guatemala's Indigenous peoples to world attention.

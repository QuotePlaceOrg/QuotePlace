“Sow a thought and you reap an action; sow an act and you reap a habit; sow a habit and you reap a character; sow a character and you reap a destiny.”

— Charles Reade · Habits quote

Novelist · 1814 – 1884

Charles Reade was an English novelist and dramatist whose The Cloister and the Hearth is a richly researched tale of 15th-century Europe. A meticulous collector of facts and documents, he campaigned through his fiction against abuses in prisons and asylums.

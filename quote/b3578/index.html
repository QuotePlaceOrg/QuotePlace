“Ignorance more frequently begets confidence than does knowledge.”

— Charles Darwin · Wisdom quote

Naturalist · 1809 – 1882

Charles Darwin was an English naturalist whose theory of evolution by natural selection, set out in On the Origin of Species in 1859, revolutionised biology. He developed his ideas over two decades after his voyage aboard HMS Beagle around the world.

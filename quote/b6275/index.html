“Happiness resides not in possessions, and not in gold, happiness dwells in the soul.”

— Democritus · Happiness quote

Philosopher · 460 BC – 370 BC

Democritus was a Greek philosopher who, with Leucippus, developed the atomic theory of the universe. He held that reality consists of indivisible atoms moving in empty space, and was known in antiquity as the laughing philosopher.

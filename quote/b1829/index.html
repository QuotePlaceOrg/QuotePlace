“Real success is finding your lifework in the work that you love.”

— DAVID MCCULLOUGH

American historian · 1933 – 2022

David McCullough was an American historian and two-time Pulitzer Prize winner, for Truman (1992) and John Adams (2001). He was also known as the narrator of documentary films including Ken Burns's The Civil War.

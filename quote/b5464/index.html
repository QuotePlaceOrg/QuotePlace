“Faith is what we do with our doubts.”

— A. Powell Davies · Wisdom quote

Unitarian Minister & Author · 1902 – 1957

British-born American Unitarian clergyman who served as minister of All Souls Church in Washington, D.C. from 1944 until his death. A leading liberal preacher, he was known for defending civil liberties during the McCarthy era.

“Silence is the absolute poise or balance of body, mind, and spirit.”

— Charles Eastman · Calm quote

Dakota writer and physician · 1858 – 1939

Dakota (Santee Sioux) physician and writer, known by his Dakota name Ohiyesa. Books such as The Soul of the Indian interpreted Native spirituality, silence and values for a wide readership in the early twentieth century.

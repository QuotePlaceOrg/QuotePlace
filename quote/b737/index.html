“Let nothing disturb you, let nothing frighten you. All things pass away; God never changes.”

— Teresa of Ávila · Calm quote

Mystic and writer · 1515 – 1582

Teresa of Ávila was a Spanish Carmelite nun, mystic and reformer of the Carmelite order. Her spiritual classics The Interior Castle and The Way of Perfection, written with disarming directness, made her one of the most influential women writers of early modern Europe.

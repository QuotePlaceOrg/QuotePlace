“I failed on to the next thing, until I found something.”

— Jim Ryun · Courage quote

Olympic runner · born 1947

Jim Ryun is an American former athlete who in 1964 became the first high school student to run a mile in under four minutes. He won a silver medal in the 1500 metres at the 1968 Mexico City Olympics and later served as a US Representative from Kansas.

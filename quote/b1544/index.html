— MARGARET MEAD

Cultural anthropologist · 1901 – 1978

Margaret Mead was an American cultural anthropologist whose fieldwork in Samoa and New Guinea, reported in 'Coming of Age in Samoa' (1928), made her one of the best-known social scientists of the twentieth century.

“The world is round and the place which may seem like the end may also be the beginning.”

— IVY BAKER PRIEST

Treasurer of the United States · 1905 – 1975

Ivy Baker Priest was an American public official who served as Treasurer of the United States under President Eisenhower, and later as treasurer of California. Her signature appeared on U.S. currency throughout the 1950s.

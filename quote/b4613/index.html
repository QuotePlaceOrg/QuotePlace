“A great city, whose image dwells in the memory of man, is the type of some great idea. Rome represents conquest; Faith hovers over the towers of Jerusalem; and Athens embodies the pre-eminent quality of the antique world, Art.”

— Benjamin Disraeli · Wisdom quote

British Prime Minister & Novelist · 1804 – 1881

British statesman who served twice as Prime Minister and led the Conservative Party. He was also a successful novelist, writing books such as Coningsby and Sybil.

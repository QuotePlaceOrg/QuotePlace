“There is only one happiness in this life, to love and be loved.”

— George Sand · Happiness quote

Novelist · 1804 – 1876

George Sand, the pen name of Amandine Dupin, was a French novelist and journalist who scandalised and fascinated 19th-century Paris. Her novels of love and rural life, and her independent personal life, made her one of the most notable women writers of her century.

“In summer the nights. Not only when the moon shines, but on dark nights too, as the fireflies flit to and fro, and even when it rains, how beautiful it is!”

— Sei Shōnagon · Calm quote

Author

Heian-era court lady (c. 966–c. 1017) whose Pillow Book, a collection of observations and lists, is a masterpiece of Japanese literature.

Actress · 1924 – 2014

Lauren Bacall was an American actress known for her distinctive voice and roles in film noir classics including 'To Have and Have Not' (1944) and 'The Big Sleep' (1946), opposite Humphrey Bogart, whom she married. She later won two Tony Awards for her work on Broadway.

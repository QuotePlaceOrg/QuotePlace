Daisaku Ikeda Quotes

Daisaku Ikeda (Japanese Buddhist philosopher, 1928 – 2023). Below are 7 quotes by Daisaku Ikeda from QuotePlace, each as a text quote card.

Japanese Buddhist philosopher · 1928 – 2023

Daisaku Ikeda was a Japanese Buddhist philosopher, educator and peace activist who led the Soka Gakkai lay Buddhist movement and founded Soka University. He wrote extensively on Buddhism, dialogue and peace.

Quotes by Daisaku Ikeda

1.What matters is the value we've created in our lives, the people we've made happy and how much we've grown as people.
2.The person who lives life fully, glowing with life's energy, is the person who lives a successful life.
3.True happiness means forging a strong spirit that is undefeated, no matter how trying our circumstances.
4.Genuine sincerity opens people's hearts, while manipulation causes them to close.
5.If we look at the world with a love of life, the world will reveal its beauty to us.
6.If you lose today, win tomorrow. In this never-ending spirit of challenge is the heart of a victor.
7.With love and patience, nothing is impossible.

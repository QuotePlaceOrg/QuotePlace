About QuotePlace

What QuotePlace is

QuotePlace is a place to find quote images, save the ones you like, and share them with friends. Thousands of quotes, one tap to save, and a share button on every card.

The collection

The library holds more than 3,500 quotes from over 1,000 authors — philosophers, writers, leaders, poets and thinkers from every era and every part of the world — organised by author, by topic and by the words inside them. New quotes are added every day.

How we choose quotes

Every quote on QuotePlace is checked before it is published. We only use quotes that are genuinely attributed to their author — no invented quotes and no famous lines pinned on the wrong person. Author portraits come from free-licensed sources such as Wikimedia Commons, and image credits are kept with the work.

Who makes it

QuotePlace is run by a small independent team in India. We pick and upload quotes by hand and keep the site free of clutter.

Henry David Thoreau (Writer, naturalist, 1817 – 1862). Below are 7 quotes by Henry David Thoreau from QuotePlace, each as a portrait card.

Writer, naturalist · 1817 – 1862

American writer known for Walden (1854) and the essay Civil Disobedience.

Quotes by Henry David Thoreau

1.Go confidently in the direction of your dreams. Live the life you have imagined.
2.It's not what you look at that matters, it's what you see.
3.I cannot make my days longer so I strive to make them better.
4.The only way to tell the truth is to speak with kindness. Only the words of a loving man can be heard.
5.If one advances confidently in the direction of his dream, and endeavours to live the life which he had imagines, he will meet with a success unexpected in common hours.
6.Things do not change, we change.
7.The world is but a canvas to the imagination.

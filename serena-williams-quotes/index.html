Serena Williams Quotes

Serena Williams (Tennis champion, born 1981). Below are 4 quotes by Serena Williams from QuotePlace, each as a text quote card.

Tennis champion · born 1981

American tennis legend who won 23 Grand Slam singles titles, the most in the Open Era, along with four Olympic gold medals. Born in Saginaw, Michigan, and raised in Compton, California, she dominated women's tennis for over two decades and became a successful businesswoman and advocate for women.

Quotes by Serena Williams

1.The success of every woman should be the inspiration to another. We should raise each other up.
2.I am lucky that whatever fear I have inside me, my desire to win is always stronger.
3.I really think a champion is defined not by their wins but by how they can recover when they fall.
4.Luck has nothing to do with it, because I have spent many, many hours, countless hours, on the court working for my one moment in time, not knowing when it would come.

Mother Teresa Quotes

Mother Teresa (Catholic nun and missionary, 1910 – 1997). Below are 43 quotes by Mother Teresa from QuotePlace, each as a portrait card.

Catholic nun and missionary · 1910 – 1997

Albanian-Indian Catholic nun who founded the Missionaries of Charity in Kolkata; Nobel Peace Prize laureate (1979).

Quotes by Mother Teresa

1.If you judge people, you have no time to love them.
2.Every time you smile at someone, it is an action of love, a gift to that person, a beautiful thing.
3.Be faithful in small things because it is in them that your strength lies.
4.Let us always meet each other with smile, for the smile is the beginning of love.
5.We shall never know all the good that a simple smile can do.
6.If you can't feed a hundred people, then feed just one.
7.Peace begins with a smile.
8.Kind words can be short and easy to speak but their echoes are truly endless.
9.We can do no great things, only small things with great love.
10.Do not wait for leaders; do it alone, person to person.
11.Yesterday is gone. Tomorrow has not yet come. We have only today. Let us begin.
12.Be kind and merciful. Let no one ever come to you without leaving better and happier.
13.Spread love everywhere you go. Let no one ever come to you without leaving happier.
14.Not all of us can do great things. But we can do small things with great love.
15.I alone cannot change the world, but I can cast a stone across the waters to create many ripples.
16.The greatest disease in the West today is not TB or leprosy; it is being unwanted, unloved, and uncared for.
17.Let us always meet each other with a smile, for the smile is the beginning of love.
18.We ourselves feel that what we are doing is just a drop in the ocean. But the ocean would be less because of that missing drop.
19.If we have no peace, it is because we have forgotten that we belong to each other.
20.The hunger for love is much more difficult to remove than the hunger for bread.
21.Love begins by taking care of the closest ones, the ones at home.
22.Prayer is not asking. Prayer is putting oneself in the hands of God, at His disposition, and listening to His voice in the depth of our hearts.
23.Let us not be satisfied with just giving money. Money is not enough, money can be got, but they need your hearts to love them.
24.God doesn't require us to succeed, he only requires that you try.
25.I have found the paradox, that if you love until it hurts, there can be no more hurt, only more love.
26.What can you do to promote world peace? Go home and love your family.
27.I know God will not give me anything I can't handle. I just wish that He didn't trust me so much.
28.The most terrible poverty is loneliness and the feeling of being unloved.
29.The fruit of silence is prayer; the fruit of prayer is faith; the fruit of faith is love; the fruit of love is service; the fruit of service is peace.
30.Intense love does not measure, it just gives.
31.If you are humble nothing will touch you, neither praise nor disgrace, because you know what you are.
32.We need to find God, and he cannot be found in noise and restlessness. God is the friend of silence. See how nature — trees, flowers, grass — grows in silence; see the stars, the moon and the sun, how they move in silence. We need silence to be able to touch souls.
33.It is not how much we do, but how much love we put in the doing. It is not how much we give, but how much love we put in the giving.
34.The first requirement for prayer is silence. People of prayer are people of silence.
35.Love is a fruit in season at all times, and within reach of every hand.
36.We are called upon not to be successful, but to be faithful.
37.Be kind to each other in your homes. Be kind to those who surround you. I prefer that you make mistakes in kindness rather than that you work miracles in unkindness.
38.Joy is prayer; joy is strength; joy is love; joy is a net of love by which you can catch souls.
39.The miracle is not that we do this work, but that we are happy to do it.
40.We do not need guns and bombs to bring peace, we need love and compassion.
41.I believe that we are not real social workers. We may be doing social work in the eyes of the people, but we are really contemplatives in the heart of the world.
42.The poor are very great people. They can teach us so many beautiful things.
43.I want you to find the poor here, right in your own home first. And begin love there.

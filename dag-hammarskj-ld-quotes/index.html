Diplomat · 1905 – 1961

Dag Hammarskjöld was a Swedish diplomat who served as the second Secretary-General of the United Nations from 1953 until his death in a plane crash during a peace mission to the Congo. He was posthumously awarded the Nobel Peace Prize; his spiritual journal Markings was published after his death.

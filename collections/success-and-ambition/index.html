Success & Ambition

Success quotes are a genre of their own, usually written in hindsight by the winners. The ones gathered here earn their place: Churchill on never giving in, Napoleon Hill on desire as the starting point of achievement, and the quieter voices pointing out what success costs. Read the ambition; keep the receipt.

In order to win, you must expect to win.
Richard Bach
There is no way to prosperity, prosperity is the way.
Wayne Dyer
Not every difficult and dangerous thing is suitable for training, but only that which is conducive to success in achieving the object of our effort.
Epictetus
The scholar who cherishes the love of comfort is not fit to be deemed a scholar.
Confucius
Ideas are the beginning points of all fortunes.
Napoleon Hill
Be not afraid of greatness: some are born great, some achieve greatness, and some have greatness thrust upon them.
William Shakespeare
Come, seek, for search is the foundation of fortune: every success depends upon focusing the heart.
Rumi
Good luck is another name for tenacity of purpose.
Ralph Waldo Emerson
Your destiny isn't just fate; it is how you use your own developed abilities to get what you want.
Byron Pulsifer
With the realization of ones own potential and self-confidence in ones ability, one can build a better world.
Dalai Lama
Try not to become a man of success, but rather try to become a man of value.
Albert Einstein
A leader is best when people barely know he exists.
Lao Tzu
Difficulties increase the nearer we get to the goal.
Johann Wolfgang von Goethe
Always bear in mind that your own resolution to succeed is more important than any one thing.
Abraham Lincoln
All fixed set patterns are incapable of adaptability or pliability. The truth is outside of all fixed patterns.
Bruce Lee
Everything comes to us that belongs to us if we create the capacity to receive it.
Rabindranath Tagore
There are basically two types of people. People who accomplish things, and people who claim to have accomplished things. The first group is less crowded.
Mark Twain
The path to success is to take massive, determined action.
Tony Robbins
The least of things with a meaning is worth more in life than the greatest of things without it.
Carl Jung
All great achievements require time.
Maya Angelou
Strategy without tactics is the slowest route to victory. Tactics without strategy is the noise before defeat.
Sun Tzu
History will be kind to me for I intend to write it.
Winston Churchill
No pessimist ever discovered the secrets of the stars, or sailed to an uncharted land, or opened a new heaven to the human spirit.
Helen Keller
Take up one idea. Make that one idea your life.
Swami Vivekananda
All of us do not have equal talent. But, all of us have an equal opportunity to develop our talents.
A.P.J. Abdul Kalam
The future belongs to those who believe in the beauty of their dreams.
Eleanor Roosevelt
I don't believe in failure. It is not failure if you enjoyed the process.
Oprah Winfrey
The secret of success is constancy to purpose.
Benjamin Disraeli
He can who thinks he can, and he can't who thinks he can't. This is an inexorable, indisputable law.
Pablo Picasso
It always seems impossible until it's done.
Nelson Mandela
My reputation grows with every failure.
George Bernard Shaw
Success is not the key to happiness. Happiness is the key to success. If you love what you are doing, you will be successful.
Albert Schweitzer
Victory belongs to the most persevering.
Napoleon Bonaparte
If one advances confidently in the direction of his dream, and endeavours to live the life which he had imagines, he will meet with a success unexpected in common hours.
Henry David Thoreau
There is no failure except in no longer trying.
Elbert Hubbard
The person who lives life fully, glowing with life's energy, is the person who lives a successful life.
Daisaku Ikeda

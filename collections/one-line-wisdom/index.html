One-Line Wisdom

Some quotes need a paragraph. These need a breath. Every line in this collection is sixty characters or shorter — proof that compression is a form of intelligence. Screenshot them, memorise them, deploy them at dinner. Brevity, as Polonius failed to practise, is the soul of wit.

Study the past, if you would divine the future.

Argue for your limitations, and sure enough theyre yours.

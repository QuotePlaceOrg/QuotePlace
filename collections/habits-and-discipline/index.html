Habits & Discipline

Motivation gets you started; habit, as everyone on this page discovered, keeps you going. Aristotle's excellence-by-repetition, Seneca's daily accounting, and the modern masters of routine — thirty quotes on the unglamorous machinery behind every visible achievement.

Practice yourself, for heavens sake in little things, and then proceed to greater.
Epictetus
The superior man thinks always of virtue; the common man thinks of comfort.
Confucius
You leave old habits behind by starting out with the thought, 'I release the need for this in my life.'
Wayne Dyer
To keep the body in good health is a duty... otherwise we shall not be able to keep our mind strong and clear.
Gautama Buddha
A disciplined mind leads to happiness, and an undisciplined mind leads to suffering.
Dalai Lama
One ought, every day at least, to hear a little song, read a good poem, see a fine picture, and, if it were possible, to speak a few reasonable words.
Johann Wolfgang von Goethe
I am a slow walker, but I never walk back.
Abraham Lincoln
Absorb what is useful, discard what is useless, add what is uniquely your own.
Bruce Lee
The mind unlearns with difficulty what it has long learned.
Seneca
Moral excellence comes about as a result of habit. We become just by doing just acts, temperate by doing temperate acts, brave by doing brave acts.
Aristotle
Repetition is the mother of skill.
Tony Robbins
Your beliefs become your thoughts, your thoughts become your words, your words become your actions, your actions become your habits, your habits become your values, your values become your destiny.
Mahatma Gandhi
Do not seek pleasure for its own sake.
Miyamoto Musashi
I can resist everything except temptation.
Oscar Wilde
Take up one idea. Make that one idea your life — think of it, dream of it, live on that idea.
Swami Vivekananda
Don't take rest after your first victory because if you fail in second, more lips are waiting to say that your first victory was just luck.
A.P.J. Abdul Kalam
Be faithful in small things because it is in them that your strength lies.
Mother Teresa
Iron rusts from disuse; water loses its purity from stagnation; even so does inaction sap the vigor of the mind.
Leonardo da Vinci
An early-morning walk is a blessing for the whole day.
Henry David Thoreau
The first and greatest victory is to conquer yourself.
Plato
Diligence is the mother of good luck.
Benjamin Franklin
Dripping water hollows out stone, not through force but through persistence.
Ovid
I hated every minute of training, but I said, 'Don't quit. Suffer now and live the rest of your life as a champion.'
Muhammad Ali
I seek constantly to improve my manners and graces, for they are the sugar to which all are attracted.
Og Mandino
Persistence is very important. You should not give up unless you are forced to give up.
Elon Musk
Custom is the great guide of human life.
David Hume
Either you run the day or the day runs you.
Jim Rohn
Quality means doing it right when no one is looking.
Henry Ford
Luck has nothing to do with it, because I have spent many, many hours, countless hours, on the court working for my one moment in time, not knowing when it would come.
Serena Williams
I have always believed that process is more important than results.
M. S. Dhoni

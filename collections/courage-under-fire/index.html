Courage Under Fire

Courage, in these quotes, is rarely loud. It is Mandela walking unbowed out of prison, Seneca noting that sometimes just living is an act of it, and Mark Twain defining it as mastery of fear rather than its absence. Thirty-six lines for the days that require more than motivation.

The meaning I picked, the one that changed my life: Overcome fear, behold wonder.
Richard Bach
To see what is right and not to do it is want of courage.
Confucius
The greater the difficulty, the more glory in surmounting it. Skillful pilots gain their reputation from storms and tempests.
Epictetus
Fears are nothing more than a state of mind.
Napoleon Hill
All the world is a stage, And all the men and women merely players.They have their exits and entrances; Each man in his time plays many parts.
William Shakespeare
The lion who breaks the enemy's ranks is a minor hero compared to the lion who overcomes himself.
Rumi
What you fear is that which requires action to overcome.
Byron Pulsifer
A hero is no braver than an ordinary man, but he is braver five minutes longer.
Ralph Waldo Emerson
He who conquers himself is the mightiest warrior.
Lao Tzu
Let men see, let them know, a real man, who lives as he was meant to live.
Marcus Aurelius
Few people are capable of expressing with equanimity opinions that differ from the prejudices of their social environment.
Albert Einstein
Compassion and tolerance are not a sign of weakness, but a sign of strength.
Dalai Lama
Daring ideas are like chessmen moved forward. They may be beaten, but they may start a winning game.
Johann Wolfgang von Goethe
Be sure you put your feet in the right place, then stand firm.
Abraham Lincoln
Defeat is a state of mind; no one is ever defeated until defeat has been accepted as a reality.
Bruce Lee
Let me not pray to be sheltered from dangers, but to be fearless in facing them. Let me not beg for the stilling of my pain, but for the heart to conquer it.
Rabindranath Tagore
Our lack of confidence is not the result of difficulty. The difficulty comes from our lack of confidence.
Seneca
We make war that we may live in peace.
Aristotle
The fear of death follows from the fear of life. A man who lives fully is prepared to die at any time.
Mark Twain
One looks back with appreciation to the brilliant teachers, but with gratitude to those who touched our human feelings.
Carl Jung
Courage is the most important of all the virtues because without courage, you can't practice any other virtue consistently.
Maya Angelou
In a gentle way, you can shake the world.
Mahatma Gandhi
You have enemies? Good. That means you've stood up for something, sometime in your life.
Winston Churchill
Never bend your head. Always hold it high. Look the world right in the eye.
Helen Keller
You cannot believe in God until you believe in yourself.
Swami Vivekananda
No one can make you feel inferior without your consent.
Eleanor Roosevelt
The biggest adventure you can take is to live the life of your dreams.
Oprah Winfrey
Never apologize for showing feeling. When you do so, you apologize for truth.

I never lose. I either win or learn.

Friedrich Nietzsche

John Lennon

Napoleon Bonaparte

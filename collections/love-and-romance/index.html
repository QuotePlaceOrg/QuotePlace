Love & Romance

Not the greeting-card version. The love quotes that survive on this page come from people who thought hard about what love actually is — Rumi's longing, Gibran's restraint, Shakespeare's madness, Austen's tenderness of heart. Forty of the collection's finest on the subject that has generated more sentences than any other.

Real magic in relationships means an absence of judgement of others.
Wayne Dyer
If you love someone, set them free. If they come back they're yours; if they don't they never were.
Richard Bach
The minute I heard my first love story, I started looking for you, not knowing how blind that was.
Rumi
Speak low, if you speak love.
William Shakespeare
Someone is special only if you tell them.
Byron Pulsifer
Love and compassion open our own inner life, reducing stress, distrust and loneliness.
Dalai Lama
Kindness in words creates confidence. Kindness in thinking creates profoundness. Kindness in giving creates love.
Lao Tzu
The most precious gift we can offer anyone is our attention. When mindfulness embraces those we love, they will bloom like flowers.
Thich Nhat Hanh
We are shaped and fashioned by what we love.
Johann Wolfgang von Goethe
Let there be spaces in your togetherness, and let the winds of the heavens dance between you.
Kahlil Gibran
With malice toward none, with charity for all.
Abraham Lincoln
Real living is living for others.
Bruce Lee
Love's gift cannot be given, it waits to be accepted.
Rabindranath Tagore
Kindness is the language which the deaf can hear and the blind can see.
Mark Twain
The quality of your life is the quality of your relationships.
Tony Robbins
Where love rules, there is no will to power, and where power predominates, love is lacking.
Carl Jung
If you have only one smile in you, give it to the people you love.
Maya Angelou
The best way to find yourself is to lose yourself in the service of others.
Mahatma Gandhi
Anybody can make history. Only a great man can write it.
Oscar Wilde
What we have once enjoyed we can never lose. All that we love deeply becomes a part of us.
Helen Keller
They alone live who live for others.
Swami Vivekananda
The heart has its reasons which reason knows not of.
Blaise Pascal
Every time you smile at someone, it is an action of love, a gift to that person, a beautiful thing.
Mother Teresa
No one is born hating another person because of the color of his skin, or his background, or his religion.
Nelson Mandela
Love is the flower you've got to let grow.
John Lennon
Life is the flower for which love is the honey.
Victor Hugo
It is not a lack of love, but a lack of friendship that makes unhappy marriages.
Friedrich Nietzsche
In the midst of hate, I found there was, within me, an invincible love.
Albert Camus
Children are the anchors that hold a mother to life.
Sophocles
The purpose of human life is to serve, and to show compassion and the will to help others.
Albert Schweitzer
A loving heart is the truest wisdom.
Charles Dickens
Love is the only force capable of transforming an enemy into friend.
Martin Luther King Jr.
The moment you have in your heart this extraordinary thing called love and feel the depth, the delight, the ecstasy of it, you will discover that for you the world is transformed.
Jiddu Krishnamurti
Age does not protect you from love. But love, to some extent, protects you from age.
Anais Nin
All, everything that I understand, I understand only because I love.
Leo Tolstoy
With love and patience, nothing is impossible.
Daisaku Ikeda
There is no charm equal to tenderness of heart.
Jane Austen

Alfred Tennyson

Faiz Ahmed Faiz

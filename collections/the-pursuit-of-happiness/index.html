The Pursuit of Happiness

Happiness has been defined by emperors, monks, physicists and poets, and they mostly agree it is not what the adverts say. The Buddha locates it in a trained mind; others here find it in gratitude, in work, in love, in low expectations wisely kept. Thirty-six attempts at the oldest question there is.

The best way to pay for a lovely moment is to enjoy it.
Richard Bach
There is only one way to happiness and that is to cease worrying about things which are beyond the power of our will.
Epictetus
Doing what you love is the cornerstone of having abundance in your life.
Wayne Dyer
To learn and at due times to repeat what one has learnt, is that not after all a pleasure?
Confucius
There is no path to happiness: happiness is the path.
Gautama Buddha
Happiness is found in doing, not merely possessing.
Napoleon Hill
What you seek is seeking you.
Rumi
He who is contented is rich.
Lao Tzu
If you think you are too small to make a difference, try sleeping with a mosquito.
Dalai Lama
Today, give a stranger a smile without waiting for it may be the joy they need to have a great day.
Byron Pulsifer
Happiness is a perfume you cannot pour on others without getting a few drops on yourself.
Ralph Waldo Emerson
A happy man is too satisfied with the present to dwell too much on the future.
Albert Einstein
The happiness of your life depends upon the quality of your thoughts.
Marcus Aurelius
Great talent finds happiness in execution.
Johann Wolfgang von Goethe
There is no way to happiness, happiness is the way.
Thich Nhat Hanh
Your living is determined not so much by what life brings to you as by the attitude you bring to life.
Kahlil Gibran
Most people are about as happy as they make up their minds to be
Abraham Lincoln
I slept and dreamt that life was joy. I awoke and saw that life was service. I acted and behold, service was joy.
Rabindranath Tagore
The greatest blessing of mankind is within us and within our reach. A wise man is content with his lot, whatever it may be, without wishing for what he has not.
Seneca
Happiness depends upon ourselves.
Aristotle

Keep yourself to the sunshine and you cannot see the shadow.

The more you praise and celebrate your life, the more there is in life to celebrate.

Happiness is not a goal; it is a by-product.
Eleanor Roosevelt

Pema Chodron

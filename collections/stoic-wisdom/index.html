Stoic Wisdom

Two thousand years ago, a Roman emperor writing in a war tent, a former slave teaching in exile, and a senator advising an emperor all arrived at the same operating system for life: control what you can, accept what you can't, and waste no time on the difference. These forty quotes from Marcus Aurelius, Seneca and Epictetus are that philosophy at full strength — no commentary needed.

Freedom is the right to live as we wish.
Epictetus
Each day provides its own gifts.
Marcus Aurelius
The greatest remedy for anger is delay.
Seneca
Difficulties are things that show a person what they are.
Epictetus
Loss is nothing else but change,and change is Natures delight.
Marcus Aurelius
The mind unlearns with difficulty what it has long learned.
Seneca
If you wish to be a writer, write.
Epictetus
Everything that happens happens as it should, and if you observe carefully, you will find this to be so.
Marcus Aurelius
If one does not know to which port is sailing, no wind is favorable.
Seneca
Practice yourself, for heavens sake in little things, and then proceed to greater.
Epictetus
He who lives in harmony with himself lives in harmony with the universe.
Marcus Aurelius
The conditions of conquest are always easy. We have but to toil awhile, endure awhile, believe always, and never turn back.
Seneca
Make the best use of what is in your power, and take the rest as it happens.
Epictetus
The universe is transformation; our life is what our thoughts make it.
Marcus Aurelius
There is no great genius without some touch of madness.
Seneca
Nature gave us one tongue and two ears so we could hear twice as much as we speak.
Epictetus
Look back over the past, with its changing empires that rose and fell, and you can foresee the future, too.
Marcus Aurelius
Most powerful is he who has himself in his own power.
Seneca
He is a wise man who does not grieve for the things which he has not, but rejoices for those which he has.
Epictetus
When you arise in the morning, think of what a precious privilege it is to be alive — to breathe, to think, to enjoy, to love.
Marcus Aurelius
Things that were hard to bear are sweet to remember.
Seneca
There is only one way to happiness and that is to cease worrying about things which are beyond the power of our will.
Epictetus
Accept the things to which fate binds you, and love the people with whom fate brings you together, but do so with all your heart.
Marcus Aurelius
Our lack of confidence is not the result of difficulty. The difficulty comes from our lack of confidence.
Seneca
If you seek truth you will not seek victory by dishonourable means, and if you find truth you will become invincible.
Epictetus
Everything that exists is in a manner the seed of that which will be.
Marcus Aurelius
Associate with people who are likely to improve you.
Seneca
When you are offended at any man's fault, turn to yourself and study your own failings. Then you will forget your anger.
Epictetus
Waste no more time arguing about what a good man should be. Be one.
Marcus Aurelius
No man is good by chance. Virtue is something which must be learned.
Seneca
Know, first, who you are, and then adorn yourself accordingly.
Epictetus
There is nothing happens to any person but what was in his power to go through with.
Marcus Aurelius
We suffer more often in imagination than in reality.
Seneca
Men are disturbed not by things, but by the view which they take of them.
Epictetus
Everything we hear is an opinion, not a fact. Everything we see is a perspective, not the truth.
Marcus Aurelius
Sometimes even to live is an act of courage.
Seneca
We have two ears and one mouth so that we can listen twice as much as we speak.
Epictetus
You will find rest from vain fancies if you perform every act in life as though it were your last.
Marcus Aurelius
He suffers more than necessary, who suffers before it is necessary.
Seneca
Not every difficult and dangerous thing is suitable for training, but only that which is conducive to success in achieving the object of our effort.
Epictetus

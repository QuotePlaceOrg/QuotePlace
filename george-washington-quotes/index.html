First President of the United States · 1732 – 1799

George Washington commanded the Continental Army during the American Revolutionary War and presided over the Constitutional Convention of 1787. He served as the first President of the United States from 1789 to 1797, setting enduring precedents for the office. He is remembered as the Father of His Country.

Quotes by George Washington

1.Liberty, when it begins to take root, is a plant of rapid growth.
2.Perseverance and spirit have done wonders in all ages.
3.Associate yourself with men of good quality if you esteem your own reputation; for it is better to be alone than in bad company.
4.It is better to offer no excuse than a bad one.

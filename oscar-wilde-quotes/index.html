Oscar Wilde Quotes

Oscar Wilde (Writer, 1854 – 1900). Below are 24 quotes by Oscar Wilde from QuotePlace, each as a portrait card.

Writer · 1854 – 1900

Irish playwright, poet and author of The Picture of Dorian Gray, famed for his wit and epigrams.

Quotes by Oscar Wilde

1.The truth is rarely pure and never simple.
2.To live is the rarest thing in the world. Most people exist, that is all.
3.Experience is simply the name we give our mistakes.
4.I can resist everything except temptation.
5.Always forgive your enemies; nothing annoys them so much.
6.The only way to get rid of a temptation is to yield to it.
7.The books that the world calls immoral are books that show the world its own shame.
8.We are all in the gutter, but some of us are looking at the stars.
9.The only thing to do with good advice is to pass it on. It is never of any use to oneself.
10.The aim of life is self-development. To realize ones nature perfectly — that is what each of us is here for.
11.The smallest act of kindness is worth more than the grandest intention.
12.Anybody can make history. Only a great man can write it.
13.To love oneself is the beginning of a lifelong romance.
14.The only difference between a caprice and a lifelong passion is that the caprice lasts a little longer.
15.Man is least himself when he talks in his own person. Give him a mask, and he will tell you the truth.
16.A little sincerity is a dangerous thing, and a great deal of it is absolutely fatal.
17.I am so clever that sometimes I don't understand a single word of what I am saying.
18.Life imitates art far more than art imitates life.
19.Experience is merely the name men gave to their mistakes.
20.There is only one thing in life worse than being talked about, and that is not being talked about.
21.An idea that is not dangerous is unworthy of being called an idea at all.
22.The basis of optimism is sheer terror.
23.Discontent is the first step in the progress of a man or a nation.
24.No great artist ever sees things as they really are. If he did, he would cease to be an artist.

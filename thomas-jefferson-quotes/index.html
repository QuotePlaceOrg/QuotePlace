Third US President · 1743 – 1826

Thomas Jefferson was an American statesman and Founding Father who wrote the Declaration of Independence and served as the third President of the United States (1801–1809). He founded the University of Virginia and doubled the nation's size with the Louisiana Purchase.

Quotes by Thomas Jefferson

Never put off till tomorrow what you can do today.
Do you want to know who you are? Don't ask. Act! Action will delineate and define you.
I'm a great believer in luck and I find the harder I work, the more I have of it.
Don't talk about what you have done or what you are going to do.
Reason and free inquiry are the only effectual agents against error.

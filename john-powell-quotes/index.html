Priest, psychologist and author · 1925 – 2009

John Powell was an American Jesuit priest, psychologist and bestselling author of inspirational books on communication and self-understanding, including 'Why Am I Afraid to Tell You Who I Am?' (1969) and 'The Secret of Staying in Love' (1974).

B.R. Ambedkar Quotes

B.R. Ambedkar (Social reformer, 1891 – 1956). Below are 6 quotes by B.R. Ambedkar from QuotePlace, each as a portrait card.

Social reformer · 1891 – 1956

Indian jurist and social reformer who chaired the drafting of India's Constitution and campaigned against caste discrimination.

Quotes by B.R. Ambedkar

1.Cultivation of mind should be the ultimate aim of human existence.
2.Life should be great rather than long.
3.I measure the progress of a community by the degree of progress which women have achieved.
4.I like the religion that teaches liberty, equality and fraternity.
5.A great man is different from an eminent one in that he is ready to be the servant of society.
6.Be educated, be organised and be agitated.

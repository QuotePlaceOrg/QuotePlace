Zig Ziglar Quotes

Zig Ziglar (American motivational speaker and author, 1926 – 2012). Below are 20 quotes by Zig Ziglar from QuotePlace, each as a quote card.

American motivational speaker and author · 1926 – 2012

Zig Ziglar was an American author and motivational speaker who became one of the most influential sales and personal-development trainers of the twentieth century. His book See You at the Top and his famous line 'Your attitude, not your aptitude, will determine your altitude' reached millions.

Quotes by Zig Ziglar

1.Positive thinking will let you do everything better than negative thinking will.
2.You are the only person on Earth who can use your ability.
3.Your attitude, not your aptitude, will determine your altitude.
4.Remember that failure is an event, not a person.
5.You don't have to be great to start, but you have to start to be great.
6.If you can dream it, then you can achieve it.
7.People often say that motivation doesn't last. Well, neither does bathing. That's why we recommend it daily.
8.You were born to win, but to be a winner, you must plan to win, prepare to win, and expect to win.
9.You can get everything in life you want if you will just help enough other people get what they want.
10.You don't build a business. You build people, and then people build the business.
11.What you get by achieving your goals is not as important as what you become by achieving your goals.
12.A goal properly set is halfway reached.
13.If you aim at nothing, you will hit it every time.
14.The chief cause of failure and unhappiness is trading what you want most for what you want right now.
15.Lack of direction, not lack of time, is the problem. We all have twenty-four hour days.
16.The foundation stones of honesty, character, faith, integrity, love, and loyalty are necessary for a balanced success that includes health, wealth, and happiness.
17.If you go looking for a friend, you're going to find they're very scarce. If you go out to be a friend, you'll find them everywhere.
18.The greatest of all mistakes is to do nothing because you think you can only do a little.
19.Failure is a detour, not a dead-end street.
20.The most important persuasion tool you have in your entire arsenal is integrity.

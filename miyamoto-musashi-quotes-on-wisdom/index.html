Miyamoto Musashi Quotes on Wisdom

12 quotes by Miyamoto Musashi on the subject of wisdom, collected from across the QuotePlace library. About the author: Miyamoto Musashi (1584–1645) — Swordsman and strategist.

Think lightly of yourself and deeply of the world.
Miyamoto Musashi
Perceive that which cannot be seen with the eye.
Miyamoto Musashi
In strategy it is important to see distant things as if they were close and to take a distanced view of close things.
Miyamoto Musashi
In all things, have no preferences.
Miyamoto Musashi
Do nothing that is of no use.
Miyamoto Musashi
You must understand that there is more than one path to the top of the mountain.
Miyamoto Musashi
From one thing, know ten thousand things.
Miyamoto Musashi
If you know the way broadly you will see it in everything.
Miyamoto Musashi
The ultimate aim of martial arts is not having to use them.
Miyamoto Musashi
Get beyond love and grief: exist for the good of man.
Miyamoto Musashi
You should not have any special fondness for a particular weapon, or anything else, for that matter.
Miyamoto Musashi
Do not, under any circumstances, depend on a partial feeling.
Miyamoto Musashi

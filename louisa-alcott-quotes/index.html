Novelist · 1832 – 1888

Louisa May Alcott was an American novelist best known for 'Little Women' (1868), the semi-autobiographical story of the March sisters, which has remained in print ever since. She also wrote sensation fiction under a pen name and supported abolition and women's suffrage.

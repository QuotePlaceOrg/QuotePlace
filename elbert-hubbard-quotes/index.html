Elbert Hubbard Quotes

Elbert Hubbard (American writer and publisher, 1856 – 1915). Below are 6 quotes by Elbert Hubbard from QuotePlace, each as a text quote card.

American writer and publisher · 1856 – 1915

Elbert Hubbard was an American writer, publisher and philosopher who founded the Roycroft arts-and-crafts community. His essay "A Message to Garcia" (1899) was one of the most widely reprinted works of its era; he died in the sinking of the Lusitania.

Quotes by Elbert Hubbard

1.There is no failure except in no longer trying.
2.To avoid criticism, do nothing, say nothing, be nothing.
3.A little more persistence, a little more effort, and what seemed hopeless failure may turn to glorious success.
4.A failure is a man who has blundered but is not capable of cashing in on the experience.
5.The final proof of greatness lies in being able to endure criticism without resentment.
6.The greatest mistake you can make in life is to be continually fearing you will make one.

Albert Einstein Quotes

Albert Einstein (Physicist, 1879 – 1955). Below are 44 quotes by Albert Einstein from QuotePlace, each as a text quote card.

Physicist · 1879 – 1955

German-born theoretical physicist who developed the theory of relativity, one of the pillars of modern physics. He won the 1921 Nobel Prize in Physics for his explanation of the photoelectric effect.

Quotes by Albert Einstein

1.God always takes the simplest way.
2.I never think of the future. It comes soon enough.
3.The only real valuable thing is intuition.

10.A man should look for what is, and not for what he thinks should be.

13.Reality is merely an illusion, albeit a very persistent one.
14.Peace cannot be kept by force. It can only be achieved by understanding.
15.We cannot solve our problems with the same thinking we used when we created them.
16.In the middle of every difficulty lies opportunity.

21.There are only two ways to live your life. One is as though nothing is a miracle. The other is as though everything is a miracle.
22.One may say the eternal mystery of the world is its comprehensibility.
23.I have no special talent. I am only passionately curious.
24.A happy man is too satisfied with the present to dwell too much on the future.
25.The important thing is not to stop questioning; curiosity has its own reason for existing.
26.Few people are capable of expressing with equanimity opinions that differ from the prejudices of their social environment.
27.The most beautiful thing we can experience is the mysterious. It is the source of all true art and science.
28.Science without religion is lame, religion without science is blind.
29.The world is a dangerous place to live; not because of the people who are evil, but because of the people who don't do anything about it.
30.Do not worry about your difficulties in mathematics. I can assure you mine are still greater.
31.God does not play dice with the universe.
32.I know not with what weapons World War III will be fought, but World War IV will be fought with sticks and stones.
33.In the middle of difficulty lies opportunity.
34.The important thing is not to stop questioning. Curiosity has its own reason for existing.
35.A person who never made a mistake never tried anything new.
36.Education is what remains after one has forgotten what one has learned in school.
37.Everything should be made as simple as possible, but not simpler.
38.Look deep into nature, and then you will understand everything better.
39.It is not that I am so smart, it is just that I stay with problems longer.
40.Try not to become a person of success, but rather try to become a person of value.

42.Only a life lived for others is a life worthwhile.

Dalai Lama Quotes on Kindness

What did Dalai Lama have to say about kindness? This page gathers 9 quotes by Dalai Lama on kindness from the QuotePlace collection — every one drawn from the site's Kindness topic, in one place. About the author: Dalai Lama (born 1935) — Spiritual leader.

If you want others to be happy, practice compassion. If you want to be happy, practice compassion.
Dalai Lama
Be kind whenever possible. It is always possible.
Dalai Lama
My religion is very simple. My religion is kindness.
Dalai Lama
Love and compassion open our own inner life, reducing stress, distrust and loneliness.
Dalai Lama
The greatest antidote to insecurity and the sense of fear is compassion — it brings one back to the basis of one's inner strength
Dalai Lama
There is no need for temples, no need for complicated philosophies. My brain and my heart are my temples; my philosophy is kindness.
Dalai Lama
Compassion and happiness are not a sign of weakness but a sign of strength.
Dalai Lama
Love and compassion are necessities, not luxuries. Without them, humanity cannot survive.
Dalai Lama
Compassion and tolerance are not a sign of weakness, but a sign of strength.
Dalai Lama

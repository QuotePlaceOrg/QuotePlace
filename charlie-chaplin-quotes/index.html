Charlie Chaplin Quotes

Charlie Chaplin (Comic actor and filmmaker, 1889 – 1977). Below are 13 quotes by Charlie Chaplin from QuotePlace, each as a quote card.

Comic actor and filmmaker · 1889 – 1977

Charlie Chaplin was an English comic actor and filmmaker whose Little Tramp became a global icon of silent cinema. He co-founded United Artists and wrote, directed and starred in classics including City Lights, Modern Times and The Great Dictator.

Quotes by Charlie Chaplin

1.We think too much and feel too little. More than machinery we need humanity; more than cleverness we need kindness and gentleness.
2.Life is a tragedy when seen in close-up, but a comedy in long-shot.
3.To truly laugh, you must be able to take your pain and play with it.
4.Failure is unimportant. It takes courage to make a fool of yourself.
5.Imagination means nothing without doing.
6.Nothing is permanent in this wicked world — not even our troubles.
7.You'll never find a rainbow if you're looking down.
8.We all want to help one another. Human beings are like that. We want to live by each other's happiness — not by each other's misery.
9.I remain just one thing, and one thing only — and that is a clown. It places me on a far higher plane than any politician.
10.You, the people, have the power to make this life free and beautiful, to make this life a wonderful adventure.
11.The saddest thing I can imagine is to get used to luxury.
12.Life is a beautiful, magnificent thing, even to a jellyfish.
13.I don't believe that the public knows what it wants; this is the conclusion that I have drawn from my career.

Desmond Tutu Quotes

Desmond Tutu (Archbishop, anti-apartheid activist, 1931 – 2021). Below are 7 quotes by Desmond Tutu from QuotePlace, each as a portrait card.

Archbishop, anti-apartheid activist · 1931 – 2021

South African Anglican archbishop and Nobel Peace Prize laureate (1984) who campaigned against apartheid.

Quotes by Desmond Tutu

1.If you are neutral in situations of injustice, you have chosen the side of the oppressor.
2.Don't raise your voice, improve your argument.
3.Do your little bit of good where you are; it's those little bits of good put together that overwhelm the world.
4.Hope is being able to see that there is light despite all of the darkness.
5.My humanity is bound up in yours, for we can only be human together.
6.We must not allow ourselves to become like the system we oppose.
7.If you want peace, you don't talk to your friends. You talk to your enemies.

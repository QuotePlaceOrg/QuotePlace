Economist · 1933

Indian economist and Nobel laureate (1998) known for work on welfare economics and famine.

Quotes by Amartya Sen

1.No famine has ever taken place in the history of the world in a functioning democracy.
2.Poverty is not just a lack of money; it is not having the capability to realize one's full potential as a human being.
3.Development consists of the removal of various types of unfreedoms that leave people with little choice and little opportunity of exercising their reasoned agency.
4.Development can be seen as a process of expanding the real freedoms that people enjoy.

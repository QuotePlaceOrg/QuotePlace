Novelist · 1920 – 1977

Brazilian novelist and short-story writer born in Ukraine, famed for the stream-of-consciousness of Near to the Wild Heart, The Passion According to G.H. and Água Viva. Her intense, interior prose made her one of Brazil's most original voices.

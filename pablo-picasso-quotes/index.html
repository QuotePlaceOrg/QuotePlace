Pablo Picasso Quotes

Pablo Picasso (Artist, 1881 – 1973). Below are 18 quotes by Pablo Picasso from QuotePlace, each as a beautiful quote card.

Artist · 1881 – 1973

Pablo Picasso was a Spanish artist who co-founded Cubism and reinvented his style across a career of nearly eighty years. Works such as Les Demoiselles d'Avignon and Guernica made him the most famous and influential artist of the 20th century.

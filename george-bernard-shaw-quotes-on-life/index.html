George Bernard Shaw Quotes on Life

What did George Bernard Shaw have to say about life? This page gathers 9 quotes by George Bernard Shaw on life from the QuotePlace collection — every one drawn from the site's Life topic, in one place. About the author: George Bernard Shaw (1856–1950) — Playwright.

A life spent making mistakes is not only more honorable, but more useful than a life spent doing nothing.
George Bernard Shaw

George Bernard Shaw
A life spent making mistakes is not only more honourable but more useful than a life spent in doing nothing.
George Bernard Shaw

George Bernard Shaw

George Bernard Shaw

George Bernard Shaw

George Bernard Shaw

George Bernard Shaw

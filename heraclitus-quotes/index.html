Quotes by Heraclitus

1.No man ever steps in the same river twice.
2.No man ever steps in the same river twice, for it is not the same river and he is not the same man.
3.All is flux; nothing stays still.
4.You cannot step twice into the same river, for other waters are continually flowing in.

Walt Whitman Quotes

Walt Whitman (Poet, 1819 – 1892). Below are 28 quotes by Walt Whitman from QuotePlace, each as a quote card.

Poet · 1819 – 1892

American poet best known for the collection Leaves of Grass (1855).

Quotes by Walt Whitman

1.Keep your face always toward the sunshine - and shadows will fall behind you.
2.Do I contradict myself? Very well then I contradict myself. I am large, I contain multitudes.
3.Re-examine all you have been told at school or church or in any book, dismiss whatever insults your own soul.
4.I celebrate myself, and sing myself.
5.I am large, I contain multitudes.
6.Henceforth I ask not good-fortune, I myself am good-fortune.
7.Resist much, obey little.
8.Not I, not any one else can travel that road for you, you must travel it for yourself.
9.Happiness, not in another place but this place, not for another hour but this hour.
10.Afoot and light-hearted I take to the open road, healthy, free, the world before me.
11.I believe a leaf of grass is no less than the journey work of the stars.
12.I sound my barbaric yawp over the roofs of the world.
13.A morning-glory at my window satisfies me more than the metaphysics of books.
14.Whatever satisfies the soul is truth.
15.I am the poet of the Body and I am the poet of the Soul.
16.I too am not a bit tamed, I too am untranslatable.
17.To have great poets, there must be great audiences too.
18.I exist as I am, that is enough.
19.To me, every hour of the light and dark is a miracle.
20.Has any one supposed it lucky to be born? I hasten to inform him or her it is just as lucky to die, and I know it.
21.Behold, I do not give lectures or a little charity, When I give I give myself.
22.Now I see the secret of the making of the best persons, It is to grow in the open air and to eat and sleep with the earth.
23.The powerful play goes on, and you may contribute a verse.
24.I have learned that to be with those I like is enough.
25.The future is no more uncertain than the present.
26.Let your soul stand cool and composed before a million universes.
27.I may be as bad as the worst, but, thank God, I am as good as the best.
28.The art of art, the glory of expression and the sunshine of the light of letters is simplicity.

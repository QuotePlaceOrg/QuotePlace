Napoleon Hill Quotes

Napoleon Hill (Self-help author, 1883 – 1970). Below are 51 quotes by Napoleon Hill from QuotePlace, each as a text quote card.

Self-help author · 1883 – 1970

Napoleon Hill was an American self-help author best known for Think and Grow Rich (1937), one of the best-selling books of its kind. His work popularized the idea that success follows from definite purpose and a positive mental attitude.

Quotes by Napoleon Hill

14.Edison failed 10,000 times before he made the electric light. Do not be discouraged if you fail a few times.
15.Every adversity, every failure, every heartache carries with it the seed of an equal or greater benefit.
16.All achievements, all earned riches, have their beginning in an idea.
17.You might well remember that nothing can bring you success but yourself.
18.Your big opportunity may be right where you are now.
19.Opportunity often comes disguised in the form of misfortune, or temporary defeat.
20.The ladder of success is never crowded at the top.
21.The world has the habit of making room for the man whose actions show that he knows where he is going.
22.First comes thought; then organization of that thought, into ideas and plans; then transformation of those plans into reality. The beginning, as you will observe, is in your imagination.
23.There are no limitations to the mind except those we acknowledge.
24.Here is one quality that one must possess to win, and that is definiteness of purpose, the knowledge of what one wants, and a burning desire to possess it.
25.Whatever the mind of man can conceive and believe, it can achieve. Thoughts are things!
26.Do not wait; the time will never be just right. Start where you stand, and work with whatever tools you may have at your command, and better tools will be found as you go along.
27.In planning to acquire your share of the riches, let no one influence you to scorn the dreamer.
28.The starting point of all achievement is desire.
29.Set your mind on a definite goal and observe how quickly the world stands aside to let you pass.
30.Success requires no explanations. Failure permits no alibis.
31.Patience, persistence and perspiration make an unbeatable combination for success.
32.It is literally true that you can succeed best and quickest by helping others to succeed.
33.The best way to sell yourself to others is first to sell the others to yourself.
34.Whatever the mind can conceive and believe, it can achieve.
35.Strength and growth come only through continuous effort and struggle.
36.Victory is always possible for the person who refuses to stop fighting.
37.Think twice before you speak, because your words and influence will plant the seed of either success or failure in the mind of another.
38.Action is the real measure of intelligence.
39.If you do not conquer self, you will be conquered by self.
40.Success comes to those who become success conscious.
41.The man who does more than he is paid for will soon be paid for more than he does.
42.Do not wait. The time will never be just right.
43.A quitter never wins and a winner never quits.
44.Don't be afraid of a little opposition. Remember that the kite of success generally rises against the wind, not with it.
45.The way of success is the way of continuous pursuit of knowledge.
46.There is one quality which one must possess to win, and that is definiteness of purpose, the knowledge of what one wants, and a burning desire to possess it.
47.Every man is what he is because of the dominating thoughts which he permits to occupy his mind.
48.Great achievement is usually born of great sacrifice, and is never the result of selfishness.
49.You are the master of your destiny. You can influence, direct and control your own environment.
50.Cherish your visions and your dreams as they are the children of your soul.
51.Desire is the starting point of all achievement, not a hope, not a wish, but a keen pulsating desire which transcends everything.

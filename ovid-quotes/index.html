Ovid Quotes

Ovid (Poet, 43 BC – 17). Below are 7 quotes by Ovid from QuotePlace, each as a beautiful quote card.

Poet · 43 BC – 17

Ovid was a Roman poet celebrated for the Metamorphoses, a vast narrative poem of mythological transformations. Exiled by the emperor Augustus to the Black Sea coast, he spent his last decade writing poems of lament and endurance.

Quotes by Ovid

1.Be patient and tough; someday this pain will be useful to you.
2.The cause is hidden. The effect is visible to all.
3.All things change; nothing perishes.
4.Chance is always powerful. Let your hook be always cast; in the pool where you least expect it, there will be a fish.
5.Let your hook always be cast; in the pool where you least expect it, there will be a fish.
6.Take rest; a field that has rested gives a bountiful crop.
7.Dripping water hollows out stone, not through force but through persistence.

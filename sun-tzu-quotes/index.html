Sun Tzu Quotes

Sun Tzu (Military strategist). Below are 16 quotes by Sun Tzu from QuotePlace, each as a portrait card.

Military strategist

Ancient Chinese general traditionally credited with The Art of War, a treatise on strategy studied worldwide for over two thousand years.

Quotes by Sun Tzu

1.If you know the enemy and know yourself, you need not fear the result of a hundred battles.
2.Victorious warriors win first and then go to war, while defeated warriors go to war first and then seek to win.
3.All warfare is based on deception.
4.The supreme art of war is to subdue the enemy without fighting.
5.Let your plans be dark and impenetrable as night, and when you move, fall like a thunderbolt.
6.He will win who knows when to fight and when not to fight.
7.He who is prudent and lies in wait for an enemy who is not, will be victorious.
8.Ponder and deliberate before you make a move.
9.Opportunities multiply as they are seized.
10.In the midst of chaos, there is also opportunity.
11.You have to believe in yourself.
12.Can you imagine what I would do if I could do all I can?
13.The art of war is of vital importance to the State.
14.Move swift as the wind and closely-formed as the wood. Attack like the fire and be still as the mountain.
15.Supreme excellence consists in breaking the enemy's resistance without fighting.
16.He will win who knows when to fight and not to fight.

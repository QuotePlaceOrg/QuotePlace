Quotes by Leo Tolstoy

1.All happy families are alike; each unhappy family is unhappy in its own way.
2.The two most powerful warriors are patience and time.
3.Everyone thinks of changing the world, but no one thinks of changing himself.
4.We lost because we told ourselves we lost.
5.All, everything that I understand, I understand only because I love.
6.If you want to be happy, be.
7.If you look for perfection, you'll never be content.
8.All happy families are alike; every unhappy family is unhappy in its own way.
9.Boredom: the desire for desires.
10.The strongest of all warriors are these two: Time and Patience.
11.I've always loved you, and when you love someone, you love the whole person, just as he or she is, and not as you would like them to be.
12.All the variety, all the charm, all the beauty of life is made up of light and shadow.
13.We can know only that we know nothing. And that is the highest degree of human wisdom.
14.There is no greatness where there is no simplicity, goodness, and truth.
15.The truth is obtained like gold, not by letting it grow bigger, but by washing off from it everything that isn't gold.
16.The vocation of every man and woman is to serve other people.
17.Wrong does not cease to be wrong because the majority share in it.
18.It is amazing how complete is the delusion that beauty is goodness.
19.Pure and complete sorrow is as impossible as pure and complete joy.
20.The hero of my tale, whom I love with all the power of my soul, whom I have tried to portray in all his beauty, who has been, is, and will be beautiful, is Truth.
21.Nothing is so necessary for a young man as the company of intelligent women.
22.Seize the moments of happiness, love and be loved! That is the only reality in the world, all else is folly.
23.A man is like a fraction whose numerator is what he is and whose denominator is what he thinks of himself. The larger the denominator, the smaller the fraction.
24.But art is not a handicraft; it is the transmission of feeling the artist has experienced.
25.Spring is the time of plans and projects.
26.Rummaging in our souls, we often dig up something that ought to have lain there unnoticed.
27.There are no conditions to which a man cannot become used, especially if he sees that all around him are living in the same way.
28.Music is the shorthand of emotion.
29.The sole meaning of life is to serve humanity by contributing to the establishment of the kingdom of God, which can only be done by the recognition and profession of the truth by every man.
30.Man cannot possess anything as long as he fears death. But to him who does not fear it, everything belongs.
31.The only absolute knowledge attainable by man is that life is meaningless.
32.If there are as many minds as there are heads, then surely there must be as many kinds of love as there are hearts.
33.If we admit that human life can be ruled by reason, then all possibility of life is destroyed.
34.He stepped down, trying not to look long at her, as if she were the sun, yet he saw her, like the sun, even without looking.
35.Man's mind cannot grasp the causes of events in their completeness, but the desire to find those causes is implanted in man's soul.
36.Respect was invented to cover the empty place where love should be.
37.War is so unjust and ugly that all who wage it must try to stifle the voice of conscience within themselves.
38.Freethinkers are those who are willing to use their minds without prejudice and without fearing to understand things that clash with their own customs, privileges, or beliefs.
39.A man can live and be healthy without killing animals for food; therefore, if he eats meat, he participates in taking animal life merely for the sake of his appetite. And to act so is immoral.
40.In the name of God, stop a moment, cease your work, look around you.
41.One of the first conditions of happiness is that the link between man and nature shall not be severed.
42.The most difficult subjects can be explained to the most slow-witted man if he has not formed any idea of them already; but the simplest thing cannot be made clear to the most intelligent man if he is firmly persuaded that he knows already, without a shadow of doubt, what is laid before him.
43.I have learnt that all men live not by care for themselves but by love.
44.He who has love, is in God, and God is in him, for God is love.
45.It is not beauty that endears, it's love that makes us see beauty.
46.I sit on a man's back, choking him and making him carry me, and yet assure myself and others that I am very sorry for him and wish to ease his lot by all possible means — except by getting off his back.
47.Maybe I did not live as I ought to have done.
48.Yes, love, but not the love that loves for something, to gain something, or because of something, but that love that I felt for the first time, when dying, I saw my enemy and yet loved him.
49.Pierre was right when he said that one must believe in the possibility of happiness in order to be happy, and I now believe in it. Let the dead bury the dead, but while I'm alive, I must live and be happy.

Anton Chekhov Quotes

Anton Chekhov (Playwright and story writer, 1860 – 1904). Below are 21 quotes by Anton Chekhov from QuotePlace, each as a quote card.

Playwright and story writer · 1860 – 1904

Anton Chekhov was a Russian playwright and master of the short story, a practising doctor who wrote The Cherry Orchard, Uncle Vanya and hundreds of stories. His restrained, humane art reshaped both the theatre and short fiction of the 20th century.

Quotes by Anton Chekhov

1.Knowledge is of no value unless you put it into practice.
2.Don't tell me the moon is shining; show me the glint of light on broken glass.
3.We shall find peace. We shall hear angels, we shall see the sky sparkling with diamonds.
4.Man is what he believes.
5.People don't notice whether it's winter or summer when they're happy.
6.Medicine is my lawful wife and literature my mistress.
7.One must be a god to be able to tell successes from failures without making a mistake.
8.He had two lives: one, open, seen and known by all who cared to know, full of relative truth and of relative falsehood, exactly like the lives of his friends and acquaintances; and another life running its course in secret.
9.What can we do? We must live our lives.
10.Love, friendship, respect do not unite people as much as common hatred for something.
11.You confuse two things: solving a problem and stating a problem correctly. It is only the second that is obligatory for an artist.
12.Anyone who says that the artist's sphere leaves no room for questions, but deals exclusively with answers, has never done any writing or done anything with imagery.
13.And it seemed as though in a little while the solution would be found, and then a new and splendid life would begin.
14.I am in mourning for my life.
15.In two or three hundred years life on earth will be unimaginably beautiful, marvellous.
16.One must not put a loaded rifle on the stage if no one is thinking of firing it.
17.Brevity is the sister of talent.
18.A good upbringing means not that you won't spill sauce on the tablecloth, but that you won't notice it when someone else does.
19.If you want to work on your art, work on your life.
20.People should be beautiful in every way—in their faces, in the way they dress, in their thoughts and in their innermost selves.
21.Only the latter is required of the artist. Not a single problem is resolved in Anna Karenina or Eugene Onegin, and yet the novels satisfy you completely because all the problems they raise are formulated correctly.

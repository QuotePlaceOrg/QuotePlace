Joan of Arc Quotes

Joan of Arc (French saint and military leader, 1412 – 1431). Below are 4 quotes by Joan of Arc from QuotePlace, each as a text quote card.

French saint and military leader · 1412 – 1431

A peasant girl from Domremy, Joan said divine voices guided her to lift the siege of Orleans in 1429 and see Charles VII crowned at Reims. Captured, tried for heresy and burned at Rouen in 1431, she was canonised in 1920 and is a patron saint of France.

Quotes by Joan of Arc

1.If I am not in the state of grace, may God put me there; and if I am, may God so keep me.
2.Hold the cross high so I may see it through the flames.
3.I am not afraid; I was born to do this.
4.Act, and God will act.

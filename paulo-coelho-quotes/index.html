Paulo Coelho Quotes

Paulo Coelho (Novelist, born 1947). Below are 8 quotes by Paulo Coelho from QuotePlace, each as a quote card.

Novelist · born 1947

Brazilian lyricist and novelist whose novel The Alchemist became one of the best-selling books in history, translated into dozens of languages. His fables about following one's dreams have inspired readers on every continent.

Quotes by Paulo Coelho

1.When you want something, all the universe conspires in helping you to achieve it.
2.There is only one thing that makes a dream impossible to achieve: the fear of failure.
3.Write your plans in pencil and give God the eraser.
4.When we love, we always strive to become better than we are. When we strive to become better than we are, everything around us becomes better too.
5.Remember that wherever your heart is, there you will find your treasure.
6.The secret of life, though, is to fall seven times and to get up eight times.
7.One is loved because one is loved. No reason is needed for loving.
8.Tell your heart that the fear of suffering is worse than the suffering itself.

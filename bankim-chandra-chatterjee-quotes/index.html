Bengali novelist · 1838 – 1894

Bankim Chandra Chatterjee pioneered the Bengali novel with works such as Durgeshnandini, Kapalkundala and Anandamath. Anandamath gave India the hymn Vande Mataram, which became a rallying song of the freedom movement. He is regarded as the father of the modern Bengali novel.

Quotes by Bankim Chandra Chatterjee

Traveller, have you lost your way?

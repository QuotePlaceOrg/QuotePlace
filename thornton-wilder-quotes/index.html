American playwright and novelist · 1897 – 1975

Thornton Wilder was an American playwright and novelist who won three Pulitzer Prizes, for the novel The Bridge of San Luis Rey and the plays Our Town and The Skin of Our Teeth. Our Town remains one of the most performed American plays.

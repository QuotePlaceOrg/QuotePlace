Charles Dickens Quotes

Charles Dickens (Novelist, 1812 – 1870). Below are 9 quotes by Charles Dickens from QuotePlace, each as a portrait card.

Novelist · 1812 – 1870

English novelist and social critic; author of A Christmas Carol, Oliver Twist and Great Expectations.

Quotes by Charles Dickens

1.No one is useless in this world who lightens the burdens of another.
2.Reflect upon your present blessings, of which every man has plenty; not on your past misfortunes, of which all men have some.
3.A loving heart is the truest wisdom.
4.Have a heart that never hardens, and a temper that never tires, and a touch that never hurts.
5.It was the best of times, it was the worst of times.
6.Don't leave a stone unturned. It's always something, to know you have done the most you could.
7.We need never be ashamed of our tears.
8.It is a far, far better thing that I do, than I have ever done.
9.I will honour Christmas in my heart, and try to keep it all the year.

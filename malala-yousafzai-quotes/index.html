Pakistani activist for girls' education; the youngest Nobel Prize laureate (2014) after surviving a Taliban assassination attempt.

I raise up my voice — not so that I can shout, but so that those without a voice can be heard.
Malala Yousafzai
When the whole world is silent, even one voice becomes powerful.
Malala Yousafzai
I don't want to be thought of as the 'girl who was shot by the Taliban' but the 'girl who fought for education'.

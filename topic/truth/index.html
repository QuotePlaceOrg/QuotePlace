Truth Quotes

These 16 quotes on truth come straight from the QuotePlace collection — lines by poets, philosophers, leaders and artists across the centuries. Open any card to read the quote on its own page and meet the author behind it.

Three things cannot be long hidden: the sun, the moon, and the truth.
Gautama Buddha
If a problem has no solution, it may not be a problem, but a fact — not to be solved, but to be coped with over time.
Shimon Peres
Bigotry tries to keep truth safe in its hand with a grip that kills it.
Rabindranath Tagore
If you are unable to find the truth right where you are, where else do you expect to find it?
Dogen
Not knowing how near the truth is, people seek it far away.
Hakuin Ekaku
All the arguments to prove man's superiority cannot shatter this hard fact: in suffering the animals are our equals.
Peter Singer
Risk! Risk anything! Care no more for the opinion of others, for those voices. Do the hardest thing on earth for you. Act for yourself. Face the truth.
Katherine Mansfield
The truth is rarely pure and never simple.
Oscar Wilde
It is a truth universally acknowledged, that a single man in possession of a good fortune, must be in want of a wife.
Jane Austen
Beauty is truth, truth beauty, — that is all ye know on earth, and all ye need to know.
John Keats
When you have eliminated the impossible, whatever remains, however improbable, must be the truth.
Arthur Conan Doyle
The truth isn't always beauty, but the hunger for it is.
Nadine Gordimer
If you tell the truth, you don't have to remember anything.
Mark Twain
Books and all forms of writing are terror to those who wish to suppress the truth.
Wole Soyinka
Solitude is the profoundest fact of the human condition. Man is the only being who knows he is alone.
Octavio Paz
Facts are facts and will not disappear on account of your likes.
Jawaharlal Nehru

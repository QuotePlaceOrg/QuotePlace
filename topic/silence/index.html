Silence Quotes

These 15 quotes on silence come straight from the QuotePlace collection — lines by poets, philosophers, leaders and artists across the centuries. Open any card to read the quote on its own page and meet the author behind it.

When the whole world is silent, even one voice becomes powerful.
Malala Yousafzai
To understand the immeasurable, the mind must be extraordinarily quiet, still.
Jiddu Krishnamurti
An old silent pond — A frog jumps into the pond — Splash! Silence again.
Matsuo Bashō
Let yourself be silently drawn by the strange pull of what you really love. It will not lead you astray.
Rumi
The man dies in all who keep silent in the face of tyranny.
Wole Soyinka
Silence is a source of great strength.
Lao Tzu
Silence is the absolute poise or balance of body, mind, and spirit.
Charles Eastman
All of humanity's problems stem from man's inability to sit quietly in a room alone.
Blaise Pascal
To burn with desire and keep quiet about it is the greatest punishment we can bring on ourselves.
Federico García Lorca
Live quietly in the moment and see the beauty of all before you. The future will take care of itself.
Paramahansa Yogananda
Silence is also conversation.
Ramana Maharshi
Meditation in the midst of activity is a thousand times superior to meditation in stillness.
Hakuin Ekaku
Music expresses that which cannot be said and on which it is impossible to be silent.
Victor Hugo
Be silent, or let thy words be worth more than silence.
Pythagoras
Fast as the wind, silent as a forest, fierce as fire, immovable as a mountain.
Takeda Shingen

Writer · 1881 – 1936

Lu Xun was China's most influential modern writer, famous for sharp short stories such as Diary of a Madman and The True Story of Ah Q. He abandoned medicine for literature, believing that changing minds mattered more than healing bodies.

Quotes by Lu Xun

1.Hope is like a road in the land: there was no road to begin with, but when many people walk the same way, a road comes into being.
2.The true warrior dares to face life's bleakness squarely, and to look straight at its flowing blood.

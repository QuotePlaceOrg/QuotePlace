Voltaire (French Enlightenment writer and philosopher, 1694 – 1778). Below are 9 quotes by Voltaire from QuotePlace, each as a text quote card.

French Enlightenment writer and philosopher · 1694 – 1778

Voltaire, born François-Marie Arouet, was a French Enlightenment writer, philosopher and historian famous for the novella Candide and for his witty defence of civil liberties, including freedom of speech and religion. He was one of the most prolific and influential writers of his age.

Quotes by Voltaire

1.No snowflake in an avalanche ever feels responsible.
2.To enjoy life, we must touch much of it lightly.
3.Think for yourselves and let others enjoy the privilege to do so too.
4.The longer we dwell on our misfortunes, the greater is their power to harm us.
5.We never live; we are always in the expectation of living.
6.Judge a man by his questions rather than by his answers.
7.It is dangerous to be right in matters on which the established authorities are wrong.
8.Let us read, and let us dance; these two amusements will never do any harm to the world.
9.God is a comedian playing to an audience too afraid to laugh.

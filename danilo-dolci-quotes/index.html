Italian social activist and writer · 1924 – 1997

Danilo Dolci was an Italian social activist, sociologist and poet who worked with the poor of Sicily using nonviolent methods. Sometimes called the "Gandhi of Sicily", he was nominated for the Nobel Peace Prize.

Lao Tzu Quotes

Lao Tzu (Philosopher). Below are 15 quotes by Lao Tzu from QuotePlace, each as a portrait quote card.

Philosopher

Ancient Chinese philosopher traditionally credited with the Tao Te Ching, the foundational text of Taoism. His historicity is debated by scholars.

Quotes by Lao Tzu

Knowing others is intelligence; knowing yourself is true wisdom. Mastering others is strength; mastering yourself is true power.
He who knows that enough is enough will always have enough.
He who conquers himself is the mightiest warrior.
A leader is best when people barely know he exists.
He who does not trust enough will not be trusted.
The best fighter is never angry.
When the best leader's work is done the people say, 'We did it ourselves.'
Those who know do not speak. Those who speak do not know.
He who talks more is sooner exhausted.
A journey of a thousand miles begins with a single step.
Nature does not hurry, yet everything is accomplished.
Silence is a source of great strength.
If you do not change direction, you may end up where you are heading.
Do your work, then step back. The only path to serenity.
Nothing in the world is softer than water, yet nothing is better at overcoming the hard and strong.

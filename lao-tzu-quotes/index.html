Lao Tzu Quotes

Lao Tzu (Philosopher). Below are 47 quotes by Lao Tzu from QuotePlace, each as a portrait card.

Philosopher

Ancient Chinese philosopher traditionally credited with the Tao Te Ching, the foundational text of Taoism. His historicity is debated by scholars.

Quotes by Lao Tzu

Knowing others is intelligence; knowing yourself is true wisdom. Mastering others is strength; mastering yourself is true power.
He who knows that enough is enough will always have enough.
He who conquers himself is the mightiest warrior.
A leader is best when people barely know he exists.
He who does not trust enough will not be trusted.
The best fighter is never angry.
When the best leader's work is done the people say, 'We did it ourselves.'
Those who know do not speak. Those who speak do not know.
He who talks more is sooner exhausted.
A journey of a thousand miles begins with a single step.
Nature does not hurry, yet everything is accomplished.
Silence is a source of great strength.
If you do not change direction, you may end up where you are heading.
Do your work, then step back. The only path to serenity.
Nothing in the world is softer than water, yet nothing is better at overcoming the hard and strong.
Be the chief but never the lord.
To lead people walk behind them.
Doing nothing is better than being busy doing nothing.
Anticipate the difficult by managing the easy.
He who is contented is rich.
The journey of a thousand miles begins with one step.
An ant on the move does more than a dozing ox
If you correct your mind, the rest of your life will fall into place.
If you would take, you must first give, this is the beginning of intelligence.
The wise man does not lay up his own treasures. The more he gives to others, the more he has for his own.
When you are content to be simply yourself and don't compare or compete, everybody will respect you.
Great indeed is the sublimity of the Creative, to which all beings owe their beginning and which permeates all heaven.
At the center of your being you have the answer; you know who you are and you know what you want.
All difficult things have their origin in that which is easy, and great things in that which is small.
I have just three things to teach: simplicity, patience, compassion. These three are your greatest treasures.
When you realize there is nothing lacking, the whole world belongs to you.
The power of intuitive understanding will protect you from harm until the end of your days.
By letting it go it all gets done. The world is won by those who let it go. But when you try and try. The world is beyond the winning.
He who conquers others is strong; He who conquers himself is mighty.
From wonder into wonder existence opens.
He who knows himself is enlightened.
Great acts are made up of small deeds.
Nothing is softer or more flexible than water, yet nothing can resist it.
When I let go of what I am, I become what I might be.
He who controls others may be powerful, but he who has mastered himself is mightier still.
To see things in the seed, that is genius.
The key to growth is the introduction of higher dimensions of consciousness into our awareness.
He who knows, does not speak. He who speaks, does not know.
Kindness in words creates confidence. Kindness in thinking creates profoundness. Kindness in giving creates love.
He who knows others is wise. He who knows himself is enlightened.
One who is too insistent on his own views, finds few to agree with him.
Music in the soul can be heard by the universe.

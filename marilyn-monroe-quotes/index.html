Marilyn Monroe Quotes

Marilyn Monroe (Actress, 1926 – 1962). Below are 4 quotes by Marilyn Monroe from QuotePlace, each as a text quote card.

Actress · 1926 – 1962

American actress and the most enduring sex symbol of Hollywood's golden age, starring in comedies such as Gentlemen Prefer Blondes and Some Like It Hot. Born Norma Jeane Mortenson, she fought to be taken seriously as an actress and founded her own production company in 1955.

Quotes by Marilyn Monroe

1.A career is wonderful, but you can't curl up with it on a cold night.
2.That's the trouble, a sex symbol becomes a thing. But if I'm going to be a symbol of something I'd rather have it sex than some other things they've got symbols of.
3.I knew I belonged to the public and to the world, not because I was talented or even beautiful, but because I had never belonged to anything or anyone else.
4.If I'd observed all the rules I'd never have gotten anywhere.

Jim Rohn Quotes

Jim Rohn (Motivational speaker and author, 1930 – 2009). Below are 16 quotes by Jim Rohn from QuotePlace, each as a quote card.

Motivational speaker and author · 1930 – 2009

Emanuel James Rohn, known as Jim Rohn, was an American entrepreneur and motivational speaker whose seminars and books on personal development influenced a generation of speakers, including Tony Robbins.

Quotes by Jim Rohn

1.Either you run the day or the day runs you.
2.Give whatever you are doing and whoever you are with the gift of your attention.
3.The more you care, the stronger you can be.
4.If you don't design your own life plan, chances are you'll fall into someone else's plan. And guess what they have planned for you? Not much.
5.You are the average of the five people you spend the most time with.
6.Discipline is the bridge between goals and accomplishment.
7.Formal education will make you a living; self-education will make you a fortune.
8.Success is nothing more than a few simple disciplines, practiced every day.
9.We must all suffer from one of two pains: the pain of discipline or the pain of regret. The difference is discipline weighs ounces while regret weighs tons.
10.Don't wish it were easier, wish you were better.
11.You cannot change your destination overnight, but you can change your direction overnight.
12.Happiness is not something you postpone for the future; it is something you design for the present.
13.Work harder on yourself than you do on your job.
14.If you are not willing to risk the usual, you will have to settle for the ordinary.
15.Let others lead small lives, but not you. Let others argue over small things, but not you. Let others cry over small hurts, but not you. Let others leave their future in someone else's hands, but not you.
16.The major key to your better future is you.

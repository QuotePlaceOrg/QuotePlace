Poet and paratrooper · 1921 – 1944

Hannah Senesh (Szenes) was a Hungarian Jewish poet who emigrated to Palestine and volunteered as a British paratrooper in World War II. Captured and executed in Budapest at 23, she left behind poems including 'Eli, Eli' that made her a national heroine of Israel.

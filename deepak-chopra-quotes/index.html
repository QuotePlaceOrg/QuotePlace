Author and alternative medicine advocate · born 1946

Deepak Chopra is an Indian-American author and alternative medicine advocate, trained as an endocrinologist, who became one of the best-known popularizers of Ayurveda and mind-body medicine. His books, including The Seven Spiritual Laws of Success (1994), have sold tens of millions of copies worldwide. He co-founded the Chopra Center for Wellbeing in California.

Friedrich Nietzsche Quotes

Friedrich Nietzsche (Philosopher, 1844 – 1900). Below are 40 quotes by Friedrich Nietzsche from QuotePlace, each as a portrait card.

Philosopher · 1844 – 1900

German philosopher known for Thus Spoke Zarathustra and the concept of the will to power.

Quotes by Friedrich Nietzsche

1.He who fights with monsters should look to it that he himself does not become a monster.
2.He who has a why to live can bear almost any how.
3.Without music, life would be a mistake.
4.You must have chaos within you to give birth to a dancing star.
5.What is done out of love always takes place beyond good and evil.
6.That which does not kill us makes us stronger.
7.You need chaos in your soul to give birth to a dancing star.
8.The higher we soar, the smaller we appear to those who cannot fly.
9.It is not a lack of love, but a lack of friendship that makes unhappy marriages.
10.And we should consider every day lost on which we have not danced at least once.
11.There are no facts, only interpretations.
12.In individuals, insanity is rare; but in groups, parties, nations and epochs, it is the rule.
13.Become who you are.
14.Whoever fights monsters should see to it that in the process he does not become a monster.
15.One must still have chaos in oneself to be able to give birth to a dancing star.
16.To live is to suffer, to survive is to find some meaning in the suffering.
17.The individual has always had to struggle to keep from being overwhelmed by the tribe. If you try it, you will be lonely often, and sometimes frightened. But no price is too high to pay for the privilege of owning yourself.
18.The snake which cannot cast its skin has to die. As well the minds which are prevented from changing their opinions; they cease to be mind.
19.He who climbs upon the highest mountains laughs at all tragedies, real or imaginary.
20.What does not kill me makes me stronger.
21.A casual stroll through the lunatic asylum shows that faith does not prove anything.
22.In every real man a child is hidden that wants to play.
23.What is great in man is that he is a bridge and not an end.
24.No one can construct for you the bridge upon which precisely you must cross the stream of life, no one but you yourself alone.
25.We have art in order not to die of the truth.
26.God is dead. God remains dead. And we have killed him.
27.And if you gaze for long into an abyss, the abyss gazes also into you.
28.There is always some madness in love. But there is also always some reason in madness.
29.The surest way to corrupt a youth is to instruct him to hold in higher esteem those who think alike than those who think differently.
30.The advantage of a bad memory is that one enjoys several times the same good things for the first time.
31.One repays a teacher badly if one always remains nothing but a pupil.
32.There is more wisdom in your body than in your deepest philosophy.
33.He who cannot obey himself will be commanded. That is the nature of living creatures.
34.He who cannot put his thoughts on ice should not enter into the heat of dispute.
35.Whoever does not have two-thirds of his day for himself, is a slave, whatever he may be: a statesman, a businessman, an official, or a scholar.
36.One must pay dearly for immortality; one has to die several times while still alive.
37.He who would learn to fly one day must first learn to stand and walk and run and climb and dance; one cannot fly into flying.
38.Art is the supreme task and the truly metaphysical activity in this life.
39.Here the ways of men part: if you wish to strive for peace of soul and pleasure, then believe; if you wish to be a devotee of truth, then inquire.
40.A witticism is an epigram on the death of a feeling.

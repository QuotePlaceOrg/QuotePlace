Pearl Buck Quotes

Pearl Buck (Writer, 1892 – 1973). Below are 7 quotes by Pearl Buck from QuotePlace, each as a text quote card.

Writer · 1892 – 1973

Pearl S. Buck was an American writer who spent much of her early life in China, where her parents were missionaries. Her novel The Good Earth won the Pulitzer Prize, and she received the Nobel Prize in Literature in 1938.

Quotes by Pearl Buck

1.One faces the future with ones past.
2.Growth itself contains the germ of happiness.
3.Every great mistake has a halfway moment, a split second when it can be recalled and perhaps remedied.
4.You cannot make yourself feel something you do not feel, but you can make yourself do right in spite of your feelings.
5.The truth is always exciting. Speak it, then. Life is dull without it.
6.The secret of joy in work is contained in one word — excellence. To know how to do something well is to enjoy it.
7.Many people lose the small joys in the hope for the big happiness.

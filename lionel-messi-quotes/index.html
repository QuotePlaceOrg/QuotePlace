Lionel Messi Quotes

Lionel Messi (Footballer, born 1987). Below are 4 quotes by Lionel Messi from QuotePlace, each as a text quote card.

Footballer · born 1987

Argentine forward regarded as one of the greatest footballers ever, winner of a record eight Ballon d'Or awards. After 21 years at Barcelona he moved to Paris Saint-Germain and then Inter Miami, and in 2022 he captained Argentina to the World Cup title.

Quotes by Lionel Messi

You have to fight to reach your dream. You have to sacrifice and work hard for it.
I have many years to get better and better, and that has to be my ambition. The day you think there is no improvement to be made is a sad one for any player.
Money is not a motivating factor. Money doesn't thrill me or make me play better. I'm just happy with a ball at my feet.
I never think about the play or visualize anything. I do what comes to me at that moment. Instinct. It has always been like that.

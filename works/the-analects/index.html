Quotes from The Analects

The sayings of Confucius, recorded by his disciples and compiled after his death. Short exchanges, aphorisms and fragments of conversation — on learning, virtue, government and how to treat other people — that shaped East Asian thought for two and a half millennia. Below: the 31 quotes in the QuotePlace collection that are verifiably from this work, each marked 'Source verified' on its own page.

Quotes from Letters to Lucilius

Seneca's 124 letters to his friend Lucilius, written in the last years of his life (around AD 62–65). Part philosophy course, part intimate correspondence, they cover time, death, anger, wealth and how to spend a day — and remain the most readable door into Stoicism. Below: the 12 quotes in the QuotePlace collection that are verifiably from this work, each marked 'Source verified' on its own page.

If one does not know to which port is sailing, no wind is favorable.
Seneca
Associate with people who are likely to improve you.
Seneca
We suffer more often in imagination than in reality.

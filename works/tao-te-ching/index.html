Quotes from Tao Te Ching

The foundational text of Daoism: 81 short, paradoxical chapters attributed to the sage Laozi. Written more than two thousand years ago, it argues that the soft overcomes the hard, that knowing you know nothing is wisdom, and that the way that can be named is not the eternal way. Below: the 41 quotes in the QuotePlace collection that are verifiably from this work, each marked 'Source verified' on its own page.

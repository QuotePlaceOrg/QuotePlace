Quotes from Think and Grow Rich

Napoleon Hill's 1937 bestseller, distilled from two decades of interviewing the era's most successful men, set out thirteen principles of achievement. Whatever one thinks of its philosophy, its sentences — desire as the starting point, the mind conceiving and believing — became the template for a century of motivational writing. Below: the 22 quotes in the QuotePlace collection that are verifiably from this work, each marked 'Source verified' on its own page.

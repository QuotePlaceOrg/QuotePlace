Quotes from The Prophet

Kahlil Gibran's 1923 masterpiece: 26 prose-poems spoken by Almustafa, a prophet preparing to sail home, answering the people's questions on love, marriage, children, work, sorrow and death. It has been in print continuously for a century and is quoted at weddings and funerals alike. Below: the 25 quotes in the QuotePlace collection that are verifiably from this work, each marked 'Source verified' on its own page.

Kahlil Gibran

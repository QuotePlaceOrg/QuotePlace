Quotes from Illusions

Richard Bach's 1977 novella Illusions: The Adventures of a Reluctant Messiah follows a barnstorming pilot who meets a retired 'messiah'. Its most-quoted lines come from the Messiah's Handbook, the book within the book whose pages the narrator opens at random — every quote below carries a verified Handbook source. Below: the 31 quotes in the QuotePlace collection that are verifiably from this work, each marked 'Source verified' on its own page.

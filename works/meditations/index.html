Quotes from Meditations

The private notebooks of the Roman emperor Marcus Aurelius, written in Greek on campaign in the 170s AD and never meant for anyone else's eyes. Part diary, part self-instruction, the Meditations is Stoicism thinking out loud — and the source of nearly every Marcus Aurelius line worth quoting. Below: the 50 quotes in the QuotePlace collection that are verifiably from this work, each marked 'Source verified' on its own page.

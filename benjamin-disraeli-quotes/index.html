Benjamin Disraeli Quotes

Benjamin Disraeli (British Prime Minister & Novelist, 1804 – 1881). Below are 12 quotes by Benjamin Disraeli from QuotePlace, each as a text quote card.

British Prime Minister & Novelist · 1804 – 1881

British statesman who served twice as Prime Minister and led the Conservative Party. He was also a successful novelist, writing books such as Coningsby and Sybil.

Quotes by Benjamin Disraeli

1.The secret of success is constancy to purpose.
2.Action may not always bring happiness; but there is no happiness without action.
3.Through perseverance many people win success out of what seemed destined to be certain failure.
4.Never apologize for showing feelings. When you do so, you apologize for the truth.
5.One secret of success in life is for a man to be ready for his opportunity when it comes.
6.The greatest good you can do for another is not just to share your riches but to reveal to him his own.
7.The greatest good you can do for another is not just share your riches, but reveal to them their own.
8.Ignorance never settle a question.
9.Never apologize for showing feeling. When you do so, you apologize for truth.
10.We make our own fortunes and we call them fate.
11.Change is inevitable. Change is constant.
12.Youth is a blunder; Manhood a struggle, Old Age a regret.

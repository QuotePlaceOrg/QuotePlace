Warren Buffett Quotes

Warren Buffett (Investor, CEO of Berkshire Hathaway, born 1930). Below are 13 quotes by Warren Buffett from QuotePlace, each as a quote card.

Investor, CEO of Berkshire Hathaway · born 1930

Investor Warren Buffett, the 'Oracle of Omaha', turned Berkshire Hathaway into one of the world's largest conglomerates through patient, disciplined value investing. Famous for his annual shareholder letters and frugal personal habits, he has pledged to give away the great majority of his fortune.

Quotes by Warren Buffett

1.Price is what you pay; value is what you get.
2.It takes 20 years to build a reputation and five minutes to ruin it. If you think about that, you'll do things differently.
3.Be fearful when others are greedy, and be greedy when others are fearful.
4.The most important investment you can make is in yourself.
5.Someone is sitting in the shade today because someone planted a tree a long time ago.
6.It's far better to buy a wonderful company at a fair price than a fair company at a wonderful price.
7.Risk comes from not knowing what you're doing.
8.After all, you only find out who is swimming naked when the tide goes out.
9.In the business world, the rearview mirror is always clearer than the windshield.
10.The stock market is a device for transferring money from the impatient to the patient.
11.It is not necessary to do extraordinary things to get extraordinary results.
12.The most important quality for an investor is temperament, not intellect. You need a temperament that neither derives great pleasure from being with the crowd or against the crowd.
13.I try to buy stock in businesses that are so wonderful that an idiot can run them. Because sooner or later, one will.

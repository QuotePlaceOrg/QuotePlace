Warren Buffett Quotes

Warren Buffett (Investor, CEO of Berkshire Hathaway, born 1930). Below are 5 quotes by Warren Buffett from QuotePlace, each as a text quote card.

Investor, CEO of Berkshire Hathaway · born 1930

Investor Warren Buffett, the 'Oracle of Omaha', turned Berkshire Hathaway into one of the world's largest conglomerates through patient, disciplined value investing. Famous for his annual shareholder letters and frugal personal habits, he has pledged to give away the great majority of his fortune.

Quotes by Warren Buffett

Price is what you pay; value is what you get.
It takes 20 years to build a reputation and five minutes to ruin it. If you think about that, you'll do things differently.
Be fearful when others are greedy, and be greedy when others are fearful.
The most important investment you can make is in yourself.
Someone is sitting in the shade today because someone planted a tree a long time ago.

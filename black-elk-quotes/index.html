Black Elk (Oglala Lakota holy man, 1863 – 1950). Below are 2 quotes by Black Elk from QuotePlace, each as a beautiful quote card.

Oglala Lakota holy man · 1863 – 1950

Oglala Lakota holy man whose great vision and life story, recorded in Black Elk Speaks, became one of the most widely read accounts of Native American spirituality. He witnessed the Little Bighorn and the Wounded Knee massacre.

Quotes by Black Elk

1.I was seeing in a sacred manner the shapes of all things in the spirit, and the shape of all shapes as they must live together like one being.
2.The first peace, which is the most important, is that which comes within the souls of people when they realize their relationship, their oneness, with the universe and all its powers.

Inventor and electrical engineer · 1856 – 1943

Nikola Tesla was a Serbian-American inventor and electrical engineer known for his contributions to alternating current (AC) electricity supply systems. He held hundreds of patents and pioneered work on motors, radio and wireless energy.

Quotes by Nikola Tesla

2.Let the future tell the truth, and evaluate each one according to his work and accomplishments. The present is theirs; the future, for which I have really worked, is mine.

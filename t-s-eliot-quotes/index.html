Poet and playwright · 1888 – 1965

Thomas Stearns Eliot, known as T. S. Eliot, was an American-born British poet and critic, author of The Love Song of J. Alfred Prufrock, The Waste Land and Four Quartets. He received the Nobel Prize in Literature in 1948 and is regarded as a central figure of modernist poetry.

Gautama Buddha Quotes on Mind

What did Gautama Buddha have to say about mind? This page gathers 9 quotes by Gautama Buddha on mind from the QuotePlace collection — every one drawn from the site's Mind topic, in one place. About the author: Gautama Buddha (c. 563 BCE–c. 483 BCE) — Spiritual teacher.

Do not dwell in the past, do not dream of the future, concentrate the mind on the present moment.
Gautama Buddha
The mind is everything. What you think you become.
Gautama Buddha
Do not overrate what you have received, nor envy others. He who envies others does not obtain peace of mind.
Gautama Buddha
To keep the body in good health is a duty... otherwise we shall not be able to keep our mind strong and clear.
Gautama Buddha
All that we are is the result of what we have thought. The mind is everything. What we think we become.
Gautama Buddha
In the sky, there is no distinction of east and west; people create distinctions out of their own minds and then believe them to be true.
Gautama Buddha
Always be mindful of the kindness and not the faults of others.
Gautama Buddha
We are shaped by our thoughts; we become what we think. When the mind is pure, joy follows like a shadow that never leaves.
Gautama Buddha
Remembering a wrong is like carrying a burden on the mind.
Gautama Buddha

Dale Carnegie Quotes

Dale Carnegie (American author and lecturer, 1888 – 1955). Below are 6 quotes by Dale Carnegie from QuotePlace, each as a text quote card.

American author and lecturer · 1888 – 1955

Dale Carnegie was an American writer and lecturer on self-improvement and public speaking. His book How to Win Friends and Influence People (1936) became one of the best-selling books of all time.

Quotes by Dale Carnegie

1.Most of the important things in the world have been accomplished by people who have kept on trying when there seemed to be no hope at all.
2.When fate hands us a lemon, lets try to make lemonade.
3.Success is getting what you want. Happiness is wanting what you get.
4.Develop success from failures. Discouragement and failure are two of the surest stepping stones to success.
5.Remember, today is the tomorrow you worried about yesterday.
6.Do the hard jobs first. The easy jobs will take care of themselves.

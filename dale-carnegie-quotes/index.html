Dale Carnegie Quotes

Dale Carnegie (American author and lecturer, 1888 – 1955). Below are 12 quotes by Dale Carnegie from QuotePlace, each as a quote card.

American author and lecturer · 1888 – 1955

Dale Carnegie was an American writer and lecturer on self-improvement and public speaking. His book How to Win Friends and Influence People (1936) became one of the best-selling books of all time.

Quotes by Dale Carnegie

1.Most of the important things in the world have been accomplished by people who have kept on trying when there seemed to be no hope at all.
2.When fate hands us a lemon, lets try to make lemonade.
3.Success is getting what you want. Happiness is wanting what you get.
4.Develop success from failures. Discouragement and failure are two of the surest stepping stones to success.
5.Remember, today is the tomorrow you worried about yesterday.
6.Do the hard jobs first. The easy jobs will take care of themselves.
7.The only way to get the best of an argument is to avoid it.
8.You can make more friends in two months by becoming genuinely interested in other people than you can in two years by trying to get other people interested in you.
9.When dealing with people, remember you are not dealing with creatures of logic, but with creatures bristling with prejudice and motivated by pride and vanity.
10.It isn't what you have or who you are or where you are or what you are doing that makes you happy or unhappy. It is what you think about it.
11.If you want to conquer fear, don't sit home and think about it. Go out and get busy.
12.Remember that a person's name is to that person the sweetest and most important sound in any language.

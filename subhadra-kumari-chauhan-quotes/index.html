Subhadra Kumari Chauhan Quotes

Subhadra Kumari Chauhan (Hindi poet, 1904 – 1948). Below are 4 quotes by Subhadra Kumari Chauhan from QuotePlace, each as a text quote card.

Hindi poet · 1904 – 1948

Subhadra Kumari Chauhan was a Hindi poet celebrated for heroic ballads such as Jhansi ki Rani, which became an anthem of Indian nationalism. She was also a freedom fighter who took part in Gandhi's Non-Cooperation Movement. Her poems and stories for children remain beloved across India.

Quotes by Subhadra Kumari Chauhan

1.Gallantly she fought, like a true warrior — she was the Rani of Jhansi.
2.This tale we have heard from the lips of the Bundela bards.
3.If this kadamba tree, Mother, stood on the banks of the Yamuna, I too would sit upon it and slowly become Kanhaiya.
4.Tell me, how should the springtime of heroes be?

Congratulations Quotes

Some moments deserve more than a thumbs-up emoji. These quotes celebrate achievement the way it actually happens — through sacrifice, perseverance and a refusal to quit, from Napoleon's victory belonging to the most persevering to Pelé on success being no accident. For promotions, wins, results and personal bests: say it like you mean it.

Be not afraid of greatness: some are born great, some achieve greatness, and some have greatness thrust upon them.
William Shakespeare
Whatever the mind of man can conceive and believe, it can achieve. Thoughts are things!
Napoleon Hill
Great achievement is usually born of great sacrifice, and is never the result of selfishness.
Napoleon Hill
Nothing great was ever achieved without enthusiasm.
Ralph Waldo Emerson
Victory belongs to the most persevering.
Napoleon Bonaparte
Well done is better than well said.
Benjamin Franklin
The more difficult the victory, the greater the happiness in winning.
Pele
Success is no accident. It is hard work, perseverance, learning, studying, sacrifice and most of all, love of what you are doing or learning to do.
Pele
Success is to be measured not so much by the position that one has reached in life as by the obstacles which he has overcome.
Booker T. Washington
Self-belief and hard work will always earn you success.
Virat Kohli
Happiness is not in the mere possession of money; it lies in the joy of achievement, in the thrill of creative effort.
Franklin Roosevelt
All men who have achieved great things have been great dreamers.
Orison Marden
Accept challenges, so that you may feel the exhilaration of victory.
George Patton
The harder the conflict, the more glorious the triumph.
Thomas Paine
If you'll not settle for anything less than your best, you will be amazed at what you can accomplish in your lives.
Vince Lombardi
People of mediocre ability sometimes achieve outstanding success because they don't know when to quit. Most men succeed because they are determined to.
George Allen

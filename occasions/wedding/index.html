Wedding Quotes

The words read at weddings have to survive being heard once, half through happy tears — so they tend to be the strongest love writing there is. Gibran's marriage blessings, Saint-Exupéry on looking together in the same direction, Juliet's love as boundless as the sea. For vows, speeches, cards and the couple themselves.

Get Well Quotes

Recovery asks for two things: rest, and reasons to believe. This collection pairs them — Napoleon's quiet mind as the best cure for the body, the Buddha on health as the greatest gift, Ovid on a rested field giving a bountiful crop. Send one to someone laid up, mending, or fighting their way back to strength.

To keep the body in good health is a duty... otherwise we shall not be able to keep our mind strong and clear.
Gautama Buddha
Health is the greatest gift, contentment the greatest wealth, faithfulness the best relationship.
Gautama Buddha
Your body is precious. It is our vehicle for awakening. Treat it with care.
Gautama Buddha
Calm mind brings inner strength and self-confidence, so that's very important for good health.
Dalai Lama
Consider that not only do negative thoughts and emotions destroy our experience of peace, they also undermine our health.
Dalai Lama
Breathing in, I calm my body. Breathing out, I smile.
Thich Nhat Hanh
Do not pray for an easy life, pray for the strength to endure a difficult one.
Bruce Lee
Rest belongs to the work as the eyelids to the eyes.
Rabindranath Tagore
It is health that is real wealth and not pieces of gold and silver.
Mahatma Gandhi
Strength does not come from physical capacity. It comes from an indomitable will.
Mahatma Gandhi
Happiness is nothing more than good health and a bad memory.
Albert Schweitzer
One who gains strength by overcoming obstacles possesses the only strength which can overcome adversity.
Albert Schweitzer
The best cure for the body is a quiet mind.
Napoleon Bonaparte
Courage is not having the strength to go on; it is going on when you do not have strength.
Napoleon Bonaparte
Take rest; a field that has rested gives a bountiful crop.
Ovid
Be patient and tough; someday this pain will be useful to you.
Ovid
Strength does not come from winning. Your struggles develop your strengths. When you go through hardships and decide not to surrender, that is strength.
Arnold Schwarzenegger
I really think a champion is defined not by their wins but by how they can recover when they fall.
Serena Williams

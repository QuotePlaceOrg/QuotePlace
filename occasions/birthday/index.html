Birthday Quotes

A birthday is the one day of the year that belongs entirely to someone — and the right words make it feel that way. These quotes celebrate growing older without growing dull: wit about age from Mark Twain and George Bernard Shaw, and reminders from Marcus Aurelius and Dr. Seuss that being alive at all is the real gift. Send one in a card, or keep them for your own next lap around the sun.

Here is the test to find whether your mission on Earth is finished: if you're alive, it isn't.
Richard Bach
Each day provides its own gifts.
Marcus Aurelius
When you arise in the morning, think of what a precious privilege it is to be alive — to breathe, to think, to enjoy, to love.
Marcus Aurelius
Write it on your heart that every day is the best day in the year.
Ralph Waldo Emerson
Because you are alive, everything is possible.
Thich Nhat Hanh
Age is an issue of mind over matter. If you don't mind, it doesn't matter.
Mark Twain
The more you praise and celebrate your life, the more there is in life to celebrate.
Oprah Winfrey
Youth is a blunder; Manhood a struggle, Old Age a regret.
Benjamin Disraeli
We don't stop playing because we grow old; we grow old because we stop playing.
George Bernard Shaw
Count your age by friends, not years. Count your life by smiles, not tears.
John Lennon
Youth is wasted on the young.
George Bernard Shaw
When I was 5 years old, my mother always told me that happiness was the key to life. When I went to school, they asked me what I wanted to be when I grew up. I wrote down 'happy'.
John Lennon
And we should consider every day lost on which we have not danced at least once.
Friedrich Nietzsche
Time is not measured by the passing of years but by what one does, what one feels, and what one achieves.
Jawaharlal Nehru
Do not take life too seriously. You will never get out of it alive.
Elbert Hubbard
I celebrate myself, and sing myself.
Walt Whitman
Today you are You, that is truer than true. There is no one alive who is Youer than You.
Dr. Seuss
I'm not interested in age. People who tell me their age are silly. You're as old as you feel.
Elizabeth Arden
I want to live another thousand years.
Chairil Anwar

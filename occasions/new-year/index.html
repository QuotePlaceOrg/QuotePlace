New Year Quotes

Every January the whole world agrees to begin again — and these are the words for it. The Buddha's promise that no matter how hard the past, you can always begin again; Seneca's instruction to begin at once to live; Mary Pickford on a fresh start at any moment you choose. Resolutions optional; hope included.

You are always free to change your mind and choose a different future, or a different past.
Richard Bach
Stop acting as if life is a rehearsal. Live this day as if it were your last. The past is over and gone. The future is not guaranteed.
Wayne Dyer
No matter how hard the past, you can always begin again.
Gautama Buddha
Old friends pass away, new friends appear. It is just like the days. An old day passes, a new day arrives.
Dalai Lama
Transformation does not start with some one else changing you; transformation is an inner self reworking of what you are now to what you will be.
Byron Pulsifer
Yesterday is but today's memory and tomorrow is today's dream.
Kahlil Gibran
As our case is new, we must think and act anew.
Abraham Lincoln
Begin at once to live, and count each separate day as a separate life.
Seneca
With the new day comes new strength and new thoughts.
Eleanor Roosevelt
If you have made mistakes, there is always another chance for you. You may have a fresh start any moment you choose.
Mary Pickford
Nothing is lost if one has the courage to proclaim that all is lost and we must begin again.
Julio Cortázar
Yesterday is history. Tomorrow is a mystery. And today? Today is a gift. That is why we call it the present.
Babatunde Olatunji

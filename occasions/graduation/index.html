Graduation Quotes

Graduation is a door: one life behind you, every road still open ahead. The classic words for the moment are here — Eleanor Roosevelt on the future belonging to those who believe in the beauty of their dreams, Lao Tzu on the thousand-mile journey, Churchill on the end of the beginning. For cards, speeches and the graduate in your life.

Go for it now. The future is promised to no one.
Wayne Dyer
Do not wait; the time will never be just right. Start where you stand, and work with whatever tools you may have at your command, and better tools will be found as you go along.
Napoleon Hill
There are only two mistakes one can make along the road to truth; not going all the way, and not starting.
Gautama Buddha
The journey of a thousand miles begins with one step.
Lao Tzu
Do not follow where the path may lead. Go, instead, where there is no path and leave a trail.
Ralph Waldo Emerson
It is not death that a man should fear, but he should fear never beginning to live.
Marcus Aurelius
You can't create in a vacuum. Life gives you the material and dreams can propel new beginnings.
Byron Pulsifer
The best thing about the future is that it only comes one day at a time.
Abraham Lincoln
It is a rough road that leads to the heights of greatness.
Seneca
The only impossible journey is the one you never begin.
Tony Robbins
Setting goals is the first step in turning the invisible into the visible.
Tony Robbins
Your past does not equal your future.
Tony Robbins
The future depends on what you do today.
Mahatma Gandhi
Step by step walk the thousand-mile road.
Miyamoto Musashi
Now this is not the end. It is not even the beginning of the end. But it is, perhaps, the end of the beginning.
Winston Churchill
The future belongs to those who believe in the beauty of their dreams.
Eleanor Roosevelt

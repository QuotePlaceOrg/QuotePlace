Sympathy Quotes

When someone you care about is grieving, finding any words at all can feel impossible. The quotes below have carried that weight for centuries — Gibran on sorrow carving room for joy, Tolkien on tears, Tagore on seeing the stars again. Use them in a card or a message; sometimes a borrowed sentence says exactly what your own heart means.

Don't grieve. Anything you lose comes round in another form.
Rumi
This place is a dream. Only a sleeper considers it real. Then death comes like dawn, and you wake up laughing at what you thought was your grief.
Rumi
Loss is nothing else but change,and change is Natures delight.
Marcus Aurelius
Your joy is your sorrow unmasked.
Kahlil Gibran
The deeper that sorrow carves into your being, the more joy you can contain.
Kahlil Gibran
We choose our joys and sorrows long before we experience them.
Kahlil Gibran
Your pain is the breaking of the shell that encloses your understanding.
Kahlil Gibran
Let me not pray to be sheltered from dangers, but to be fearless in facing them. Let me not beg for the stilling of my pain, but for the heart to conquer it.
Rabindranath Tagore
If you cry because the sun has gone out of your life, your tears will prevent you from seeing the stars.
Rabindranath Tagore
So long as you can sweeten another's pain, life is not in vain.
Helen Keller
Blessed are the hearts that can bend; they shall never be broken.
Albert Camus
One word frees us of all the weight and pain of life. That word is love.
Sophocles
We need never be ashamed of our tears.
Charles Dickens
Friendship improves happiness and abates misery, by the doubling of our joy and the dividing of our grief.
Cicero
I will not say: do not weep; for not all tears are an evil.
J.R.R. Tolkien
The rose and the thorn, and sorrow and gladness are linked together.
Saadi Shirazi
We never understand how little we need in this world until we know the loss of it.
James Barrie
But as long as you remember what you have seen, then nothing is gone. As long as you remember, it is part of this story we have together.
Leslie Marmon Silko
The pain passes, but the beauty remains.
Pierre Auguste Renoir

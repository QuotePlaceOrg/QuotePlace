Good Luck Quotes

Luck, the great writers mostly agree, is not an accident — it is what preparation, courage and timing look like from the outside. Jefferson found the harder he worked, the more of it he had; Pasteur said chance favours the prepared mind. Send these to someone before an exam, an interview, a match or a leap — and may fortune favour them.

You are never given a wish without also being given the power to make it come true. You may have to work for it, however.
Richard Bach
Good luck is another name for tenacity of purpose.
Ralph Waldo Emerson
Remember that sometimes not getting what you want is a wonderful stroke of luck.
Dalai Lama
I will prepare and some day my chance will come.
Abraham Lincoln
Diligence is the mother of good luck.
Benjamin Franklin
Chance is always powerful. Let your hook be always cast; in the pool where you least expect it, there will be a fish.
Ovid
I'm a great believer in luck and I find the harder I work, the more I have of it.
Thomas Jefferson
Each misfortune you encounter will carry in it the seed of tomorrows good luck.
Og Mandino
Fortune favours the brave.
Virgil
The chance which now seems lost may present itself at the last moment.
Jules Verne
I wish I could show you, when you are lonely or in darkness, the astonishing light of your own being.
Hafiz
The day you decide to do it is your lucky day.
Japanese proverb
Chance favors the prepared mind.
Louis Pasteur
Be glad of life because it gives you the chance to love, to work, to play, and to look up at the stars.
Henry Van Dyke
Life is just a chance to grow a soul.
A. Powell Davies

Valentine's Day Quotes

Valentine's Day runs on declarations, and these are the heavy artillery: Neruda's cherry trees, Rumi learning how to love in your light, Hafez's sun that never says "you owe me." Romantic, quotable and safe to send — every line here is about love itself, spoken to a beloved, not about humanity in general.

In your light I learn how to love. In your beauty, how to make poems.
Rumi
Love is the bridge between you and everything.
Rumi
Only from the heart can you touch the sky.
Rumi
Your task is not to seek for love, but merely to seek and find all the barriers within yourself that you have built against it.
Rumi
Love is a smoke made with the fume of sighs.
William Shakespeare
Speak low, if you speak love.
William Shakespeare
Love is merely a madness.
William Shakespeare
If you have only one smile in you, give it to the people you love.
Maya Angelou
The heart has its reasons which reason knows not of.
Blaise Pascal
All you need is love.
John Lennon
Love is the flower you've got to let grow.
John Lennon
There is no charm equal to tenderness of heart.
Jane Austen
Kiss me, and you will see how important I am.
Sylvia Plath
I want to do with you what spring does with the cherry trees.
Pablo Neruda
Love doesn't make the world go round, love is what makes the ride worthwhile.
Elizabeth Browning
Whoso loves, believes the impossible.
Elizabeth Browning
She walks in beauty, like the night.
Lord Byron
Even after all this time, the sun never says to the earth, "You owe me." Look what happens with a love like that: it lights the whole sky.
Hafez
Intellect ties you down and risks nothing, but love dissolves all risks and puts everything at stake.
Shams Tabrizi
Tears and fears and feeling proud, to say 'I love you' right out loud.
Joni Mitchell
Where there is great love, there are always miracles.
Willa Cather
We come to love not by finding a perfect person, but by learning to see an imperfect person perfectly.
Sam Keen
